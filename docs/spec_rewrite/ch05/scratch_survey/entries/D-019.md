704	### D-019 — Harmonic tick grid with virtual assemblies and rate scopes
705	
706	**Status.** ratified
707	
708	**Position.** Harmonic tick grid on step boundaries; discrete stages gated to
709	own tick instants (ZOH by construction); assemblies virtual for execution, rate
710	scopes for declaration (integer multipliers $K \ge 1$ composing down the tree,
711	compiled to absolute divisors); `Δt` arrives as a discrete-bundle field
712	([D-074][d-074]), single source of truth, no stored `Δt`-derived parameters.
713	
714	**Spec.** [§5.4][s5-4], [§10.5][s10-5]
715	
716	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
717	
718	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-186][d-186] and [D-283][d-283]. Phases are
719	adopted, reversing the phase-offset rejection below, and rates compile to
720	anchor-relative triples whose divisors deployment binds.
721	
722	**Rejected.**
723	- *Superseded position — a `comp.Δt` virtual property (the earlier design of
724	  record):* impossible, because `===`-identical siblings can sit under
725	  different `rates` keys, so the period is a schedule-position fact, not a
726	  component fact ([D-074][d-074]).
727	- *Atomic assemblies, incl. opt-in:* coarsened schedulable unit → [§5.4][s5-4]
728	  artificial loops at assembly scale; interleaving protection meaningless under
729	  the signal table; FlightCore's whole-tree atomicity was a call-tree artifact.
730	  The atomic-assembly census behind that rejection: Simulink documents the
731	  artificial-loop hazard for its Atomic Subsystems, and the construct's other
732	  roles there — code-generation units, enabled/triggered execution — have no
733	  counterpart in the consumers, conditional behavior ("on ground, force
734	  `direct`") being mode logic.
735	- *Arbitrary tick periods via time queue:* variable `h`, irregular frames, no
736	  demonstrated need.
737	- *Absolute-period declaration as default:* welds deployment rates into
738	  reusable designs; base-period variables don't compose across independently
739	  authored assemblies.
740	- *Re-running discrete stages every boundary:* un-samples sampled-data
741	  semantics.
742	- *Phase offsets:* no demonstrated use.
743	- *`Δt` via `h`-argument only:* discretized laws live in the feedthrough stage.
