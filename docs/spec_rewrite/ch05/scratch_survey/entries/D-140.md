4625	### D-140 — Add a three-rung remedy ladder for artificial algebraic-loop dependencies
4626	
4627	**Status.** ratified
4628	
4629	**Position.** [§5.4][s5-4]'s escape hatch becomes a **three-rung remedy ladder** for
4630	artificial loops; [§8.3][s8-3] and [§4.3][s4-3] are unchanged.
4631	
4632	**Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§5.4][s5-4], [§5.6][s5-6], [§8.3][s8-3]
4633	
4634	**Rationale.** (i) The **two-stage split** dissolves most of the class —
4635	the `VehicleDynamics` instance of `flight_case_studies.md` is the canonical dissolution. (ii)
4636	**Contract re-factoring**, the new middle rung: when [§5.6][s5-6]'s tracer says
4637	*artificial*, re-examine the cycle's wires before moving any code, because an
4638	input the neighbor consumes **only in a fallback branch** is the archetypal
4639	false dependency — the neighbor is computing, on the component's behalf, a
4640	fallback whose semantics belong on the component's own side of the boundary;
4641	move the branch to its natural owner and the wire disappears. Canonical
4642	instance, taken from the shipped code the finding walked
4643	(`landinggear.jl:255-323`, `:63-72`): `Strut` hands the contact-velocity
4644	azimuth `ψ_v` to the steering model and consumes the returned `ψ_sw` throughout
4645	the contact-frame construction, but `ψ_v` is used *only* in the steering's
4646	disengaged (castoring) branch, and castoring is free-swiveling wheel physics —
4647	the strut's business, not the steering law's; re-factoring `AbstractSteering`
4648	to emit `(engaged, ψ_cmd)` and computing `ψ_sw = engaged ? ψ_cmd : ψ_v` inside
4649	`Strut` deletes the backward wire, and the factoring **survives substitution**
4650	— a stateful steering actuator produces `ψ_cmd` from its own state and still
4651	needs nothing from the strut — which is the test that it records structure
4652	rather than dodging the diagnostic. (iii) **Splitting the component** stays the
4653	residual remedy, for cycles whose halves genuinely belong to one component, and
4654	its cost is now **stated where it bites**: [§8.3][s8-3]'s visibility is binary, so
4655	every intermediate shared across the new boundary becomes `output_types` —
4656	public, connectable, substitution-relevant. The cap is [§4.3][s4-3]'s granularity
4657	guideline, which the split case satisfies trivially (one producing stage, one
4658	consumer): **one struct-valued bundle port** (a `StrutGeometry`-shaped value),
4659	not N loose ports — the bundle type becomes contract, a real cost but a bounded
4660	and honest one. [§5.6][s5-6]'s hint offers **both exits** ("split this component, or
4661	narrow the neighbor's contract"), continuous members only as before, and the
4662	classifier machinery is untouched: it already knows which hop died. [§5.4][s5-4]'s
4663	"rare" is thereby **earned** rather than asserted — the split is rare *because*
4664	rungs (i) and (ii) absorb the common shapes. `migration_outline.md` records the strut/steering
4665	pair as the contract-re-factoring worked instance, beside the dissolving
4666	`VehicleDynamics` of `flight_case_studies.md`, with the split and its bundle port noted as the residual
4667	remedy not taken; the `AbstractSteering` contract change is an
4668	**aircraft-library migration call living in `migration_outline.md`**, not framework vocabulary.
4669	
4670	**Rejected.**
4671	- *A visibility level for split-orphaned intermediates (`unlisted`,
4672	  `Private(T)`):* an explicit non-reopening of [D-034][d-034] and [D-055][d-055] — the promotion
4673	  cost is real but bounded, and the bundle port keeps it to one name, whereas a
4674	  hiding annotation would buy back that name at the price of "public always
4675	  means someone wrote it down".
4676	- *Leaving [§5.4][s5-4] split-only:* the first real migration instance shows the
4677	  diagnostic steering the author toward mid-computation surgery on seven shared
4678	  intermediates (`l`, `r_bs_b`, `q_bs`, `ks_e`, `v_ec_b_body`, `ξ`, terrain
4679	  data) when deleting one false wire was the answer — and the "rare" claim is
4680	  only true with the ladder in place.
4681	- *Framework-side auto-splitting or auto-narrowing:* the classifier's trace
4682	  speaks only for the branch taken at the probe point ([D-012][d-012]'s diagnostic-only
4683	  doctrine), so remedy selection is the author's — and both [§5.4][s5-4] remedies
4684	  document real structure precisely because a human chose them.
