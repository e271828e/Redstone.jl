7467	### D-205 — Boundary zero publishes every discrete output stage, due or not
7468	
7469	**Status.** ratified
7470	
7471	**Position.** At boundary zero every discrete component's output stages run
7472	in the ordinary sorted walk, publishing from the authored `s` and the `t₀`
7473	table, while `g` updates remain gated by `Φ` — an offset component's first
7474	consumed sample stays its `Φ·Δt_base` tick's. No published cell holds the
7475	probe's synthesized values (the `t₀` snapshot is the authored world, fully
7476	evaluated); the ruling amends the boundary-zero consequence recorded in
7477	[D-185][d-185], whose gate composition and residue invariant stand.
7478	
7479	**Spec.** [§10.5][s10-5], [§14.5][s14-5], [§14.8][s14-8]
7480	
7481	**Rationale.** The probe-seed residue was mechanism economy, not a modeling
7482	claim: [D-185][d-185]'s gate at index 0, "implemented by nothing", left non-due output
7483	cells holding [§9.3][s9-3]'s synthesized values, and the spec carried the consequence
7484	as a caveat at both sites ([§14.5][s14-5]'s sweep bullet, [§14.8][s14-8]'s committed-residual
7485	read-back). The residue leaked exactly the values [§14.6][s14-6]'s barrier keeps out
7486	of the running world — a fabricated probe input is a fine shape exercise and
7487	a terrible flight condition — and [§10.5][s10-5]'s content defense ("what a tick at
7488	`t₀⁻` would have produced") held only in the build's own world, the probe
7489	running before any condition exists. Publishing every output stage restores
7490	tier symmetry (the interior sweep already evaluates every continuous output
7491	at `t₀`), dissolves [§14.8][s14-8]'s caveat — the committed snapshot sits fully at
7492	the solved point, the projection and handler movers aside — and retires the
7493	virgin-table precondition wholesale: with every cell boundary-zero-written,
7494	a warm restart has no stale-cell class to guard against. The accepted cost
7495	is one off-grid output evaluation: an input-reading stage publishes from
7496	`u(t₀)` at an instant not on its grid. That evaluation is establishment
7497	under [§14.5][s14-5]'s charter — authorship replaces history — and a modeled startup
7498	hole (a device dark until its first tick) is a modeling decision for a
7499	validity flag or mode, never a framework default drawn from build plumbing.
7500	
7501	**Rejected.**
7502	- *Probe-seed residue — the prior position:* non-due cells holding [§9.3][s9-3]'s
7503	  synthesized values until first tick. Mechanism economy with an
7504	  anti-diagnostic default; the caveats it forced onto [§14.5][s14-5] and [§14.8][s14-8] are
7505	  the record of its cost, and its `t₀⁻` story fails under any condition
7506	  overlay.
7507	- *State-only publication (`h_s` due-or-not, `h_su` still gated):* spares
7508	  the off-grid sample but leaves the input-sampling stages — the ZOH
7509	  archetype, the commonest offset shape — holding probe residue; buys
7510	  little.
7511	- *Authorable output cells:* would give the pre-first-tick value an owner,
7512	  but outputs are derived data ([§14.1][s14-1]), and deriving them from the authored
7513	  world is what the adopted rule does.
