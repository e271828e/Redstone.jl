6054	### D-169 — `y_x`/`y_z` carry stage-1 ports only, auto-published excluded
6055	
6056	**Status.** superseded → [D-252][d-252]
6057	
6058	**Position.** `y_x`/`y_z` carry the stage-1 **return only, auto-published names
6059	excluded** — the underspecification left by the bundle law's "iff stage-1 ports
6060	exist", settled on the narrow reading: an auto-published port is the framework
6061	copying a state, mode or `z` field into a cell at stage-1 position ([§5.3][s5-3]), and
6062	stage 2 already holds `x`/`m`/`z` directly, so carrying it in the hand-down
6063	would be [§7.4][s7-4] step 4's rejected identity transport relocated from the table
6064	into a bundle.
6065	
6066	**Spec.** [§5.2][s5-2], [§5.3][s5-3], [§5.6][s5-6], [§7.4][s7-4], [§9.3][s9-3], [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix D][sD]
6067	
6068	**Rationale.** It is also what [§9.3][s9-3] already sources (the stage-1 *probe's
6069	return*), so the clause records existing machinery rather than changing it;
6070	corollary, stated because it is the case a reader will hit: a component whose
6071	only stage-1 ports are auto-published has no `y_x` in its stage-2 bundle at
6072	all, and a stage-2 function destructuring one meets [§13.2][s13-2]'s framing diagnostic.
6073	
6074	Context: this arose while re-examining whether `y_x` survives [D-165][d-165] at all — it
6075	does, and the theme it was suspected of violating ("`y` is public-only") is
6076	what [D-165][d-165] delivered: `y_x` is now declared ports only, `w` carries the private
6077	population, and the pair is one hand-down mechanism over the two populations
6078	the design already distinguishes, with visibility deciding the channel rather
6079	than the author.
6080	
6081	**Rejected.**
6082	- *Broad reading (`y_x` = every cell written at stage-1 position,
6083	  auto-published included):* transport for its own sake, since the underlying
6084	  stores are already in the same bundle, and it would make the hand-down's
6085	  contents depend on the *contract* rather than on what the stage computed.
6086	- *Dropping `y_x` entirely and re-routing public stage-1 values through `w`
6087	  (raised and rejected in the same discussion):* forces a public value into the
6088	  channel defined as private, costs a `(y, y)`-style repack idiom or
6089	  hand-copied fields with a drift hazard, makes stage 2 the one own-function
6090	  denied the fused idiom's "publish once, read your own product" that
6091	  `f`/`g`/guards get through `y`, and would unpick [§5.6][s5-6]'s tracer boundary
6092	  argument, in which `y_x` is the named never-seeded stage-2 input.
