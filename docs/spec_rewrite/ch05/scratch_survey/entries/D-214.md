7829	### D-214 — Split Appendix C's severity column into severity, raised and policy
7830	
7831	**Status.** ratified
7832	
7833	**Position.** A kind's severity, a property of the kind read as `severity(d)`, is
7834	`error` or `warning`, nothing else.
7835	[Appendix C][sC]'s former severity column, which braided seriousness with stage and
7836	reporting policy, splits into three: **severity** (the kind's), **raised** (the
7837	stage an occurrence surfaces in — `build`, `service`, `runtime` — with its
7838	placement notes) and **policy** (how it is reported — `collected`, `fail-fast`,
7839	`logged`, `rate-limited`). A kind raised at more than one stage lists each.
7840	
7841	**Spec.** [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix C][sC]
7842	
7843	**Rationale.** Surfaced while briefing the prototype's diagnostic-carrier
7844	increment: [§13.2][s13-2] says severity is a payload field from which the column derives,
7845	but the column's vocabulary — `build (collected)`, `warning (service)`, `build
7846	(fail-fast) at probe; runtime as a StepError species` — is not a value one field
7847	can hold. Stage and policy belong to the occurrence: `BundleFieldError` and
7848	`ConformanceFailure` are raised at the probe and again at runtime. Seriousness
7849	alone is the kind's — whether an occurrence ever throws — and that two-way split
7850	is what a test needs to know first. Stage and policy are independent facts with
7851	vocabularies of three and four words, so they take a column each: the preamble
7852	defines nine words instead of seven compound pairs, and the occupied cells of the
7853	stage × policy grid fall out of the rows. `fail-fast` deliberately covers three
7854	mechanisms — the build's phase abort, a service call's single throw, the runtime
7855	`StepError` — because the policy is the same in each: the first occurrence
7856	throws and nothing else is gathered. No kind currently needs two rows; the two
7857	dual-stage kinds are fail-fast at both stages.
7858	
7859	**Rejected.**
7860	- *A stage or policy field on the kind, the columns mapping onto it:* falsifies
7861	  the two-stage kinds, and puts a per-occurrence fact into a value that is data,
7862	  not a throw site.
7863	- *Severity as the braided vocabulary (a kind carrying `build_collected`):* the
7864	  same objection, with the enum growing a value per stage × policy pair while
7865	  the question tests ask is two-way.
7866	- *One compound `raised` column holding stage-policy pairs:* seven values to
7867	  memorize for what are three independent facts, some pairs valid for one
7868	  severity only.
7869	- *Dropping stage and policy, leaving them to the owning sections:* the table is
7870	  the acceptance-test index; whether a test expects a thrown collection, a
7871	  single throw, or a logged warning beside a return value is exactly what it
7872	  has to know.
