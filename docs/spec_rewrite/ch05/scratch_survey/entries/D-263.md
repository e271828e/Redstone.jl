10370	### D-263 — One arity on both tiers: plain contracts, the `Pinned` marker and the mandatory store
10371	
10372	**Status.** ratified
10373	
10374	**Position.** Every contract declaration takes the component alone on both
10375	tiers, is written at nominal `Float64`, and is retyped by the framework. The
10376	tier is never spelled in a signature.
10377	
10378	- On the continuous tier `input_types` and `output_types` are **walked**: the
10379	  leaf walk that types `init_x` and the root inputs is applied to the declared
10380	  types, so every `Float64` position follows the activation scalar. A leaf
10381	  wrapped as `Pinned{P}` is pinned at every activation, and the wrapper is
10382	  stripped at nominal. On the discrete tier the declarations pin wholesale,
10383	  as before, and a `Pinned` entry there is `DeclarationOnWrongTier`.
10384	- The permissive reading of `input_types` ([D-167][d-167]) stands with the marker as
10385	  its spelling: an unpinned position is tolerant, a `Pinned` one demands a
10386	  frozen arrival. Both wire clauses of [§6.1][s6-1] stand, decided at the marker
10387	  scalar by retyping the declarations instead of calling them.
10388	- The type walk substitutes `Float64` in type-parameter positions, yields `P`
10389	  at `Pinned{P}`, and does not enter a mutable type's parameters. A handle's
10390	  data references are fields and never walk; a handle with a scalar parameter
10391	  walks like any type, and `Pinned` freezes one built from build-time data.
10392	- `init_workspace(::C, ::Type{T})` keeps its scalar and takes it on **both**
10393	  tiers; the framework passes `Float64` to a discrete allocator at every
10394	  activation.
10395	- Every leaf declares exactly one of `init_x` and `init_s`, and a stateless
10396	  leaf declares it empty, `init_x(::C) = (;)` or `init_s(::C) = (;)`. The
10397	  store is the tier marker, mandatory even when empty, as `child_connections`
10398	  is the class marker ([§8.5][s8-5]). `StoreWithoutUpdate` applies to a non-empty
10399	  store only.
10400	- `TierSignatureMismatch` retires with all three arms. `TierUnreadable` names
10401	  a primitive declaring neither store, and an empty-store leaf with no
10402	  `output_types` is `StatelessWithoutOutputs`.
10403	
10404	**Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§7.2][s7-2], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.1][s9-1],
10405	[§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix A][sA], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
10406	
10407	**Rationale.** The two-argument form charged every continuous leaf for a
10408	choice almost none of them make: the suite carries 11 pinned leaves against
10409	192 `T`-form declarations, and a flight library's components promote by
10410	default because trim and linearization sweep whole assemblies. [D-166][d-166]'s own
10411	criterion says a `T` belongs in a signature exactly where the declaration's
10412	non-nominal behavior is underdetermined by its nominal restriction. With a
10413	leaf-level marker that underdetermination is gone: the plain declaration,
10414	`Pinned` included, fixes the declaration at every scalar, so the signature's
10415	`T` would record nothing the page does not already say. The retype walk is
10416	not new machinery. It is `retype`, the walk `init_x`, conditions and the
10417	root-input cells already go through; the contracts were the one declaration
10418	kind it did not reach, and carrying two conventions cost more than carrying
10419	one.
10420	
10421	[D-166][d-166] reversed [D-079][d-079] on two grounds. Deliberate pinning of a real leaf was
10422	inexpressible under the plain form, and the plain form was reader-opaque,
10423	misleading to anyone not carrying the walk rule in their head. The marker is
10424	the element neither entry had on the table, and it answers the first ground
10425	outright: the pin is on the page, per leaf, schema-visible and
10426	conformance-checked, so [§14.10][s14-10]'s freeze door and the FFI door survive as
10427	declared doors. The second ground is revalued rather than answered. The walk
10428	rule is one rule, already taught for the state side; a reader who knows that
10429	`Float64` follows the scalar on the continuous tier reads every declaration
10430	in the framework with it.
10431	
10432	The forgotten-`T` classes vanish rather than move. A `Float64` written at a
10433	participating leaf now walks, which is what the author meant. The class that
10434	takes its place, a forgotten pin on an FFI leaf, fails where the forgotten
10435	`T` did and never silently: an input pin is a wire check at the first
10436	nominal build only when written, and an output built from frozen data is
10437	refused at the first `Dual` activation by the identity comparison on the
10438	opaque leaf or by the `MethodError` inside the user's math, because no lossy
10439	`Dual → Float64` cast exists. The embedding guarantee ([§9.5][s9-5]) is untouched.
10440	
10441	A stateless leaf loses its only tier signal with the arity, and the store
10442	supplies it. [§3.1][s3-1]'s continuous component has a state vector that may be
10443	empty, so a stateless continuous leaf is honestly spelled as a continuous
10444	leaf with zero state fields, `init_x(::C) = (;)`, and a sampled map as
10445	`init_s(::C) = (;)`. The empty NamedTuple is already the vocabulary's "no
10446	state": the bundle law puts a store's letter in the bundle only when the
10447	store is non-empty, so nothing downstream changes. Making the declaration
10448	mandatory is the move [§8.5][s8-5] already makes for `child_connections`,
10449	mandatory even when empty because defining it is the class marker; the store
10450	is the tier marker on the same terms. Every leaf's tier is then on the page
10451	in one place, stateful or not, with no tier by omission. The alternative
10452	that was on the table, an `is_discrete(::C)` trait defaulting to `false`,
10453	would have added a family name and a default, and it would have widened
10454	[D-178][d-178]'s recorded gap: an optional declaration lost to a local scope or a
10455	forgotten import silently drops its feature, so a sampler whose
10456	`is_discrete = true` was lost would silently become a transparent
10457	continuous wire, where a lost `init_s(::C) = (;)` fails loud as a leaf
10458	declaring no store. [D-173][d-173]'s and [D-178][d-178]'s rejections of the trait therefore
10459	stand. The cost is one line on every stateless leaf, the same line an
10460	assembly author already writes for `child_connections`.
10461	
10462	The workspace keeps its scalar because its declaration is by allocation, not
10463	by type. Retyping a type is a pure substitution and total over the
10464	vocabulary; retyping an allocated value is undefined for a factorization, a
10465	plan or a buffer whose size depends on the scalar, which is [D-077][d-077]'s standing
10466	rejection of `similar`-based re-scalaring. The scalar is consumed by the
10467	allocation, so it is not ceremony in the contract sense. Taking it on both
10468	tiers removes the last arity-shaped tier fact: the discrete tier never runs
10469	off-nominal, so `Float64` is the only scalar its allocator can honestly
10470	receive; the ceremony falls on a rare declaration; and [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data
10471	door would bring the scalar there anyway.
10472	
10473	Opaque leaves need no stop of their own. The type walk substitutes
10474	parameters and never looks at fields, so a bulk-data handle whose references
10475	are fields passes through untouched, which is [§4.4][s4-4]'s frozen build-time data
10476	by construction. A handle with a scalar parameter is the case [D-237][d-237] admits, a
10477	different cell type per activation built at `T` by the stage; it walks like
10478	any type, and `Pinned` spells [D-237][d-237]'s other remedy. A mutable type's
10479	parameters pin by rule because no stage can produce a `Vector{Dual}` inside a
10480	handle without copying the grid at every evaluation, so no choice exists
10481	there for a marker to record.
10482	
10483	Annotation (2026-09-25): the marker is read at the top of an entry alone, and
10484	one below it is `IllegalPortType` on both tiers ([D-265][d-265]); a frozen opaque leaf
10485	is admitted at a tolerant entry ([D-264][d-264]); the marker's meaning is "carries no
10486	partials", and an AD-opaque implementation that must participate supplies a
10487	local rule under a tolerant entry ([D-266][d-266]).
10488	
10489	**Rejected.**
10490	- *Superseded position — the mandated two-argument forms on the continuous
10491	  tier ([D-166][d-166], [D-167][d-167]):* ceremony on every continuous leaf for a per-leaf
10492	  choice made on few; reader honesty bought at the price of a second typing
10493	  convention beside the state side's walk; the whole-signature and per-leaf
10494	  forgotten-`T` classes it had to contain exist only because the form exists.
10495	- *A one-argument `init_workspace` with framework re-scalaring:* [D-077][d-077]'s
10496	  grounds stand; reconstruction cannot cover plans, factorizations or
10497	  scalar-dependent structure.
10498	- *Keeping `init_workspace` tier-split:* one arity would stay a tier fact,
10499	  with its vote, its `DeclarationOnWrongTier` arm and a paragraph explaining
10500	  why this declaration alone still splits.
10501	- *A scalar-only marker (`PinnedFloat64`):* cannot pin an `SVector{3,Float64}`
10502	  or a handle whole; the parametric marker pins any leaf type.
10503	- *A parameter-position pin (`MyStruct{Float64, Pinned{Float64}}`):* fails a
10504	  `<: Real` bound unless `Pinned <: Real`, a lie in the type lattice; the
10505	  leaf-level pin covers every known case, and the limit is recorded.
10506	- *Stopping the type walk at every opaque leaf:* removes the handle-with-a-
10507	  scalar-parameter case [D-237][d-237] admits, and re-imports the inexpressibility
10508	  [D-166][d-166] charged [D-079][d-079] with.
10509	- *An `is_discrete(::C)` trait, default `false`:* a new family name and a
10510	  tier by omission; an optional declaration a local scope or a forgotten
10511	  import drops silently, turning a sampler into a transparent wire.
10512	- *Reading a stateless leaf's tier from its parent's `sample_times`:* a
10513	  block that is discrete by design, a `ZOH` or a sampler, could not say so,
10514	  and a user who forgets the entry gets a continuous wire with no ground for
10515	  the build to complain; substituting a stateless map into an unlisted slot
10516	  would silently flip its tier; the tier is the leaf's own fact ([D-056][d-056]).
10517	- *`nothing` as the empty store:* a sentinel return, the class [D-178][d-178] refuses
10518	  and `StoreNotNamedTuple` ([D-247][d-247]) already rejects; the empty NamedTuple
10519	  says the same thing inside the vocabulary.
10520	- *A stateless leaf with no outputs as a legal no-op:* admits a component
10521	  that produces nothing and stores nothing.
10522	- *Accepting `Pinned` silently on a discrete leaf:* a spelling that says
10523	  nothing is the class the declaration layer refuses.
