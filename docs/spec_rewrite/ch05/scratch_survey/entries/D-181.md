6545	### D-181 — Replace once-per-boundary firing with budgeted re-firing
6546	
6547	**Status.** ratified
6548	
6549	**Position.** Once-per-event-per-boundary and its deferral compensation are
6550	replaced by budgeted re-firing: an event may fire up to `firing_budget` times
6551	per boundary (deployment keyword, default 4, integer ≥ 1,
6552	validated/recorded/replay-compared with its siblings), eligibility =
6553	intra-boundary not-holding → holding edge on a last-observed sample initialized
6554	from the prior.
6555	
6556	**Spec.** [§10.4][s10-4], [§10.6][s10-6]
6557	
6558	**Rationale.** Priors are always honest quiescent samples (manufactured-prior
6559	exception, re-arm flag and `EventDeferred` retired; registers = prior +
6560	last-observed + firing count); a re-enabled event fires at its true boundary
6561	against a fresh sweep (no one-step h-artifact); exhaustion loses that event's
6562	further edges for the boundary under a `FiringBudget` warning naming the
6563	chatterer — degrades, never errors; termination budget-bounded (≤
6564	budget·events) instead of structural; rescues [D-020][d-020]'s rejected rounds-cap in
6565	repaired form (per-event, degrade-not-error — the [§10.4][s10-4] doctrine that postdates
6566	the rejection); `event_budget` renamed `localization_budget` (a second budget
6567	made the name ambiguous; semantics unchanged).
6568	
6569	**Rejected.**
6570	- *Deferral, the design of record:* manufactured not-holding prior — a lie
6571	  breaking the localization left-end argument; fires one step late, an
6572	  h-artifact on a logically-instantaneous cascade; collapses multiple re-arms
6573	  into one firing.
6574	- *Plain loss:* once fired, further edges dropped — permanent silent misses
6575	  with no step-size mitigation — cascades are logically simultaneous,
6576	  h-independent.
6577	- *Honest prior + explicit pending bit:* identical behavior to the lie with one
6578	  more register, and composes worse with [D-182][d-182]'s θ=0 check.
6579	- *A joint firing pool:* one chatterer starves innocent cascades — [D-020][d-020]'s
6580	  collective-punishment defect.
6581	- *Reusing one knob for both budgets:* irreconcilable exhaustion semantics —
6582	  localization exhaustion must preserve firings, firing exhaustion must
6583	  suppress them or the iteration never terminates.
