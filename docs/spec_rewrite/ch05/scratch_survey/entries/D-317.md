13480	### D-317 — Split the `Model` from the `Simulation`, and run only the nominal one
13481	
13482	**Status.** ratified
13483	
13484	**Position.** A `Model{T}` is a deployment materialized at one scalar, the
13485	`Deployment` and its `Executor`, and a `Simulation` runs a `Model{Float64}`
13486	beside the data plane, the control plane and the run.
13487	
13488	- `Model{T}` holds the deployment, the executor, its status and its
13489	  diagnostic cell. `Simulation` is a mutable struct of four fields, the
13490	  model, the run, the data plane and the control plane. Every field of each
13491	  type is used by every instance of it.
13492	- Only a `Model{Float64}` is run, and the rule is a type on the one
13493	  constructor that owns a loop. `Simulation(model::Model{Float64};
13494	  join_timeout = 5.0)` refuses a model at any other scalar as a
13495	  `MethodError`, and `Simulation`, `Run`, `Trace` and `TerminationRecord`
13496	  lose their scalar parameter. `Model(deployment, T = Float64; chunk_size =
13497	  16)` is the materialization, and `Simulation(deployment; …)`,
13498	  `Simulation(build; …)` and `Simulation(root; …)` compose through a
13499	  `Float64` model. `_frozen` and the event compile guard in `build.jl` stay
13500	  as they are, and no `AbstractFloat` heuristic exists anywhere.
13501	- A `Model` at another scalar is public, and it is a service's scratch. It
13502	  can be written to, evaluated and stepped frame by frame, with no ticks and
13503	  no events. Activations stay cached on the `Build` by scalar type, and
13504	  executors are instantiated per use and never cached ([D-282][d-282]).
13505	- The clock keeps `t` and `t₀` on the executor, and `init!` on the model
13506	  sets both. The frame index and the boundary ordinal are the run's
13507	  counters. Every door sets them as it builds the run, the loop advances
13508	  them, and every snapshot publishes them.
13509	- The model owns the frame. `frame!(model, k, hooks) → Bool` runs
13510	  `frame_top!(hooks)` before it touches any state and `settled!(hooks)` at
13511	  every settled boundary, `t*` boundaries and the frame top's included. A
13512	  `true` from `settled!` abandons the frame's remainder and is `frame!`'s
13513	  return. The hooks are one `FrameHooks` struct with concrete fields, built
13514	  once per advance, and `NoHooks` does nothing and returns `false`. `init!`
13515	  on the model takes the hooks too, so boundary zero publishes through
13516	  `settled!`. The frame's chattering and firing-budget reports go to the
13517	  model's diagnostic cell, and the drain folds it into the loop's account
13518	  beside the loop's own cell.
13519	- The execution cursor is the model's. It stays on the executor, and
13520	  `frame!` writes every phase, the drain's included, before `frame_top!`
13521	  runs.
13522	- The model's status is `:built`, `:consistent` or `:inconsistent`, written
13523	  only by the model's own doors and catch sites. `Control` holds a running
13524	  flag in place of the lifecycle state, and `lifecycle(sim)` derives the
13525	  five states from the flag, the status and whether the run is closed. A
13526	  set flag reads `running`. With the flag clear, `:built` reads `built`,
13527	  `:inconsistent` reads `errored`, and `:consistent` reads `initialized`
13528	  while the run is open and `stopped` once it is closed.
13529	- The model's `frame!` and `init!` host the catch and build the
13530	  `StepError` through the one constructor. A throw inside a frame writes
13531	  `:inconsistent`, and a throw inside boundary zero writes `:built`. An
13532	  `InterruptException` is never wrapped and writes no status inside a
13533	  frame. The simulation's catch keeps the disposition alone, the mask, the
13534	  interrupt arms and the termination.
13535	- The model's doors are `init!`, `apply!`, `evaluate!`, `integrate!`,
13536	  `boundary!`, `offtick_boundary!`, `boundary_zero!`, `frame!`,
13537	  `phase_bodies`, `warnings`, `port`, `state` and `modes`. The simulation's
13538	  are `run!`, `step!`, `replay!`, `restore!`, `checkpoint`, `live!`, the
13539	  devices, the recording and its accessors. `init!` on a simulation
13540	  forwards to the model's after the operational gate and does the loop's
13541	  bookkeeping around it. The seven model doors a simulation already had
13542	  keep a forwarding method on it, and no model door carries a gate.
13543

13570	**Spec.** [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [§11.5][s11-5], [§11.8][s11-8], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.6][s12-6], [§13.4][s13-4], [§13.5][s13-5], [§14.5][s14-5], [Appendix C][sC]
13571	
13572	**Rationale.** A `Simulation` built at any scalar other than `Float64` ran
13573	without discrete ticks and without state events. Two rules in `build.jl` do
13574	it. `_frozen` gates a discrete component off at every non-`Float64` scalar,
13575	and the event system compiles only under `T === Float64`. Both rules are
13576	correct, since they transcribe [§9.4][s9-4] and the glossary's `nominal`. The
13577	`Float64` activation is the only one that runs in real time, and every
13578	other activation evaluates the model at a frozen instant. A `Dual`
13579	simulation stepped through `run!` was that evaluation, and the suite
13580	exercised it on purpose. The defect was that `Simulation(deployment,
13581	Float32)` was accepted, so a request that reads as a precision choice was
13582	answered with a frozen-instant evaluator, silently.
13583	
13584	The deeper defect was that `Simulation` fused two things. The model's
13585	structure and behaviour lived in the deployment and the executor, and the
13586	runtime that steps it through time and talks to devices lived in the plane,
13587	the control and the run. The stopped-sim services and `init!` need only the
13588	first pair. `apply!` on a simulation was `apply!` on its executor,
13589	`boundary_zero!` read the executor and nothing else, and `port`, `state`
13590	and `modes` read the executor and the build. `trim!`'s `_scratch` already
13591	built the pair anonymously, as `compile(build, activation, schedule)` with
13592	no plane, control or run around it. A `Dual` simulation therefore allocated
13593	a data plane, a control block and a placeholder run it could never use, and
13594	exposed a loop whose semantics at that scalar were an accident of
13595	genericity. Naming the pair puts the scalar rule on the one constructor
13596	that owns a loop, as a type.
13597	
13598	The model owns the frame because a hybrid trajectory is defined by where
13599	its events fire. The `t*` boundaries are model behaviour, and whether
13600	anyone publishes them is the runtime's business. The frame computes its
13601	target as `t₀ + k·h`, so the model needs `t₀`. The origin is time, not a
13602	count, and it stays on the clock beside `t`. The frame index and the
13603	boundary ordinal are counts the trajectory owns, reset at every door, so
13604	they are the run's. The model's `frame!` and `init!` host the catch because
13605	they hold every fact a `StepError` carries, the cursor, `t`, the entry
13606	boundary `k - 1` and the host. The status takes three values because a
13607	model never initialized and one whose sequence threw are unusable for
13608	different reasons, which `lifecycle(sim)` reports as `built` and `errored`.
13609	With `frame!` writing the drain phase and its `comp = 0; fn = :none` reset,
13610	no writer of the cursor remains outside the model.
13611	
13612	**Rejected.**
13613	- *Widening the two `build.jl` rules to `AbstractFloat`:* an unspecified
13614	  third mode, the continuous tier at `Float32` beside a discrete tier
13615	  pinned at `Float64` ([§9.4][s9-4], [D-263][d-263]), with [D-053][d-053]'s embedding rounding at
13616	  every wire and nothing in CI pinning any of it.
13617	- *A type heuristic refusing `AbstractFloat` scalars at the `Simulation`
13618	  door:* it refuses the symptom and keeps the fusion, so a `Dual`
13619	  simulation would still carry a plane, a control and a run it cannot use.
13620	- *Refusing only the advance doors at a non-nominal simulation:* the same
13621	  fusion, and a lifecycle whose legal doors depend on the scalar.
13622	- *`t₀` on the `Deployment`:* the deployment is a value shared by many
13623	  simulations and compared as one at replay and restore. The origin is
13624	  chosen per trajectory at every door, two runs of one deployment start at
13625	  different origins, and replay checks the header's `t₀` as a clock
13626	  mismatch, not a deployment one.
13627	- *`t₀` on the run, with the counters:* the frame computes its target from
13628	  `t₀` and the model owns the frame, so a standalone model would need a run
13629	  to step.
13630	- *The model rethrowing raw and the simulation wrapping:* a standalone
13631	  model's failure would reach its caller without the cursor frame, and the
13632	  facts the wrap reads are the frame's own.
13633	- *Ad hoc closures as hooks:* a reassigned capture becomes a `Core.Box` and
13634	  an abstract captured field makes the return abstract, either one turning
13635	  the hook call into dynamic dispatch. A struct with concrete fields
13636	  specializes `frame!` as a closure would.
13637	- *A boolean model status:* it cannot tell a model never initialized from
13638	  one whose sequence threw.
13639	- *Two cursors, one the model's and one the loop's:* once `frame!` writes
13640	  the drain phase no foreign writer remains, so a second cursor has nothing
13641	  to record.
13642	- *Absorbing the drain into the frame:* the writers' cells, the trace record
13643	  and the replay feed are the simulation's, and a model that drained them
13644	  would hold the runtime again. The top hook keeps the ordering rule that a
13645	  batch taken at the top of frame `k` is recorded at `k`, because the run's
13646	  counter advances where the simulation chooses.
13647	- *Caching executors:* a cached buffer set has two owners, which is [D-282][d-282]'s
13648	  aliasing.
13649	- *Superseded position — the five-field `Simulation` ([D-256][d-256]):* it held the
13650	  model's pair beside the runtime, so every simulation carried a loop
13651	  whatever its scalar.
