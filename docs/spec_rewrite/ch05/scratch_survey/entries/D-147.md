5078	### D-147 — Split the sweep into static interior and boundary variants
5079	
5080	**Status.** ratified
5081	
5082	**Position.** The sweep is two statically distinct variants compiled from one
5083	entry list, because discreteness is a build-time fact and the split by
5084	discreteness is therefore static rather than a runtime gate. The interior sweep
5085	walks continuous entries only and is what RK stage evaluations and localization
5086	guard probes run, so the mid-step ZOH holds by construction ([D-019][d-019]'s ZOH
5087	semantics reaffirmed) — discrete entries are not gated out at runtime, they are
5088	absent from the walk at compile time, and the hottest path in the framework
5089	carries no gating test at all. The boundary sweep walks the full list, with
5090	discrete entries gated by counter modulo against the boundary's tick index —
5091	the pre-existing [§10.5][s10-5] mechanism, now explicitly scoped to boundaries, the only
5092	place anything discrete ever happens ([§10.6][s10-6]'s macro-sequence is its sole
5093	caller). Both blocks split identically: discrete `h_z` entries are as absent
5094	from the interior `h_x` walk as discrete `h_zu` entries are from the interior
5095	`h_xu` walk. Phase-body surface, amending [D-116][d-116]: the two sweep bodies gain an
5096	arity distinction — zero-arg `sweep_hx()`/`sweep_hxu()` are the interior
5097	variants, which is what makes `@ballocated(sweep_hxu()) == 0` a well-defined
5098	measurement of the interior path rather than of whichever tick phase the
5099	simulation happens to be sitting in, while `sweep_hx(tick)`/`sweep_hxu(tick)`
5100	are the boundary variants gating by modulo against the passed index, symmetric
5101	with `ticks(tick)`; `rhs` is unchanged, and each arity is asserted in its own
5102	right at the CI seam. The due set is a property of the boundary, computed once
5103	and reused by every re-sweep of its quiescence iteration (a due component is at
5104	its tick instant for the whole boundary, not for one round of it): the
5105	counter-modulo image of the frame index at a frame top, empty at `t*` — the
5106	tick counter has not advanced there, so no component is at a tick instant and a
5107	naive modulo test against the unadvanced index would wrongly re-admit the
5108	previous frame's due set — and everything at boundary zero, which is [§14.5][s14-5]'s
5109	existing rule (`t₀` is a grid point of every divisor, and no earlier tick
5110	exists for a ZOH to hold) rather than a new one.
5111	
5112	**Spec.** [§5.3][s5-3], [§10.3][s10-3], [§10.4][s10-4], [§10.5][s10-5], [§10.6][s10-6], [§9.7][s9-7], [§14.5][s14-5], [Appendix D][sD]
5113	
5114	**Rationale.** The split also recovers an implicit commitment: [§9.7][s9-7]'s
5115	recompilation-granularity sentence — editing a discrete component invalidates
5116	the boundary body, not the RHS body — is true only under a two-body split, and
5117	was quietly falsified by the single-mechanism reading.
5118	
5119	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-205][d-205] and [D-291][d-291] for
5120	boundary zero, as on [D-067][d-067], and renamed by [D-196][d-196]: `sweep_hx` and
5121	`sweep_hxu` are `sweep_1` and `sweep_2`.
5122	
5123	**Rejected.**
5124	- *Superseded position — counter-modulo gating as the sole mechanism (the prior
5125	  single-mechanism reading, and the finding):* at divisor 1 — the common `n = K
5126	  = 1` configuration — the test admits every discrete entry at every
5127	  evaluation, so an `h_zu` re-runs against interpolated `u` at each interior RK
5128	  stage and at every localization probe, and the `f` reading its cell
5129	  integrates a continuously re-sampled controller — precisely the un-sampling
5130	  [§10.5][s10-5]'s own rejected alternative names, and undiagnosable: such a run is as
5131	  type-stable, allocation-free and bit-reproducible against itself as the
5132	  correct one, so a C172X at `h = 0.02, n = 1` under a 50 Hz FCS simply flies a
5133	  different trajectory with nothing anywhere to flag it.
5134	- *A single parameterized body taking a sentinel "no ticks due" index* on
5135	  mid-step calls: identical semantics, rejected on three counts — it pays a
5136	  dead runtime test per discrete entry in the framework's hottest path, where
5137	  the static split pays nothing at all; it puts discrete entries back into the
5138	  RHS-side body, falsifying [§9.7][s9-7]'s recompilation-granularity claim; and it
5139	  conflates two execution contexts inside one measured body, so `@ballocated`
5140	  at the seam measures whichever context the sentinel selects and the
5141	  zero-allocation canary loses its per-path meaning.
5142	- *Reading the tick index out of simulation state inside an unparameterized
5143	  `sweep_hxu()`:* [D-116][d-116]'s surface read literally — the accessor can then no
5144	  longer express "the interior variant", and the measurement silently depends
5145	  on the tick phase the simulation happens to be paused at.
