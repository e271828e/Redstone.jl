12117	### D-288 — The executor's structure: phase bodies, views, construction, the gate and publication
12118	
12119	**Status.** ratified
12120	
12121	**Position.** The executor's structure is fixed in five parts, and two
12122	options it opens stay uncommitted.
12123	
12124	- Phase bodies are the outer decomposition, and they are semantically
12125	  forced: the order-free stage-1, `x_deriv` and `s_update` blocks, the
12126	  topologically ordered stage-2 block, and the event callables.
12127	- Views are rebuilt per call. Hoisting is the code generator's
12128	  common-subexpression elimination, and the framework maintains none.
12129	- Construction is type-opaque. Entry tuples are built from untyped buffers,
12130	  and the compiled tuple's type has one consumer, the unrolled walk.
12131	- Two options are recorded, not committed: deterministic parallel
12132	  evaluation of the order-free blocks, and finer recompilation granularity.
12133	  Editing a discrete component already invalidates the boundary body, not
12134	  the RHS body, under the two-arity split.
12135	- One gate serves the three tick-sensitive blocks, because due-ness is per
12136	  component, per boundary, never per stage. At a localized event time `t*`
12137	  the empty due set is arity selection.
12138	- Publication is not a phase body. Invoking phase bodies in isolation leaves
12139	  the buffers valid but off-trajectory, and continuing takes `init!`.
12140	
12141	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.5][s7-5], [§9.7][s9-7], [§10.5][s10-5]
12142	
12143	**Rationale.** The phase bodies are the semantically forced outer
12144	decomposition, and their seams cost nothing; views rebuild per call, hoisting
12145	being compiler CSE, whose legality condition is the staleness rule; schedule
12146	tuples are constructed type-opaquely and consumed only by the walk; and the
12147	parallel-evaluation and incremental-recompile doors are recorded (as recorded
12148	in [D-086][d-086]). [D-086][d-086]'s chunking and mitigation ladder are not restated here: they
12149	wait on the compile-time ruling in `pending.md`. That a discrete edit
12150	invalidates the boundary body and not the RHS body is an implicit commitment
12151	the two-body split makes true (as recorded in [D-147][d-147]).
12152	
12153	The boundary gate is one gate for all three tick-sensitive blocks, due-ness
12154	per component per boundary and never per stage, and `t*` emptiness remains
12155	arity selection, with no sentinel index failing every gate (as recorded in
12156	[D-185][d-185]). [D-147][d-147] rules the due set empty at `t*` and the two arities.
12157	
12158	Publication is not a phase body, which is the [§7.5][s7-5] carve-out made
12159	structural; isolated invocation leaves buffers valid but off-trajectory, and
12160	re-`init!` continues (as recorded in [D-116][d-116]).
12161	
12162	Annotation (2026-10-02): [D-289][d-289] is the compile-time ruling this Rationale
12163	waited on. It restates [D-086][d-086]'s chunking in [§9.7][s9-7] and replaces its mitigation
12164	ladder.
12165	
12166	**Rejected.**
12167	- *Framework-maintained view hoisting:* manual cache-invalidation duty for
12168	  loads the compiler hoists exactly where legal; mis-scoping = silent
12169	  stale-state reads. (As recorded in [D-086][d-086].)
12170	- *A single parameterized body taking a sentinel "no ticks due" index on
12171	  mid-step calls:* it pays a dead runtime test per discrete entry in the
12172	  framework's hottest path; it puts discrete entries back into the RHS-side
12173	  body, falsifying [§9.7][s9-7]'s recompilation-granularity claim; and it conflates
12174	  two execution contexts inside one measured body. (As recorded in [D-147][d-147].)
12175	- *A no-publish advance mode on `step!`:* forks execution semantics for CI's
12176	  benefit and breaks [§10.1][s10-1]'s publication-after-every-boundary property. (As
12177	  recorded in [D-116][d-116].)
12178	- *None recorded for type-opaque construction or the two uncommitted
12179	  options.*
