6750	### D-186 — Legalize absolute declarations in any scope via anchors
6751	
6752	**Status.** ratified
6753	
6754	**Position.** Absolute declarations become legal in any scope — anchors,
6755	severing, and the derive-vs-declare rule: the `(T, τ)` pair an `Absolute` entry
6756	establishes is an **anchor**, severing its child from the enclosing scope's
6757	grid — no relation to the scope's ticks remains, so `K ≥ 1` reads "a child
6758	cannot tick faster than the scope it is *relative* to" (an anchored child may
6759	outpace its scope), the fastest-member convention counts relative members only,
6760	and anchored-vs-relative phase relationships are deployment-emergent, the
6761	printable bound schedule the way to audit them.
6762	
6763	**Spec.** [§10.5][s10-5], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [Appendix B][sB]
6764	
6765	**Rationale.** The doctrinal line sharpened rather than dropped: an absolute
6766	declaration inside a library type is legitimate when the rate is **a fact about
6767	the modeled system, not a preference about the simulation** (a GPS's emission
6768	rate, a bus schedule, an ADC's conversion offset — forcing them to the root
6769	breaks encapsulation, the root re-declaring device internals), deployment
6770	preferences keep the exposed-multiplier idiom, and the framework cannot police
6771	the distinction — authoring doctrine, one paragraph beside the declaration
6772	forms; never-cache-`Δt` fully intact (the pinning sits in the enclosing
6773	assembly's `sample_times`, the same site the multiplier lives; the component
6774	type stays rate-agnostic and consumes the bundle's `Δt`); Stratum A compiles to
6775	**`(anchor, m, c)` triples** — seed `(A₀, 1, 0)` with anchor 0 the symbolic
6776	base grid, `Relative(K, φ)` step `(a, K·mₛ, cₛ + φ·mₛ)`, `Absolute` severs and
6777	re-seeds `(Aₖ, 1, 0)`, nested anchors just seeding again — the `Build` gaining
6778	the anchor and component tables with rate chain, final divisors for
6779	anchored entries deferred to binding (they do not exist until `Δt_base` does,
6780	and one `Build` backs many `Simulation`s); deployment: the **constraint pool**
6781	is every anchor period plus every nonzero offset, `Δt_base` admissible iff it
6782	divides the pool's GCD; **derive `Δt_base` only when every discrete component
6783	is anchored** — an unanchored period is `m·Δt_base`, and silent derivation
6784	would let an anchor edit anywhere rescale it, action at a distance — otherwise
6785	deployment must declare it, the refusal constructive; `Δt_base` binds from
6786	exactly one of three cross-validated sources — explicit keyword
6787	(`Rational`/`Period`/`Hz`, `n` derived and validated an integer ≥ 1), the `n·h`
6788	product, or derivation at the pool GCD — and resolution is one exact division
6789	pair per anchor (`DeploymentInvalid` naming the anchor's declaring scope and
6790	key when non-dividing) plus one multiply-add per component, the residue
6791	invariant surviving into the bound `(D, Φ, Δt)`.
6792	
6793	**Rejected.**
6794	- *Root-only absolute declaration (the superseded [§10.5][s10-5] clause):* right about
6795	  deployment preferences, wrong for device-intrinsic rates; kept only as the
6796	  default-form rationale.
6797	- *Silent `Δt_base` derivation with unanchored components present:* the
6798	  logger-rescaled-by-an-offset-edit case.
6799	- *Absolute pinning from outside a subtree's contract:* standing rejection,
6800	  upheld.
6801	- *Any required relation between anchors:* they share only the base lattice,
6802	  which the grid derivation guarantees.
