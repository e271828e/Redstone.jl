13065	### D-312 — Settle the leaf blocks: `Constant` pins, `UnitDelay` holds its initial value, `Freeze` strips by broadcast
13066	
13067	**Status.** ratified
13068	
13069	**Position.** The three leaf blocks of [§13.7][s13-7] take these spellings.
13070	
13071	- `Constant{V}` declares `(out = Pinned{V},)` and is constructed as
13072	  `Constant(v)`. The sketch's `(out = V,)` would walk a `Float64`-bearing `V`
13073	  with the activation.
13074	- `UnitDelay{V}` carries `v0::V`, which `s_init` returns, and is constructed
13075	  as `UnitDelay(v0)`. `V` is isbits by [D-231][d-231]. Its ports are `in` and `out`.
13076	- `Freeze{V}` strips with `ForwardDiff.value.(u.in)`, and `V` is constrained
13077	  to `Real` and `StaticArray` at the type, so a struct `V` is refused at the
13078	  spelling. Its ports are `in` and `out`.
13079	
13080	**Spec.** [§13.7][s13-7]
13081	
13082	**Rationale.** The prose already calls `Constant` deliberately pinned, and
13083	`Pinned{V}` is the spelling of that under [D-263][d-263]. A tolerant consumer accepts
13084	the frozen arrival, which is what the zero-contributor wire and the rig stub
13085	need, and the build accepts the marker on a `Bool` and on a handle. A
13086	constant is never seeded, so it has zero partials under every activation,
13087	and the pinned form is strictly more connectable than a walking one: a
13088	walking producer may not feed a pinned entry. An initial value is instance
13089	data like the constant's value, and the contract rule constrains a store's
13090	shape, not its values; `zero(V)` demands a method a custom `V` may not
13091	define. The broadcast is the identity at nominal, allocates nothing on a
13092	`Real` or a `StaticArray` and strips a `Dual` one leafwise, measured in a
13093	stage body. The leaf-walk helpers go through an untyped vector, so they are
13094	build-time tools, and a struct-valued `Freeze` needs an allocation-free
13095	leafwise map that waits for a model to demonstrate it.
13096	
13097	**Rejected.**
13098	- *`(out = V,)` on `Constant`:* a walking port whose body returns a nominal
13099	  value, the opposite of what the prose states.
13100	- *The marker as the type parameter, `Constant{Pinned{Float64}}` beside a
13101	  walking `Constant{Float64}`:* `Pinned` is never instantiated, so the value
13102	  field cannot be typed by the parameter and `Constant(v)` can no longer
13103	  infer it; and the walking form has no case where it is correct, so the
13104	  choice is a question with one answer. `Junction` already passes the marker
13105	  through where the choice is the user's.
13106	- *`zero(V)` as the delay's initial value:* an extra method on the user's
13107	  type for a value the instance should carry.
13108	- *Stripping through the build-time leaf helpers:* allocates in a stage body,
13109	  against [§7.5][s7-5].
13110	- *Writing the allocation-free leafwise map now:* machinery with no
13111	  demonstrated user.
13112	
13113	Annotation (2026-10-06): the pin generalizes. A library block whose output
13114	depends on neither its continuous state nor a walking input, only on instance
13115	data and a mode, declares `(out = Pinned{V},)` by the same argument: it is
13116	never seeded, carries zero partials under every activation, and the pinned
13117	form is the more connectable one. `Step` and `Relay` are the second and third
13118	instances, ruled when they shipped; `UnitDelay` stays plain because the
13119	discrete tier pins wholesale ([D-263][d-263]).
