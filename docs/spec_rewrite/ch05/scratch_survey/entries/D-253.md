9661	### D-253 — Stratum products as artifacts: `Structure`, `Dataflow`, `Events`, one activation store
9662	
9663	**Status.** ratified
9664	
9665	**Position.** Each stratum returns a named, immutable artifact, and the
9666	`Build` is their bundle.
9667	
9668	- `Structure` replaces `Flat` as Stratum A's product, with the tiers moved
9669	  in, per component its rate chain of `Relative`/`Absolute` links, and the
9670	  scope triples of assemblies with an explicit `sample_times` key.
9671	- `Dataflow` is Stratum B's product: per component the stage-1 and stage-2
9672	  name sets, the feedthrough edges with their port and face, and the
9673	  execution order.
9674	- `Events` is Stratum B's other product: per component the event names,
9675	  detection policies and bundle names, produced as B's last step, after the
9676	  nominal stage probes.
9677	- Stratum B is its own function, consuming the structure and returning the
9678	  dataflow, the events and the nominal `Float64` activation, assembled from
9679	  its own probe chain. Stratum C runs for every other scalar, taking the
9680	  structure, the dataflow and the nominal activation as the carry for frozen
9681	  components. No product changes across activations. (Amended by [D-259][d-259]:
9682	  `Events` is probed against the complete nominal products, so B runs the
9683	  whole nominal chain, and a C run at `Float64` would be a second pass.)
9684	- `Build` is structure, dataflow, events and the activations, the nominal
9685	  and the cache merged into one dictionary keyed by scalar type under the
9686	  existing lock.
9687	
9688	  Annotation (2026-10-02): "under the existing lock" defers to [D-135][d-135] and
9689	  [D-281][d-281]. The mechanism is unspecified, and a lock is one that suffices.
9690	- Structural consumers read the products, not the `Activation`: readers,
9691	  trim, the tracer, `compile`'s key slicing and the runtime field-error
9692	  species take their name lists from `Structure` and `Dataflow`.
9693	- [§9.1][s9-1] says Stratum B is the nominal evaluation's structural half, and the
9694	  nominal activation is B's products plus C's typing.
9695	
9696	  Annotation (2026-10-02): superseded by [D-259][d-259]. The nominal evaluation
9697	  fixes both the structure and the `Float64` typing, and no activation step
9698	  runs at `Float64`.
9699	
9700	**Spec.** [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.3][s9-3], [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.7][s9-7], [§13.3][s13-3], [§13.4][s13-4], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.1][s14-1], [§14.4][s14-4]
9701	
9702	**Rationale.** A step is well-defined when it consumes one new piece of
9703	information and fixes everything that piece determines. Stratum A consumes
9704	the instance. Stratum B consumes one nominal evaluation. Stratum C consumes
9705	a scalar type. The strata were sound as an order, but their products were
9706	not named: A returned a `Flat` with the tiers alongside, B returned nothing
9707	of its own, and every structural fact B fixed lived on the `Float64`
9708	`Activation`, so a reader that wanted a name list took it from a `T`-typed
9709	product. That made the tracer, the readers and trim depend on an activation
9710	for facts no activation determines, and it made "the nominal activation" a
9711	special field rather than the entry for `Float64` in a store of activations.
9712	Naming the products makes the dependency order the type signature. `Events`
9713	is separate from `Dataflow` because it is fixed by a different input, the
9714	event declarations read after the stage probes, and a consumer of the
9715	execution order should not need the event table. [D-049][d-049]'s reasons for a
9716	standalone `Build` hold unchanged: CI targets `build`, conditions resolve
9717	against it, bindings validate against it, one build backs many
9718	deployments. The `Dataflow` name stays although the prose calls the order
9719	"execution order" ([D-258][d-258]): the artifact carries the edges and the name sets
9720	as well as the order, and a graph is what "dataflow" names.
9721	
9722	**Rejected.**
9723	- *Keeping `Flat` and the `Activation` as the only carriers:* structural
9724	  consumers stay coupled to a scalar type, and Stratum B has no product to
9725	  test or print.
9726	- *Folding `Events` into `Dataflow`:* produced from different inputs at
9727	  different points in B; a consumer of the order would carry the event table.
9728	- *One function for Strata B and C:* the nominal activation would again fix
9729	  structure and type in one call, which is the coupling being removed.
9730	- *Renaming `Dataflow` to `ExecutionOrder`:* misdescribes two of its three
9731	  parts.
