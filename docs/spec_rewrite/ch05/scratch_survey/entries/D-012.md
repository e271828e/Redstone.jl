524	### D-012 — Set-propagation tracers and SCC-based cycle diagnostics
525	
526	**Status.** ratified
527	
528	**Position.** Set-propagation tracers (global + sampled-local),
529	diagnostic-only; cycle diagnostics from SCC decomposition of the Stratum-B
530	topological stall (one SCC = one named loop), classified by a schedule-free
531	per-member local trace at the probe point (each member evaluated once in
532	isolation; in-cycle cells from `probe_value` under tracer tags) — real iff
533	every structural hop survives, artificial iff one dies; conservative on
534	discrete members (pinned signatures admit no tracer scalar), classification
535	optional on the error. Superseded on the verdict clause by [D-245][d-245] — over a
536	cluster the loop is real iff a traced cycle survives, which reduces to this
537	reading on a simple cycle.
538	
539	**Spec.** [§5.6][s5-6], [§9.4][s9-4]
540	
541	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
542	
543	**Rejected.**
544	- *Dual-based tracing:* derivative-zero blind spot.
545	- *Tracing as scheduling input:* soundness.
546	- *Naming the cycle by the topological stall's raw residue:* over-reports — the
547	  residue holds the innocent downstream cone.
548	- *Naming the cycle by a single DFS back edge:* under-reports — one edge of a
549	  possibly large tangle; both alternatives rejected, which is what the SCC
550	  decomposition buys.
