9221	### D-245 — Classify a cycle cluster by a surviving traced cycle and carry each member's tracing mode
9222	
9223	**Status.** ratified
9224	
9225	**Position.** The classifier decides a cluster as one graph question, and the
9226	diagnostic carries what that question rested on.
9227	- Every wire holds. A hop inside a member holds iff the member's traced map
9228	  routes the entering face to the leaving port. The loop is real iff a cycle
9229	  survives in that graph, artificial iff none does.
9230	- Every dead hop is listed under either verdict.
9231	- Each member records its tracing mode: global, sampled or structural.
9232	
9233	**Spec.** [§5.6][s5-6], [Appendix C][sC]
9234	
9235	**Rationale.** [D-012][d-012] states the verdict for a simple cycle: real iff every
9236	hop survives. A strongly connected component is one cluster of possibly many
9237	cycles, and read literally over a cluster that sentence misreports the common
9238	tangle. A genuine loop with one false chord would come out artificial, and
9239	the ladder ([D-140][d-140]) would steer the author toward narrowing a contract on a
9240	loop that only a state can break. The graph question keeps [D-012][d-012]'s answer on
9241	a simple cycle and gives the tangle its true one. Listing the dead hops under
9242	a real verdict keeps the false chords visible, since each is a wire the
9243	author can still delete. The mode per member is [§5.6][s5-6]'s own demand that the
9244	caveats ride in the diagnostic rather than being assumed away. A dead hop the
9245	sampled tracer found rests on the paths it took, a structural map can never
9246	kill a hop, and a reader weighing the hint needs to know which. The
9247	structural answer extends from discrete members to any continuous face or
9248	port declared without a walking leaf, for the same reason: no tracer scalar
9249	can enter it.
9250	
9251	**Rejected.**
9252	- *Real iff every hop of every cycle in the cluster survives:* the literal
9253	  reading of [D-012][d-012] over a cluster; it calls a tangle with one dead chord
9254	  artificial and offers the wrong rung.
9255	- *Listing dead hops only under an artificial verdict:* hides false wires the
9256	  author could delete from a loop that is real through another path.
9257	- *Omitting the tracing mode:* a sampled "artificial" and a global one would
9258	  read alike, and the sampled miss [§5.6][s5-6] documents would be assumed away in
9259	  the message.
