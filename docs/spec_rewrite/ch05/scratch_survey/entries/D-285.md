11974	### D-285 — Probe return checks, hand-down and placeholder sources, and totality's two homes
11975	
11976	**Status.** ratified
11977	
11978	**Position.** The probe checks a stage return's shape, sources the hand-down
11979	and the period from what the build holds, and two habits that totality
11980	forbids in stage code have homes outside it.
11981	
11982	- A stage returning anything other than a `NamedTuple` fails at the probe.
11983	- A stage returning bare `(;)` produces no ports and is `DeadStage`,
11984	  fail-fast.
11985	- The stage-1 hand-down, `y_x` on the continuous tier and `y_s` on the
11986	  discrete, is sourced from the stage-1 probes' returns.
11987	- Discrete-tier probes supply a placeholder period, `1.0`, in the bundle. It
11988	  is a fabricated, probe-scoped value like `t`.
11989	- A plausibility check meaning "stop the run" is a published `Bool` output
11990	  face plus `stop_on`.
11991	- A self-consistency assert is a regression test, and its home is the test
11992	  suite.
11993	
11994	**Spec.** [§5.2][s5-2], [§9.3][s9-3], [Appendix C][sC]
11995	
11996	**Rationale.** [D-036][d-036] rules that stage returns are `NamedTuple`s. That the
11997	probe is where any other return fails has no prior entry; [§9.3][s9-3] states it.
11998	
11999	[D-165][d-165] made a stage producing nothing a dead-stage build error, `DeadStage`
12000	(as recorded in [D-165][d-165]). [D-194][d-194], which superseded it, simplified the ground to
12001	a stage returning `(;)` and did not restate the error. Its fail-fast policy
12002	is [Appendix C][sC]'s: the probe is user-code evaluation, which fails fast under
12003	[§13.1][s13-1].
12004	
12005	The stage-1 probe's return is what [§9.3][s9-3] already sourced the hand-down from
12006	(as recorded in [D-169][d-169]). [D-252][d-252], which superseded [D-169][d-169], removed the
12007	auto-published names the hand-down had to exclude and did not restate the
12008	sourcing.
12009	
12010	[D-115][d-115] calls the `Δt = 1.0` placeholder settled, and no entry settling it was
12011	found. [§9.3][s9-3] states it, with its reason: `Δt` in seconds does not exist until
12012	the `Deployment` constructor binds `Δt_base`, and deployment post-dates the
12013	build.
12014	
12015	Three patterns in shipped landing-gear code get three dispositions (as
12016	recorded in [D-142][d-142]). Plausibility termination migrates to a published `Bool`
12017	output face plus `stop_on`, [§13.5][s13-5]'s existing machinery. A numerical
12018	self-consistency assert is a regression test living in stage code, and its
12019	legitimate home is the test suite. It is also the most probe-fragile of the
12020	three, since a near-degenerate synthesized geometry keeps the cancellation
12021	algebraically exact while missing an absolute tolerance in floating point.
12022	The third, defensive exhaustiveness, is [D-142][d-142]'s Position.
12023

12025	the run" publishes a `StopFlag` port, which `StopRequest` spells, and no
12026	keyword names it.

12033	- *None recorded for the return-shape, dead-stage, hand-down and placeholder
