11714	### D-279 — Name the assembly's boundary declarations by `u` and `y`, and shorten `x_derivative`
11715	
11716	**Status.** ratified
11717	
11718	**Position.** The letters `u` and `y` name the two sides of a component's
11719	contract at either class, and four declarations and one keyword are renamed
11720	on that ground or beside it.
11721	
11722	- The renames: `input_connections` → `u_connections`, `output_connections` →
11723	  `y_connections`, `child_connections` → `inner_connections`, `x_derivative`
11724	  → `x_deriv`, and `fragment`'s `inputs` keyword → `u`. Signatures, entry
11725	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
11726	- [D-267][d-267]'s rule is widened, not replaced. A leaf declaration is still named
11727	  by the bundle field it defines. An assembly has no bundle, and its boundary
11728	  declarations take the letter of the contract side they declare.
11729	- What stays: `x_projection`, `state_events`, `sample_times`, `Group`'s
11730	  keywords `wires`, `inputs` and `outputs`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
11731	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, every `get_` selector and every
11732	  diagnostic kind name. Prose keeps "root input", "the state derivative" and
11733	  "the projection".
11734	- `deriv` joins `implementation.md`'s abbreviation roster.
11735	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
11736	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.

11743	**Rationale.** [§8.6][s8-6] already says that the root's input faces come from the
11744	assembly's boundary declaration or from `u_types`, following the root's
11745	class, and that nothing downstream distinguishes the two ([D-208][d-208]). Two
11746	declarations that fill one role at the two classes were spelled in two
11747	vocabularies. Under this entry `u_types` and `u_connections` read as the two
11748	ways a component declares its input side, and `u_` lists both under tab
11749	completion. Control convention gives a composite system a `u` and a `y` as
11750	it gives them to a block. [D-267][d-267] kept the three connection names because an
11751	assembly defines no bundle field. That is still true. The letter names the
11752	contract side, which on a leaf is also the bundle field. The suffix keeps
11753	the difference [D-041][d-041] and [D-170][d-170] drew: `_types` declares typed faces, and
11754	`_connections` routes faces whose types derive from the internal endpoints.
11755	
11756	`fragment` took three letters and one word. Its fourth payload holds values
11757	for the fields of `u`, so the letter fits. `x`, `s` and `m` resolve only at a
11758	leaf, while this payload resolves at any level, assemblies included. The
11759	keyword is therefore clean only because the boundary rename makes `u` mean
11760	something on an assembly.
11761	
11762	[D-170][d-170] rejected `inner_connections` for its visual near-collision with
11763	`input_connections`. That neighbour is gone. Every entry of all three
11764	declarations names a child face, so "child" told none of them apart. "Inner"
11765	says that both ends sit inside the boundary, against one end on it. The trio
11766	is uneven in form, one word and two letters, and the form is accurate: the
11767	first is the class marker with no leaf counterpart, and the other two mirror
11768	`u_types` and `y_types`.
11769	
11770	[D-267][d-267] rejected `x_deriv` on two grounds, and neither holds. The family does
11771	not spell its words in full: `x_init`, `s_init`, `m_init` and `ws_init`
11772	abbreviate already. `x_deriv` beside `x_projection` is no more uneven than
11773	`x_init` beside it. The read side already says `get_deriv`, so the
11774	declaration and its selector now spell one word one way. It is also the
11775	declaration authors write most, once per continuous leaf. "Deriv" could be
11776	misread as "derived", a word the spec uses for contracts, but `get_deriv`
11777	had fixed its meaning before this entry.
11778	
11779	Annotation (2026-10-02): partly superseded by [D-303][d-303]. The three connection
11780	names fall, with the widening of [D-267][d-267]'s rule to an assembly's boundary
11781	declarations that produced two of them. The declarations are `inner_wires`,
11782	`input_wires` and `output_wires`, the first `local_wires` since [D-314][d-314]. The listing of `Group`'s keywords among
11783	what stays falls too, since those keywords and fields now take the
11784	declaration names and `sample_times`. `x_deriv`, the `fragment` keyword `u`
11785	and the rejection of bare `wires` as a function name stand. The keyword now
11786	rests on [§8.6][s8-6] alone, because its ground in the `u_connections` name is gone.
11787	
11788	**Rejected.**
11789	- *`sibling_connections`:* [§6.1][s6-1]'s own adjective for these wires, but a child
11790	  wired to itself is expressible (`"plant/power" => "plant/u"`), and a child
11791	  is not its own sibling.
11792	- *`wires`:* the spec's everyday noun and `Group`'s field, but it drops the
11793	  `_connections` suffix that ties the three declarations, and a package
11794	  function of that name would take it from every `wires` local and from the
11795	  `Group` field under `implementation.md`'s naming rules.
11796	- *Keeping `child_connections` beside `u_connections` and `y_connections`:*
11797	  the name discriminates nothing, since all three declarations connect child
11798	  faces.
11799	- *`x_proj`:* `proj` is neither frequent nor established. Few leaves write a
11800	  projection, tab completion spares the writer, and a reader who meets the
11801	  name rarely is better served by the full word.
11802	- *`u` and `y` as `Group`'s keywords:* they hold connection entries, not
11803	  values, and `u = ("in" => "c/e",)` would read as a value of `u`.
11804	- *Letters on the helpers and selectors:* [D-267][d-267] separates declarations from
11805	  these, and [D-076][d-076]'s separation of `u_types` from `input_faces` stands.
