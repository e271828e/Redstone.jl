11807	### D-280 — Build a `Dual` activation of every component in CI
11808	
11809	**Status.** ratified
11810	
11811	**Position.** The repository's test suite builds a `Dual` activation of every
11812	component, with `build(world; activations = (Float64, ProbeDual))`.
11813	Linearizability is an invariant held by this policy.
11814	
11815	**Spec.** [§7.2][s7-2], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [Appendix B][sB]
11816	
11817	**Rationale.** Activations are lazy ([D-052][d-052]), so a successful `build` does not
11818	certify the model linearizable. A pinned `Float64` hidden in a constructor, or
11819	a misplaced `Pinned` leaf, lurks until the first `Dual` activation. The policy
11820	moves that first activation into CI. An activation is a re-run of the
11821	activation step alone, cheap per the kernel prototype ([D-166][d-166]). [D-166][d-166]'s annotation records
11822	that the policy survived [D-166][d-166]'s supersession by [D-263][d-263]. [D-099][d-099] spells the probe
11823	scalar, and [D-275][d-275] makes the keyword the whole entry point.
11824	
11825	**Rejected.**
11826	- *Eager `Dual` at every build:* doubles compile latency for a CI-only
11827	  guarantee. (As recorded in [D-052][d-052].)
