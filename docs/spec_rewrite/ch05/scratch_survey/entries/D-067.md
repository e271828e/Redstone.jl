1956	### D-067 — Boundary zero runs the macro-sequence with an empty integrate
1957	
1958	**Status.** ratified
1959	
1960	**Position.** Boundary zero is the [§10.6][s10-6] macro-sequence run with an empty
1961	integrate: project → sweep (every tick due; discrete stages publish from the
1962	authored `z`) → events to quiescence → due `g` updates → header capture + first
1963	snapshot.
1964	
1965	- Interval alignment is a taught contract, sibling of [§8.6][s8-6]'s boundary-sampling
1966	  line: a boundary's update is the *outgoing* transition — `z_{k+1}` from
1967	  `t_k`'s samples — so boundary zero's incoming transitions on both tiers are
1968	  replaced by authorship, and the update at `t₀` is the `t₀` sample's only
1969	  chance.
1970	- `t₀` is an init-service argument anchoring the harmonic grid (conditions
1971	  time-free; `capture` returns condition and time separately).
1972	- Trim iterations bypass boundaries entirely — raw write→sweep→read on the
1973	  activation — and only the commit runs boundary zero, a guard firing at commit
1974	  replacing today's hand-written trim asserts.
1975	
1976	**Spec.** [§10.6][s10-6], [§14.5][s14-5], [§5.3][s5-3], [§3.4][s3-4], [§8.6][s8-6]
1977	
1978	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
1979	
1980	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-205][d-205] and [D-291][d-291]. At
1981	boundary zero the due set is the `Φ = 0` components, which governs the
1982	`s_update` calls alone; every discrete output stage publishes, due or not.
1983	
1984	**Rejected.**
1985	- *Condition-authoritative boundary zero:* no events/updates: delays the
1986	  identical firings one step while hiding non-quiescence — [§11.7][s11-7]'s
1987	  insurance-masking-invariants pattern; skipping the update deletes the `t₀`
1988	  sample and starts the sampled-data lattice one period late — the authored
1989	  `z(0)` needs no protection, it is published at `t₀` regardless.
1990	- *Update before the sweep or republish-from-`z⁺`:* stale-table sampling or
1991	  Mealy update-feedthrough: same-boundary circularity, kills [§10.6][s10-6]'s structural
1992	  termination.
1993	- *`t₀` as a condition entry:* time is not a store.
