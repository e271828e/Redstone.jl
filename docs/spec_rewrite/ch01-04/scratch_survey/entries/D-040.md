1278	### D-040 — Slash-string paths as the canonical path form
1279	
1280	**Status.** ratified
1281	
1282	**Position.** Slash-string paths, relative to the declaring assembly, one
1283	canonical form shared by declarations, diagnostics, devices and logs.
1284	
1285	**Spec.** [§8.6][s8-6]
1286	
1287	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
1288	
1289	**Rejected.**
1290	- *Instance navigation:* `===`-identical symmetric siblings make
1291	  path-from-instance unrecoverable — proxies remain sugar.
1292	- *Symbol tuples:* structure without readability.
1293	- *Dotted paths:* false Julia-property affordance — the last segment is a port,
1294	  not a field.
