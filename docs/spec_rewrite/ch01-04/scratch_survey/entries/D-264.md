10572	### D-264 — Admit a frozen opaque leaf at a tolerant entry
10573	
10574	**Status.** ratified
10575	
10576	**Position.** The wire relation admits a pinned or discrete producer of an
10577	opaque leaf at an unpinned entry of that leaf's type, as the producer's cell.
10578	The identity rule for an opaque leaf ([D-237][d-237]) governs a store and the
10579	producer's own conformance check alone, never a wire.
10580	
10581	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5], [Appendix C][sC]
10582	
10583	**Rationale.** The refusal came from one predicate serving two questions.
10584	`_accepts` decides both the store's conformance check and the wire relation,
10585	and at a store its opaque short-circuit is essential: a producer's output
10586	cell at a `Dual` activation is a `Vector{Handle{Dual}}` when the declaration
10587	walks, and a `Handle{Float64}` cannot be placed there without the framework
10588	constructing a handle, which it refuses to do. At a wire nothing is
10589	constructed. The entry is a bound, and the consumer gathers the producer's
10590	cell as stored. Embedding at a tolerant scalar entry is a type-level
10591	admission plus Julia's promotion inside the consumer's math, and a handle
10592	behaves identically: the consumer reads `u.terrain.h0::Float64` beside `Dual`
10593	state and its arithmetic promotes. The derivative through the handle is zero
10594	along every seeded direction, and that is the true derivative, because the
10595	producer declared that no partials enter it. [§9.5][s9-5]'s argument for why a
10596	`Float64` at a walking leaf is honest applies unchanged. [D-238][d-238]'s parameter
10597	walk already gives the right answer once the short-circuit is lifted: lifting
10598	`Handle{Float64}` at the parameter position where the entry has `T` yields
10599	`Handle{Dual}`, the entry itself.
10600	
10601	The positive ground is [§8.2][s8-2]'s substitution promise, that a walking producer,
10602	a frozen discrete producer and a root input are all admissible behind a
10603	tolerant entry. For handles that promise was broken in both frozen forms. A
10604	library's landing gear declaring `terrain = TerrainField{Float64}` could not
10605	be fed by a static terrain source at all, and its author could not repair it
10606	without pinning the entry, which would then refuse a moving deck instead.
10607	The wire pass reported the direction under `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` with
10608	the endpoints reversed, calling the entry frozen and the producer walking.
10609	
10610	Two risks are specific to handles, and both fail loudly at the `Dual` probe.
10611	For numbers Julia promotes a `Float64` and a `Dual` in any expression; for a
10612	handle there is no such machinery, so a consumer signature that ties the
10613	handle's parameter to another argument's (`height(f::H{T}, p::SVector{3,T})
10614	where {T}`) fails with a `MethodError` whenever the handle is frozen and the
10615	position is not. That is the genericity obligation a tolerant entry already
10616	claims, checked by the probe as [§8.2][s8-2] says. And pass-through is refused where
10617	scalar pass-through is embedded: a consumer that re-emits a frozen handle
10618	under an unpinned output hits the store's identity rule, so a handle's pin is
10619	a property of its chain, and every component that forwards it pins that
10620	output too. No silent wrong Jacobian was found. The misplaced-pin class is
10621	covered identically: a pinned producer that builds `Handle(x.h + 1.0, z)` at
10622	a `Dual` activation produces `Handle{Dual}`, the identity check refuses it,
10623	and the pin hint fires. `companions/handle_walk_walkthrough.md` works the
10624	example through.
10625	
10626	**Rejected.**
10627	- *A named, collected refusal for the direction (a frozen face at a walking
10628	  entry), with remedies to pin the entry or build the handle at `T`:* breaks
10629	  the substitution promise for every frozen handle producer, discrete ones
10630	  included, and asks a library consumer to choose between pinning its entry
10631	  and refusing every static source; the refusal was the store's identity rule
10632	  applied to a question it was not written for.
10633	- *An embedding for opaque leaves (constructing the handle at the entry):*
10634	  the framework never builds handles; a cross-eltype construction can be a
10635	  grid copy per evaluation, which [D-237][d-237]'s identity rule exists to refuse.
