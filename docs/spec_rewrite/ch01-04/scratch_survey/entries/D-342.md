14067	### D-342 — Pure output stages, the dependence-class names and supervisory resets
14068	
14069	**Status.** ratified
14070	
14071	**Position.** Seven stage-role rulings that [§5.3][s5-3] states are fixed here.
14072	
14073	- A stage's name states its dependence class, state-only or
14074	  state-plus-input. Modes fold under the state, and the ambient `t`, `Δt`
14075	  and `ws` go unnamed.
14076	- Output stages are pure. No mutable cache carries values between the
14077	  derivative and the outputs.
14078	- Accepting duplicate computation is rejected as a drift bug class, an
14079	  edited law in one place and a stale copy in the other.
14080	- The orthodox `f(x, u)` with atomized components is not the standard idiom,
14081	  since it doubles components and wiring in the domain-normal overlap case.
14082	- A one-shot design-time gain is a construction-time parameter or a
14083	  stopped-sim service output.
14084	- A commanded reset is a same-tick input consumed in the output stage, and
14085	  the update law stores the matching state. A reset honored only in
14086	  `s_update` gives one-tick-late outputs, legal where that is meant and never
14087	  the idiom.
14088	- The update law does not republish from `s⁺`, so the output stage is the
14089	  only same-tick path.
14090	
14091	**Spec.** [§5.3][s5-3], [Appendix A][sA]
14092	
14093	**Rationale.** Each bullet restates what an existing entry recorded outside
14094	its Position. The dependence class and the folded modes were recorded in
14095	[D-075][d-075]'s Rationale, and the class again in [D-220][d-220]'s. Purity, the drift bug
14096	class and the orthodox split were recorded in [D-015][d-015]'s Rejected list. The
14097	gains and the reset were recorded in [D-089][d-089]'s Rationale and Rejected list.
14098	The refused republishing was recorded in [D-067][d-067]'s Rejected list and applied
14099	in [D-089][d-089]'s.
