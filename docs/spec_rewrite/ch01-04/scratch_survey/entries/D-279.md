11761	### D-279 — Name the assembly's boundary declarations by `u` and `y`, and shorten `x_derivative`
11762	
11763	**Status.** ratified
11764	
11765	**Position.** The letters `u` and `y` name the two sides of a component's
11766	contract at either class, and four declarations and one keyword are renamed
11767	on that ground or beside it.
11768	
11769	- The renames: `input_connections` → `u_connections`, `output_connections` →
11770	  `y_connections`, `child_connections` → `inner_connections`, `x_derivative`
11771	  → `x_deriv`, and `fragment`'s `inputs` keyword → `u`. Signatures, entry
11772	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
11773	- [D-267][d-267]'s rule is widened, not replaced. A leaf declaration is still named
11774	  by the bundle field it defines. An assembly has no bundle, and its boundary
11775	  declarations take the letter of the contract side they declare.
11776	- What stays: `x_projection`, `state_events`, `sample_times`, `Group`'s
11777	  keywords `wires`, `inputs` and `outputs`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
11778	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, every `get_` selector and every
11779	  diagnostic kind name. Prose keeps "root input", "the state derivative" and
11780	  "the projection".
11781	- `deriv` joins `implementation.md`'s abbreviation roster.
11782	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
11783	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.
11784

11790	**Rationale.** [§8.6][s8-6] already says that the root's input faces come from the
11791	assembly's boundary declaration or from `u_types`, following the root's
11792	class, and that nothing downstream distinguishes the two ([D-208][d-208]). Two
11793	declarations that fill one role at the two classes were spelled in two
11794	vocabularies. Under this entry `u_types` and `u_connections` read as the two
11795	ways a component declares its input side, and `u_` lists both under tab
11796	completion. Control convention gives a composite system a `u` and a `y` as
11797	it gives them to a block. [D-267][d-267] kept the three connection names because an
11798	assembly defines no bundle field. That is still true. The letter names the
11799	contract side, which on a leaf is also the bundle field. The suffix keeps
11800	the difference [D-041][d-041] and [D-170][d-170] drew: `_types` declares typed faces, and
11801	`_connections` routes faces whose types derive from the internal endpoints.
11802	
11803	`fragment` took three letters and one word. Its fourth payload holds values
11804	for the fields of `u`, so the letter fits. `x`, `s` and `m` resolve only at a
11805	leaf, while this payload resolves at any level, assemblies included. The
11806	keyword is therefore clean only because the boundary rename makes `u` mean
11807	something on an assembly.
11808	
11809	[D-170][d-170] rejected `inner_connections` for its visual near-collision with
11810	`input_connections`. That neighbour is gone. Every entry of all three
11811	declarations names a child face, so "child" told none of them apart. "Inner"
11812	says that both ends sit inside the boundary, against one end on it. The trio
11813	is uneven in form, one word and two letters, and the form is accurate: the
11814	first is the class marker with no leaf counterpart, and the other two mirror
11815	`u_types` and `y_types`.
11816	
11817	[D-267][d-267] rejected `x_deriv` on two grounds, and neither holds. The family does
11818	not spell its words in full: `x_init`, `s_init`, `m_init` and `ws_init`
11819	abbreviate already. `x_deriv` beside `x_projection` is no more uneven than
11820	`x_init` beside it. The read side already says `get_deriv`, so the
11821	declaration and its selector now spell one word one way. It is also the
11822	declaration authors write most, once per continuous leaf. "Deriv" could be
11823	misread as "derived", a word the spec uses for contracts, but `get_deriv`
11824	had fixed its meaning before this entry.
11825	
11826	Annotation (2026-10-02): partly superseded by [D-303][d-303]. The three connection
11827	names fall, with the widening of [D-267][d-267]'s rule to an assembly's boundary
11828	declarations that produced two of them. The declarations are `inner_wires`,
11829	`input_wires` and `output_wires`, the first `local_wires` since [D-314][d-314]. The listing of `Group`'s keywords among
11830	what stays falls too, since those keywords and fields now take the
11831	declaration names and `sample_times`. `x_deriv`, the `fragment` keyword `u`
11832	and the rejection of bare `wires` as a function name stand. The keyword now
11833	rests on [§8.6][s8-6] alone, because its ground in the `u_connections` name is gone.
11834	
11835	**Rejected.**
11836	- *`sibling_connections`:* [§6.1][s6-1]'s own adjective for these wires, but a child
11837	  wired to itself is expressible (`"plant/power" => "plant/u"`), and a child
11838	  is not its own sibling.
11839	- *`wires`:* the spec's everyday noun and `Group`'s field, but it drops the
11840	  `_connections` suffix that ties the three declarations, and a package
11841	  function of that name would take it from every `wires` local and from the
11842	  `Group` field under `implementation.md`'s naming rules.
11843	- *Keeping `child_connections` beside `u_connections` and `y_connections`:*
11844	  the name discriminates nothing, since all three declarations connect child
11845	  faces.
11846	- *`x_proj`:* `proj` is neither frequent nor established. Few leaves write a
11847	  projection, tab completion spares the writer, and a reader who meets the
11848	  name rarely is better served by the full word.
11849	- *`u` and `y` as `Group`'s keywords:* they hold connection entries, not
11850	  values, and `u = ("in" => "c/e",)` would read as a value of `u`.
11851	- *Letters on the helpers and selectors:* [D-267][d-267] separates declarations from
11852	  these, and [D-076][d-076]'s separation of `u_types` from `input_faces` stands.
