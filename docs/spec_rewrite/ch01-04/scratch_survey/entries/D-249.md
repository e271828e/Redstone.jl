9485	### D-249 — Settle three payload columns: contract arity, runtime time and face routes
9486	
9487	**Status.** ratified
9488	
9489	**Position.** Three [Appendix C][sC] columns move to what the design needs, and
9490	every other payload shortfall `pending.md` enumerates is a fill from data its
9491	site holds.
9492	
9493	- `TierSignatureMismatch` owns all three signature violations [§8.5][s8-5] names, the
9494	  arity arms on a stateful leaf included. `DeclarationOnWrongTier` keeps the
9495	  update laws, the stores, `state_events`, `init_m`, `state_projection` and
9496	  the `init_workspace` arity.
9497	- A runtime `ConformanceFailure` carries no simulation time of its own. The
9498	  `StepError` carrier's frame holds the boundary time and the replay index,
9499	  and that satisfies [§9.5][s9-5]. A handler's occurrence names its event, at the
9500	  probe and at run time alike.
9501	- `FaceNameCollision` carries the assembly path and the colliding names, with
9502	  no per-entry declaration.
9503	- `StopFaceInvalid`'s binding site is one of three, the constructor, `run!`
9504	  or `replay!`, since a replay binds the run policy as `run!` does ([§12.7][s12-7]).
9505	
9506	**Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.5][s9-5], [§12.7][s12-7], [§13.5][s13-5], [Appendix C][sC]
9507	
9508	**Rationale.** [D-216][d-216] kept the payload columns as the design and left the
9509	prototype's gaps visible under them. Closing those gaps found three columns
9510	the sites argue against. [§8.5][s8-5] said two things about a contract arity on a
9511	stateful leaf. Its tier paragraph listed a `state_update` beside a
9512	two-argument `output_types` under `DeclarationOnWrongTier`, and its
9513	signature-shape paragraph made any of three signature violations
9514	`TierSignatureMismatch`. One violation family split across two kinds by
9515	whether the leaf holds a store would make an author match on the leaf's
9516	statefulness to catch a wrong contract form. The contract's own kind takes
9517	all three arms, and its payload already spells the form found against the
9518	form mandated. Simulation time on a runtime `ConformanceFailure` would be a
9519	second copy of a value the catch site stamps on every carrier, and filling it
9520	means passing a clock into every generated store write, whose only context
9521	today is the path and the function. The carrier's time and boundary index
9522	are what the replay idiom reads. Naming the declaration behind a colliding face
9523	name would say which of two declarations the author wrote by hand, when a
9524	computed entry collides only under an empty or a coinciding prefix, the message
9525	already names the assembly and the faces, and the fix is an `except` entry or a
9526	prefix either way. Carrying it would make the passthrough helpers return
9527	a marked pair, every reader of the two boundary declarations unwrap it, and
9528	the root arm carry a tag with nothing to say, a primitive root having no
9529	computed entries. The `replay!` site is a factual completion: [§12.7][s12-7] binds
9530	`stop_on` there exactly as at `run!`.
9531	
9532	Annotation (2026-09-24): `TierSignatureMismatch` retires with the contract
9533	arities, and the `init_workspace` arity leaves `DeclarationOnWrongTier`'s
9534	member set; a `Pinned` entry on a discrete leaf joins it ([D-263][d-263]).
9535	
9536	**Rejected.**
9537	- *Keeping the contract arity under `DeclarationOnWrongTier` on a stateful
9538	  leaf:* the same wrong form reports as two kinds by the leaf's statefulness,
9539	  and the contract kind's form-found-versus-form-mandated payload goes unused
9540	  on the arity arms.
9541	- *Threading the clock into the generated writes:* duplicates the carrier's
9542	  field, and every store write grows an argument for a value read from one
9543	  place.
9544	- *Marking computed passthrough entries:* a public helper's return shape
9545	  changes, and the root arm's declaration is meaningless, for one word in one
9546	  message.
9547	- *Leaving the three columns as written:* `pending.md` would carry three
9548	  permanent gaps that no increment could close without one of the mechanisms
9549	  above.
