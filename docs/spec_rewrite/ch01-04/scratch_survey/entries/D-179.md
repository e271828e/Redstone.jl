6534	### D-179 — Derive detection policy from the guard's return type
6535	
6536	**Status.** ratified
6537	
6538	**Position.** Detection policy is declared by the guard's return type — `Bool`
6539	⇒ boundary-detected, nominal scalar ⇒ localized — and `Event(guard, handler)`
6540	loses the `localize` keyword: de-localization = casting the guard to its
6541	predicate (`σ ≥ 0`, the [§2.1][s2-1] definition, semantics-preserving by construction).
6542	
6543	**Spec.** [§2.1][s2-1], [§10.4][s10-4], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.3][s9-3], [§9.5][s9-5]
6544	
6545	**Rationale.** The localized-`Bool` combination becomes unrepresentable and
6546	`LocalizedGuardForm` retires, `GuardForm` remaining the sole guard diagnostic;
6547	recorded doctrine: guards over `u`/`m` alone are piecewise frame-constant so
6548	boundary detection is *exact* for them, and the gate idiom `(gate) ? σ :
6549	-one(σ)` is the blessed way to localize a mixed predicate (gates frame-constant
6550	through probes).
6551	
6552	**Rejected.**
6553	- *The `localize` keyword, the prior design:* redundant with the probed form —
6554	  the [§9.3][s9-3] probe already observes it; every policy flip touches two sites,
6555	  manufacturing the desync mistake the build check then polices.
6556	- *Two nominal `Event` types (`LocalizedEvent`/`NonLocalizedEvent`):* the best
6557	  spelling of declared-intent-plus-check, but isomorphic to the flag — restores
6558	  the two-axis design, the resurrected build error and two-site flips; kept as
6559	  a guarded addition should habit-driven accidental localization prove a real
6560	  nuisance in migration.
6561	- *Per-relation-atom crossing decomposition à la Modelica:* requires owning the
6562	  AST — excluded by [§8.1][s8-1]'s no-macros/no-introspection foundations.
