12959	### D-308 — Keep integers, enums and Bools in modes, and the workspace out of snapshots and replay
12960	
12961	**Status.** ratified
12962	
12963	**Position.** Two rulings on where data lives, which [§7.1][s7-1] and [§7.3][s7-3] state, are
12964	fixed here.
12965	
12966	- A continuous component's `Int`s, enums and `Bool`s belong in its modes.
12967	- A workspace is not snapshotted and not replayed.
12968	
12969	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.3][s7-3]
12970	
12971	**Rationale.** Continuous state takes only real scalars and `SArray`s
12972	([D-094][d-094]), so a discrete-valued field of a continuous component has no leaf
12973	there. The mode store admits it, since its fields are isbits or a `Symbol`
12974	([D-231][d-231]), and the structure step's `IllegalStateLeaf` message sends such a
12975	field to `m_init` ([§8.2][s8-2]). A workspace carries no information between calls
12976	([D-183][d-183]), so a snapshot or a replay has nothing in it to keep. [D-306][d-306] already
12977	keeps it out of conditions.
12978	
12979	**Rejected.** None recorded.
