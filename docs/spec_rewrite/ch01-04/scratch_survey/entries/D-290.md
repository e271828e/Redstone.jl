12343	### D-290 — Localization's exact detection, left-end discriminator, endpoint and `t*` boundary
12344	
12345	**Status.** ratified
12346	
12347	**Position.** Four localization rulings that [§10.4][s10-4] states are fixed here.
12348	
12349	- A guard over `u` and `m` alone is piecewise constant within a frame, so
12350	  boundary detection is exact for it. The gate idiom
12351	  `(gate) ? σ : -one(σ)` is the endorsed way to localize a mixed predicate.
12352	- Only `u` can differ between the prior's context and the θ = 0 trial
12353	  evaluation, so the frame-top drain is the sole source of disagreement.
12354	  σ₀ not-holding marks a trajectory-caused edge, which pays for ẋₙ₊₁ and the
12355	  interpolant and root-finds. σ₀ holding marks an epoch-caused edge. Its
12356	  localization is discarded, and the event fires in the boundary's ordinary
12357	  iteration at the cost of one sweep, with no localization budget spent and
12358	  no warning.
12359	- The root-finder returns the holding endpoint of its final bracket. `t* =
12360	  tₙ` is therefore structurally impossible, and the guard observably holds
12361	  at `t*`. `t* = tₙ₊₁` degenerates to the grid boundary, with one snapshot.
12362	  Grid times are indexed, never accumulated, and the remainder step targets
12363	  the grid point.
12364	- At `t*` the full [§10.6][s10-6] iteration runs, a snapshot publishes, the [§12.3][s12-3]
12365	  boundary counter increments and `stop_on` is checked. Ticks are never due
12366	  at `t*`, staged inputs are not drained, and the publication is not
12367	  separately paced. The trace stays frame-indexed.
12368	
12369	**Spec.** [§10.4][s10-4]
12370	
12371	**Rationale.** Each bullet restates what an existing entry recorded outside
12372	its Position. Exact detection for a guard over `u` and `m`, and the gate
12373	idiom, were recorded in [D-179][d-179]'s Rationale. The θ = 0 discriminator and the
12374	discarded epoch-caused edge were recorded in [D-182][d-182]'s Rationale. The holding
12375	endpoint, the `t* = tₙ₊₁` degeneracy and indexed grid times were recorded in
12376	[D-082][d-082]'s Rationale. What runs at a `t*` boundary was recorded in [D-081][d-081]'s
12377	Rationale, less its once-per-event firing, which [D-181][d-181] replaced.
12378	
12379	**Rejected.** None beyond the source entries' lists.

12381	Annotation (2026-10-07): the gate form also serves a predicate that must
12382	exclude equality, gated on the strict comparison of a wired signal,
12383	`u < 0 ? -u : -one(u)`. The gate is then not constant over the bracket, and
12384	the root-finder pays bisection's count on that event. [§10.4][s10-4] states the idiom;
12385	`companions/limited_integrator_variants.md` records the case that needed it
12386	and why the `σ ≥ 0` convention stands.
