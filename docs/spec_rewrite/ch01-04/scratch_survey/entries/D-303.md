12774	### D-303 — Name the connection declarations for wires, and `Group`'s keywords for the declarations
12775	
12776	**Status.** ratified
12777	
12778	**Position.** An assembly's three connection declarations are named for the
12779	wires they return, and `Group`'s keywords and fields take the names of the
12780	declarations they feed.
12781	
12782	- The renames: `inner_connections` → `inner_wires`, `u_connections` →
12783	  `input_wires`, `y_connections` → `output_wires`. Signatures, entry
12784	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
12785	- `Group`'s keywords and fields: `wires` → `inner_wires`, `inputs` →
12786	  `input_wires`, `outputs` → `output_wires`, `rates` → `sample_times`.
12787	- In prose, "wire" covers an entry of any of the three declarations. A
12788	  sentence that means the child-to-child kind alone says "inner wire".
12789	- `fragment`'s `u` keyword stays, and it stands on [§8.6][s8-6] alone. The root's
12790	  input faces come from the boundary declaration or from `u_types`,
12791	  following the root's class, and nothing downstream tells the two apart.
12792	  [D-279][d-279] grounded the keyword on the `u_connections` name, and that ground is
12793	  replaced.
12794	- What stays: `u_types` and `y_types`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
12795	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, `x_deriv` and every diagnostic
12796	  kind name.
12797	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
12798	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.
12799	
12800	**Spec.** [§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.8][s8-8], [§9.1][s9-1], [§11.3][s11-3],
12801	[§11.7][s11-7], [§13.1][s13-1], [§13.3][s13-3], [§13.5][s13-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.1][s14-1], [§14.2][s14-2], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC],
12802	[Appendix D][sD]
12803	
12804	**Rationale.** An assembly writes the trio on adjacent lines, and no
12805	component writes both `u_types` and `u_connections`. Parallel names inside
12806	the trio therefore serve the author more than [D-279][d-279]'s pairing across the two
12807	classes. "Wire" is the spec's everyday noun, and the names are shorter. Each
12808	boundary declaration now shares its prefix with the helper splatted into it,
12809	`input_wires` with `input_passthrough` and `output_wires` with
12810	`output_passthrough`.
12811	
12812	`Group`'s docstring says its declarations are textually identical to a named
12813	assembly's. With keywords equal to declaration names, an author learns one
12814	vocabulary. And `inputs = ("in" => "c/e",)` no longer reads as a list of
12815	inputs, the objection [D-279][d-279] itself raised against `u` and `y` as keywords.
12816	
12817	The `inner_`/`input_` visual near-collision that [D-170][d-170] rejected returns. It
12818	is a reading nuisance only, because an entry written under the wrong
12819	declaration fails at build. Three `Group` builds checked this against the
12820	tree on 2026-10-02. One put an input entry under the inner declaration, one
12821	an inner entry under the input declaration, and one an output entry under
12822	the inner declaration. Each raised a `DiagnosticError`.
12823	
12824	Bare `wires` stays free for locals, so [D-279][d-279]'s ground for rejecting `wires`
12825	as a function name does not apply to the prefixed names.
12826	
12827	**Rejected.**
12828	- *`inner_conns`/`input_conns`/`output_conns`:* a coined abbreviation on the
12829	  authoring surface, where a plain word costs the same characters.
12830	- *`wires_in`/`wires_out`/`wires_local`:* noun-first order breaks the
12831	  pairing with `input_passthrough` and `output_faces`. `wires_in` also reads
12832	  as "the wires in this assembly", and `local` names a scope beside two
12833	  directions.
12834	- *`input_routing`/`output_routing`/`inner_routing`:* the boundary pair is no
12835	  shorter than the names it replaces, and "routing" names an arrangement,
12836	  not the pairs returned ([D-301][d-301]).
12837	- *Short `Group` keywords (`wires`, `inputs`, `outputs`, `rates`) beside the
12838	  declaration names:* two vocabularies for one thing.
12839	
12840	Annotation (2026-10-06): [D-314][d-314] renames `inner_wires` to `local_wires`;
12841	the near-collision this entry accepted as a nuisance is removed rather than
12842	tolerated. The boundary names, `Group`'s keywords, the "wire" convention
12843	and the rest stand.
