6216	### D-172 — Rule "face" kind-blind, defined at first use
6217	
6218	**Status.** ratified
6219	
6220	**Position.** "Face" is ruled kind-blind and defined at first use ([§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3],
6221	[§8.2][s8-2] fixed; the term was never adjudicated — presupposed by [D-033][d-033]/[D-041][d-041] — this
6222	row records it as deliberate): a component's *ports* are its signal endpoints
6223	(one cell, one producer — [§4.3][s4-3]'s periphery atom); its *faces* are the names
6224	those ports wear on its boundary — coinciding for a leaf, aliasing interior
6225	ports for an assembly ([D-170][d-170]'s declarations), never creating an endpoint; N
6226	faces across nesting levels may alias one port; wiring and the periphery
6227	address a child's faces without knowing whether it is primitive or composite
6228	([§6.1][s6-1]'s generic seam requires the kind-blindness).
6229	
6230	**Spec.** [§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§11.2][s11-2], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.6][s8-6]
6231	
6232	**Rationale.** Wording fixes: [§3.3][s3-3] "exported ports" → boundary faces; [§8.2][s8-2] "a
6233	primitive root has no faces" → no root slots (its faces are its own port
6234	names).
6235	
6236	**Rejected.**
6237	- *Unifying on "port"/"assembly ports":* kind-qualified vocabulary cannot state
6238	  [§6.1][s6-1]'s generic-seam rule; calling aliases ports falsifies [§4.3][s4-3]'s
6239	  one-port-one-cell atomicity and forces "port alias" repair vocabulary; "port
6240	  name vs port path" loses the two-notation rule's lexical anchor, [§8.6][s8-6]/[§11.2][s11-2].
6241	- *Replacement terms surveyed and rejected:* `gate` (flow-control connotation
6242	  plus fatal collision with multi-rate gating), `pin` (collides with pinned
6243	  cells, [D-166][d-166]–[D-168][d-168], and is the endpoint, not the name), `terminal`
6244	  (`resolve_terminal`; endpoint again), `handle` (`binding(handle)`),
6245	  `alias`/`label` (mechanism-true but obligation-weak — faces carry claims and
6246	  unconnected-input errors), `socket`/`portal` (passage metaphors); "face" is
6247	  the morphological root of [§8.3][s8-3]'s own "interface".
