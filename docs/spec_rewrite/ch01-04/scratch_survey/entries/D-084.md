2573	### D-084 — Drop the unconnected-output warning
2574	
2575	**Status.** ratified
2576	
2577	**Position.** No unconnected-output warning: under mandatory `output_types`
2578	models carry many observation-oriented ports no wire consumes ([§11.2][s11-2] blesses
2579	exactly that), and [D-083][d-083]'s path-bound readers attach post-build, so "unused" is
2580	undecidable at build.
2581	
2582	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§11.2][s11-2], [§11.7][s11-7], [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix C][sC]
2583	
2584	**Rationale.** Such a warning fires on every honest port and poisons the sole
2585	warning stream ([§11.7][s11-7]'s anti-diagnostic lesson); the hazard it would guard (a
2586	wire someone meant to draw) is the consumer side's unconnected-*input* error,
2587	where the information actually lives; the warning stream survives, empty.
2588	
2589	**Rejected.**
2590	- *Superseded position — warning on unconnected outputs (earlier design of
2591	  record):* a stream of false positives trains users to skip it.
2592	- *Narrowing it:* no principled subset exists — "should have been wired" is not
2593	  decidable from structure, and every candidate predicate still hits honest
2594	  diagnostic ports.
