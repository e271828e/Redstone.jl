10417	### D-263 — One arity on both tiers: plain contracts, the `Pinned` marker and the mandatory store
10418	
10419	**Status.** ratified
10420	
10421	**Position.** Every contract declaration takes the component alone on both
10422	tiers, is written at nominal `Float64`, and is retyped by the framework. The
10423	tier is never spelled in a signature.
10424	
10425	- On the continuous tier `input_types` and `output_types` are **walked**: the
10426	  leaf walk that types `init_x` and the root inputs is applied to the declared
10427	  types, so every `Float64` position follows the activation scalar. A leaf
10428	  wrapped as `Pinned{P}` is pinned at every activation, and the wrapper is
10429	  stripped at nominal. On the discrete tier the declarations pin wholesale,
10430	  as before, and a `Pinned` entry there is `DeclarationOnWrongTier`.
10431	- The permissive reading of `input_types` ([D-167][d-167]) stands with the marker as
10432	  its spelling: an unpinned position is tolerant, a `Pinned` one demands a
10433	  frozen arrival. Both wire clauses of [§6.1][s6-1] stand, decided at the marker
10434	  scalar by retyping the declarations instead of calling them.
10435	- The type walk substitutes `Float64` in type-parameter positions, yields `P`
10436	  at `Pinned{P}`, and does not enter a mutable type's parameters. A handle's
10437	  data references are fields and never walk; a handle with a scalar parameter
10438	  walks like any type, and `Pinned` freezes one built from build-time data.
10439	- `init_workspace(::C, ::Type{T})` keeps its scalar and takes it on **both**
10440	  tiers; the framework passes `Float64` to a discrete allocator at every
10441	  activation.
10442	- Every leaf declares exactly one of `init_x` and `init_s`, and a stateless
10443	  leaf declares it empty, `init_x(::C) = (;)` or `init_s(::C) = (;)`. The
10444	  store is the tier marker, mandatory even when empty, as `child_connections`
10445	  is the class marker ([§8.5][s8-5]). `StoreWithoutUpdate` applies to a non-empty
10446	  store only.
10447	- `TierSignatureMismatch` retires with all three arms. `TierUnreadable` names
10448	  a primitive declaring neither store, and an empty-store leaf with no
10449	  `output_types` is `StatelessWithoutOutputs`.
10450	
10451	**Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§7.2][s7-2], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.1][s9-1],
10452	[§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix A][sA], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
10453	
10454	**Rationale.** The two-argument form charged every continuous leaf for a
10455	choice almost none of them make: the suite carries 11 pinned leaves against
10456	192 `T`-form declarations, and a flight library's components promote by
10457	default because trim and linearization sweep whole assemblies. [D-166][d-166]'s own
10458	criterion says a `T` belongs in a signature exactly where the declaration's
10459	non-nominal behavior is underdetermined by its nominal restriction. With a
10460	leaf-level marker that underdetermination is gone: the plain declaration,
10461	`Pinned` included, fixes the declaration at every scalar, so the signature's
10462	`T` would record nothing the page does not already say. The retype walk is
10463	not new machinery. It is `retype`, the walk `init_x`, conditions and the
10464	root-input cells already go through; the contracts were the one declaration
10465	kind it did not reach, and carrying two conventions cost more than carrying
10466	one.
10467	
10468	[D-166][d-166] reversed [D-079][d-079] on two grounds. Deliberate pinning of a real leaf was
10469	inexpressible under the plain form, and the plain form was reader-opaque,
10470	misleading to anyone not carrying the walk rule in their head. The marker is
10471	the element neither entry had on the table, and it answers the first ground
10472	outright: the pin is on the page, per leaf, schema-visible and
10473	conformance-checked, so [§14.10][s14-10]'s freeze door and the FFI door survive as
10474	declared doors. The second ground is revalued rather than answered. The walk
10475	rule is one rule, already taught for the state side; a reader who knows that
10476	`Float64` follows the scalar on the continuous tier reads every declaration
10477	in the framework with it.
10478	
10479	The forgotten-`T` classes vanish rather than move. A `Float64` written at a
10480	participating leaf now walks, which is what the author meant. The class that
10481	takes its place, a forgotten pin on an FFI leaf, fails where the forgotten
10482	`T` did and never silently: an input pin is a wire check at the first
10483	nominal build only when written, and an output built from frozen data is
10484	refused at the first `Dual` activation by the identity comparison on the
10485	opaque leaf or by the `MethodError` inside the user's math, because no lossy
10486	`Dual → Float64` cast exists. The embedding guarantee ([§9.5][s9-5]) is untouched.
10487	
10488	A stateless leaf loses its only tier signal with the arity, and the store
10489	supplies it. [§3.1][s3-1]'s continuous component has a state vector that may be
10490	empty, so a stateless continuous leaf is honestly spelled as a continuous
10491	leaf with zero state fields, `init_x(::C) = (;)`, and a sampled map as
10492	`init_s(::C) = (;)`. The empty NamedTuple is already the vocabulary's "no
10493	state": the bundle law puts a store's letter in the bundle only when the
10494	store is non-empty, so nothing downstream changes. Making the declaration
10495	mandatory is the move [§8.5][s8-5] already makes for `child_connections`,
10496	mandatory even when empty because defining it is the class marker; the store
10497	is the tier marker on the same terms. Every leaf's tier is then on the page
10498	in one place, stateful or not, with no tier by omission. The alternative
10499	that was on the table, an `is_discrete(::C)` trait defaulting to `false`,
10500	would have added a family name and a default, and it would have widened
10501	[D-178][d-178]'s recorded gap: an optional declaration lost to a local scope or a
10502	forgotten import silently drops its feature, so a sampler whose
10503	`is_discrete = true` was lost would silently become a transparent
10504	continuous wire, where a lost `init_s(::C) = (;)` fails loud as a leaf
10505	declaring no store. [D-173][d-173]'s and [D-178][d-178]'s rejections of the trait therefore
10506	stand. The cost is one line on every stateless leaf, the same line an
10507	assembly author already writes for `child_connections`.
10508	
10509	The workspace keeps its scalar because its declaration is by allocation, not
10510	by type. Retyping a type is a pure substitution and total over the
10511	vocabulary; retyping an allocated value is undefined for a factorization, a
10512	plan or a buffer whose size depends on the scalar, which is [D-077][d-077]'s standing
10513	rejection of `similar`-based re-scalaring. The scalar is consumed by the
10514	allocation, so it is not ceremony in the contract sense. Taking it on both
10515	tiers removes the last arity-shaped tier fact: the discrete tier never runs
10516	off-nominal, so `Float64` is the only scalar its allocator can honestly
10517	receive; the ceremony falls on a rare declaration; and [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data
10518	door would bring the scalar there anyway.
10519	
10520	Opaque leaves need no stop of their own. The type walk substitutes
10521	parameters and never looks at fields, so a bulk-data handle whose references
10522	are fields passes through untouched, which is [§4.4][s4-4]'s frozen build-time data
10523	by construction. A handle with a scalar parameter is the case [D-237][d-237] admits, a
10524	different cell type per activation built at `T` by the stage; it walks like
10525	any type, and `Pinned` spells [D-237][d-237]'s other remedy. A mutable type's
10526	parameters pin by rule because no stage can produce a `Vector{Dual}` inside a
10527	handle without copying the grid at every evaluation, so no choice exists
10528	there for a marker to record.
10529	
10530	Annotation (2026-09-25): the marker is read at the top of an entry alone, and
10531	one below it is `IllegalPortType` on both tiers ([D-265][d-265]); a frozen opaque leaf
10532	is admitted at a tolerant entry ([D-264][d-264]); the marker's meaning is "carries no
10533	partials", and an AD-opaque implementation that must participate supplies a
10534	local rule under a tolerant entry ([D-266][d-266]).
10535	
10536	**Rejected.**
10537	- *Superseded position — the mandated two-argument forms on the continuous
10538	  tier ([D-166][d-166], [D-167][d-167]):* ceremony on every continuous leaf for a per-leaf
10539	  choice made on few; reader honesty bought at the price of a second typing
10540	  convention beside the state side's walk; the whole-signature and per-leaf
10541	  forgotten-`T` classes it had to contain exist only because the form exists.
10542	- *A one-argument `init_workspace` with framework re-scalaring:* [D-077][d-077]'s
10543	  grounds stand; reconstruction cannot cover plans, factorizations or
10544	  scalar-dependent structure.
10545	- *Keeping `init_workspace` tier-split:* one arity would stay a tier fact,
10546	  with its vote, its `DeclarationOnWrongTier` arm and a paragraph explaining
10547	  why this declaration alone still splits.
10548	- *A scalar-only marker (`PinnedFloat64`):* cannot pin an `SVector{3,Float64}`
10549	  or a handle whole; the parametric marker pins any leaf type.
10550	- *A parameter-position pin (`MyStruct{Float64, Pinned{Float64}}`):* fails a
10551	  `<: Real` bound unless `Pinned <: Real`, a lie in the type lattice; the
10552	  leaf-level pin covers every known case, and the limit is recorded.
10553	- *Stopping the type walk at every opaque leaf:* removes the handle-with-a-
10554	  scalar-parameter case [D-237][d-237] admits, and re-imports the inexpressibility
10555	  [D-166][d-166] charged [D-079][d-079] with.
10556	- *An `is_discrete(::C)` trait, default `false`:* a new family name and a
10557	  tier by omission; an optional declaration a local scope or a forgotten
10558	  import drops silently, turning a sampler into a transparent wire.
10559	- *Reading a stateless leaf's tier from its parent's `sample_times`:* a
10560	  block that is discrete by design, a `ZOH` or a sampler, could not say so,
10561	  and a user who forgets the entry gets a continuous wire with no ground for
10562	  the build to complain; substituting a stateless map into an unlisted slot
10563	  would silently flip its tier; the tier is the leaf's own fact ([D-056][d-056]).
10564	- *`nothing` as the empty store:* a sentinel return, the class [D-178][d-178] refuses
10565	  and `StoreNotNamedTuple` ([D-247][d-247]) already rejects; the empty NamedTuple
10566	  says the same thing inside the vocabulary.
10567	- *A stateless leaf with no outputs as a legal no-op:* admits a component
10568	  that produces nothing and stores nothing.
10569	- *Accepting `Pinned` silently on a discrete leaf:* a spelling that says
10570	  nothing is the class the declaration layer refuses.
