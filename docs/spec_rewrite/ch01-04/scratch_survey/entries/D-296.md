12545	### D-296 — Abstract entries, the record-and-check doctrine, the tight root bound and the tap's pinning consumer
12546	
12547	**Status.** ratified
12548	
12549	**Position.** Four input-contract rulings that [§8.2][s8-2] states are fixed here.
12550	
12551	- Abstract entries state structural substitutability, as [§4.4][s4-4]'s field
12552	  handles use them. They are never needed for eltype genericity, because an
12553	  eltype-generic producer's nominal face is concrete by construction.
12554	- Declarations record choices, and obligations are checked.
12555	- Only a tight, concrete bound determines a root input's type, since a root
12556	  input has no producer to take its cell type from.
12557	- A `B`-matrix tap selecting a root input that a consumer's entry pins is
12558	  rejected at tap resolution, naming the pinning consumer and its entry, not
12559	  the face alone. The author's next move, promoting that leaf or routing the
12560	  tap around it, depends on knowing which leaf froze the root input.
12561	
12562	**Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§14.10][s14-10]
12563	
12564	**Rationale.** Each bullet restates what an existing entry recorded outside
12565	its Position. Abstract entries, the record-and-check doctrine and the tight
12566	root bound were recorded in [D-078][d-078]'s Rationale. The tap's pinning consumer was
12567	recorded in [D-168][d-168]'s Rationale.
12568	
12569	**Rejected.** None beyond the source entries' lists.
