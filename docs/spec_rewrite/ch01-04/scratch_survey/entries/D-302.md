12746	### D-302 — Let a store be the home of any state letter
12747	
12748	**Status.** ratified
12749	
12750	**Position.** A store is the framework-owned home of one of a component's
12751	state letters, `x`, `s` or `m`. The buffer is the concrete layout of
12752	continuous state, the flat `x` vector and the `ẋ` vectors the integrator
12753	fills, so an `x` store is a range of the buffer. Stores are not cells
12754	([D-121][d-121]).
12755	
12756	**Spec.** [§4.1][s4-1], [§7.3][s7-3], [Appendix D][sD]
12757	
12758	**Rationale.** [§4.1][s4-1] and [§7.3][s7-3] reserved the word for the `s` and `m` registers,
12759	reading [D-121][d-121]'s "`z`/`m` live in *stores*" as exclusive. The log used it for
12760	`x` throughout: [D-035][d-035]'s views of "the stores it genuinely reads", [D-197][d-197]'s
12761	"`init_x` stores", [D-247][d-247]'s single store declaration form for `x_init`,
12762	`s_init` and `m_init`, and [D-263][d-263]'s "mandatory store". So did over a hundred
12763	spec sentences, the diagnostic kinds `StoreWithoutUpdate` and
12764	`StoreNotNamedTuple`, and the store selectors `get_state` and `get_deriv`.
12765	This entry adopts the usage the log and the surface follow. The rules that
12766	hold for the `s` and `m` stores alone, isbits fields, no arithmetic and copy
12767	by bits, say so where they are stated.
12768	
12769	**Rejected.**
12770	- *Reserve "store" for `s` and `m`, as [§4.1][s4-1] and [§7.3][s7-3] stated it:* over a
12771	  hundred spec rewrites, and renames of two diagnostic kinds and the store
12772	  selectors in `src/`, for a distinction the buffer already carries.
