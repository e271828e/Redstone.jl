14103	### D-343 — The remedy ladder's content, the closed loop remedies and the trace's reach
14104	
14105	**Status.** ratified
14106	
14107	**Position.** Nine loop rulings that [§5.4][s5-4], [§5.5][s5-5] and [§5.6][s5-6] state are fixed
14108	here.
14109	
14110	- An evaluation unit coarser than the stage admits artificial loops. A
14111	  component-atomic unit makes false loops everywhere, and an atomic assembly
14112	  makes them at assembly scale.
14113	- An input the neighbor consumes only in a fallback branch is the archetypal
14114	  false dependency. Contract re-factoring moves the branch to its natural
14115	  owner, and the wire disappears.
14116	- A re-factoring records structure when it survives substitution.
14117	- The component split's cost is stated where it bites. Every intermediate
14118	  shared across the new boundary becomes a declared port, capped at one
14119	  struct-valued bundle port.
14120	- The cycle diagnostic's hint offers both exits, split this component or
14121	  narrow the neighbor's contract, for continuous members only.
14122	- The component split is rare because the first two rungs absorb the common
14123	  shapes.
14124	- Implicit delays and per-step numerical loop solving are closed.
14125	- The classifier's trace speaks only for the branch taken at the probe
14126	  point, so the author selects the remedy. The framework neither splits nor
14127	  narrows on its own.
14128	- A continuous face or port declared without a walking leaf traces
14129	  structurally, as a discrete member does.
14130
