12845	### D-304 — The flat declaration, the unchanged buffer, the store-field limits and authored genericity
12846	
12847	**Status.** ratified
12848	
12849	**Position.** Six state and genericity rulings that [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.2][s7-2] and [§7.3][s7-3] state
12850	are fixed here.
12851	
12852	- The `x_init` declaration is flat. Each field is one leaf, because the
12853	  condition algebra and the readers address a field as one leaf, and
12854	  structure is the component tree's to express. The check that enforces the
12855	  closed vocabulary refuses nesting with the rest.
12856	- Hoisting the views rebuilt per call is the code generator's CSE, whose
12857	  legality condition is the staleness rule: the buffer is unchanged within a
12858	  sweep.
12859	- A struct nesting a `Symbol` does not qualify as a field of an `s` or `m`
12860	  store. The check is one exact predicate per field and does not
12861	  recurse.
12862	- No arithmetic is done on the `s` and `m` stores, a rule that holds for
12863	  those stores alone.
12864	- Type stability under `Dual` is an authoring rule, not a conformance
12865	  predicate.
12866	- Differentiation participation is never inferred by a probe. Probe
12867	  inference would invert the rule that declarations define and probes check,
12868	  and one probe point cannot speak for branch-dependent participation.
12869	
12870	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.2][s7-2], [§7.3][s7-3]
