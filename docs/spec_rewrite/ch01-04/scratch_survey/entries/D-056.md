1678	### D-056 — Uphold the two-kind taxonomy against the integrate-and-dump challenge
1679	
1680	**Status.** ratified
1681	
1682	**Position.** The two-kind taxonomy is upheld under the integrate-and-dump
1683	challenge ([§3.4][s3-4]): the kinds are time bases, sweep-driven vs. tick-driven, and
1684	cross-tier coupling always routes through table cells.
1685	
1686	- The idiom is integrate-and-difference: cumulative integrals in `x`,
1687	  previous-sample latch in the sampler's `z`.
1688	- It is exact whenever interval-dependence is a left action by the
1689	  interval-start value of a cumulatively-integrable quantity (inertial-rate
1690	  anchoring required; RK-exact by linearity of the kinematics).
1691	- A latch-back wire — a feedthrough-stage ZOH latch — carries interval-relative
1692	  flow terms.
1693	- Tick-triggered continuous handlers are the recorded, unbuilt escape hatch.
1694	- Boundary-sampling semantics are promoted to taught contract.
1695	
1696	**Spec.** [§3.2][s3-2], [§3.3][s3-3], [§3.4][s3-4], [§8.6][s8-6]
1697	
1698	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
1699	
1700	**Rejected.**
1701	- *All-in-one component kind:* an assembly in a trench coat: the halves still
1702	  communicate through cells — one home per datum, sole-reader `z`, no
1703	  cross-tier state views — so zero expressiveness gained; costs stage doubling
1704	  with tier-tagged names, per-port tier vocabulary, facet-conditional
1705	  obligations; hides the sampling seam — Simulink/FMI's documented sample-time
1706	  confusion.
1707	- *`z` view in `f` / discrete writes into `x`:* un-samples the sampled-data
1708	  semantics, breaks held-`z` linearization exactness, coupling invisible to
1709	  table, trace and feedthrough graph.
1710	- *Periodic reset via time-guard events:* hand-rolls the tick scheduler,
1711	  forfeits the harmonic grid and the discrete-bundle `Δt`.
