12884	### D-305 — The zero-allocation invariant's scope, its grounds and its budgets
12885	
12886	**Status.** ratified
12887	
12888	**Position.** Three allocation rulings that [§7.5][s7-5] states are fixed here.
12889	
12890	- The zero-allocation invariant is scoped to the stepping loop. The
12891	  stopped-sim services were always allocation-tolerant.
12892	- The policy is scoped, not blanket dogma, which fights logging reality. A
12893	  policy is kept, because without one the type-instability canary is lost.
12894	- Guards and `x_projection`, which run unconditionally per boundary or frame,
12895	  take the exactly-zero budget. Handlers, which are episodic and run only on
12896	  firing, take the ticks' zero-by-idiom budget.
12897	
12898	**Spec.** [§7.5][s7-5]
12899	
12900	**Rationale.** Each bullet restates what an existing entry recorded outside a
12901	Position. The invariant's scope and the services' tolerance were recorded in
12902	[D-135][d-135]'s Rationale. Blanket dogma and the lost canary were recorded in [D-014][d-014]'s
12903	Rejected list. The budgets' membership was recorded in [D-116][d-116]'s Rationale,
12904	which calls the budgets tiers and the projection `project`.
12905	
12906	**Rejected.** None beyond the source entries' lists.
