711	### D-019 — Harmonic tick grid with virtual assemblies and rate scopes
712	
713	**Status.** ratified
714	
715	**Position.** Harmonic tick grid on step boundaries; discrete stages gated to
716	own tick instants (ZOH by construction); assemblies virtual for execution, rate
717	scopes for declaration (integer multipliers $K \ge 1$ composing down the tree,
718	compiled to absolute divisors); `Δt` arrives as a discrete-bundle field
719	([D-074][d-074]), single source of truth, no stored `Δt`-derived parameters.
720	
721	**Spec.** [§5.2][s5-2], [§10.5][s10-5]
722	
723	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
724	
725	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-186][d-186] and [D-283][d-283]. Phases are
726	adopted, reversing the phase-offset rejection below, and rates compile to
727	anchor-relative triples whose divisors deployment binds.
728	
729	**Rejected.**
730	- *Superseded position — a `comp.Δt` virtual property (the earlier design of
731	  record):* impossible, because `===`-identical siblings can sit under
732	  different `rates` keys, so the period is a schedule-position fact, not a
733	  component fact ([D-074][d-074]).
734	- *Atomic assemblies, incl. opt-in:* coarsened schedulable unit → [§5.4][s5-4]
735	  artificial loops at assembly scale; interleaving protection meaningless under
736	  the signal table; FlightCore's whole-tree atomicity was a call-tree artifact.
737	  The atomic-assembly census behind that rejection: Simulink documents the
738	  artificial-loop hazard for its Atomic Subsystems, and the construct's other
739	  roles there — code-generation units, enabled/triggered execution — have no
740	  counterpart in the consumers, conditional behavior ("on ground, force
741	  `direct`") being mode logic.
742	- *Arbitrary tick periods via time queue:* variable `h`, irregular frames, no
743	  demonstrated need.
744	- *Absolute-period declaration as default:* welds deployment rates into
745	  reusable designs; base-period variables don't compose across independently
746	  authored assemblies.
747	- *Re-running discrete stages every boundary:* un-samples sampled-data
748	  semantics.
749	- *Phase offsets:* no demonstrated use.
750	- *`Δt` via `h`-argument only:* discretized laws live in the feedthrough stage.
