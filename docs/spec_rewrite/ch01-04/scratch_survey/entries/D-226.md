8472	### D-226 — Reach the public surface by qualified name until the export audit
8473	
8474	**Status.** ratified
8475	
8476	**Position.** A framework name is public by standing on [Appendix B][sB]'s surface,
8477	reached by qualified name or per-name `import`. Julia's `export` list is
8478	decided once, by the full-surface audit in `pending.md`, and until then the module exports
8479	nothing.
8480	
8481	- "Exported" keeps two senses in the spec: an assembly face raised to its
8482	  boundary, and the exported-name audit. A function or name on the API surface is
8483	  "public". The rename sweeps the spec and its companions, not the log.
8484	- The interim is conformance, not a gap: the suite reaches every name through
8485	  `using Redstone` plus a per-name `import` list, [D-117][d-117]'s idiom, and a REPL
8486	  session loads the same list through `test/repl.jl`.
8487	- The authoring family stays unexported under [D-117][d-117] whatever the audit
8488	  decides for the operator half.
8489	
8490	**Spec.** [§4.4][s4-4], [§8.1][s8-1], [§9.4][s9-4], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix D][sD]
8491	
8492	**Rationale.** The 2026-09-04 conformance audit read "exported" at three sites
8493	as an obligation the module does not meet, `names(Redstone)` being
8494	`[:Redstone]`. The sites describe visibility. [§4.4][s4-4]'s value-level constructor is
8495	a function a model package must expose so condition math can call it outside
8496	any sweep; [§9.4][s9-4]'s `ProbeDual` is a name a CI activation list spells. Neither
8497	needs `export`; both need a public name. `pending.md` already records the export list
8498	as open, to be decided by a full-surface audit under the four-class
8499	convention, and names what the operator half carries: `condition`,
8500	`fragment`, `at` and `combine` share a namespace with FlightPhysics domain
8501	code, and whether the condition algebra ships behind a submodule is the
8502	packaging question. Exporting ahead of that audit would claim names it may
8503	rename or move. `step!` is exported by SciMLBase, `combine` by DataFrames and
8504	`mode` by StatsBase, and Julia settles such a clash at the first unqualified
8505	use, which is the by-accident outcome the audit exists to prevent. Nobody is
8506	inconvenienced meanwhile: the package has no external user, and the
8507	developer's session pays one `-L` flag.
8508	
8509	**Rejected.**
8510	- *Export the operator surface now:* decides the list by accident ahead of
8511	  the audit `pending.md` records, and turns a later rename or submodule move into a
8512	  breaking change.
8513	- *Rule that nothing is ever exported:* forecloses the audit.
8514	- *Read "exported" as Julia's `export` and register the code as deviant:*
8515	  creates an obligation the design itself defers.
8516	- *A re-export submodule for the operator verbs:* the packaging `pending.md` lists as
8517	  open, half-decided.
