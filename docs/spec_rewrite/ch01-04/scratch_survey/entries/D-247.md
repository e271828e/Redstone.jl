9369	### D-247 — Accept the `NamedTuple` as the only store declaration form
9370	
9371	**Status.** ratified
9372	
9373	**Position.** Every by-value store declaration, `init_x`, `init_s` and
9374	`init_m`, returns a `NamedTuple` with one named field per leaf, and no other
9375	form is admitted. Stratum A checks the form on every primitive before the
9376	tier classifier and the vocabulary checks read the value, and reports a bare
9377	value as `StoreNotNamedTuple`, collected, carrying the store, the observed
9378	type and the wrap that fixes it.
9379	
9380	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.1][s9-1], [Appendix C][sC]
9381	
9382	**Rationale.** Nothing checked the form, and every reader of a store value
9383	assumes a `NamedTuple`: the tier classifier, the two Stratum A field checks,
9384	`Decls`, the leaf walk and the bundle. Probed on Julia 1.13.0 with leaves
9385	sound otherwise, a `Float64` or an `SVector` from `init_x` and an `Int` from
9386	`init_s` failed as a raw `MethodError` converting to `NamedTuple` in Stratum
9387	C; a `Tuple` from `init_x` failed inside the generated `reconstruct` and one
9388	from `init_s` as a `BoundsError`; a `Vector` from `init_x` segfaulted in the
9389	generated `reconstruct`; a `Symbol` from `init_m` failed as
9390	`isempty(::Symbol)` in the classifier; and an `Int` from `init_m` built
9391	silently as the mode store. The field checks read `pairs(1.0)` without
9392	complaint, so the first refusal came from a stratum that runs user code, or
9393	from the compiler.
9394	
9395	The bare leaf was weighed as an ergonomic form for one-state components and
9396	rejected on where the field name does work. Every service reaches a leaf by
9397	its field name: the condition overlay is a `NamedTuple` merge keyed on it
9398	([§14.1][s14-1]), readers and the trace header spell `plant/x.q` ([§11.5][s11-5], [§14.4][s14-4]), the
9399	trim problem's decisions and residuals name it ([§14.7][s14-7]), auto-publication
9400	publishes a state field as the port of the same name ([§5.3][s5-3]), the return laws
9401	pair by it ([§9.5][s9-5]), and the payloads of `IllegalStateLeaf` and
9402	`ConformanceFailure` carry it. A bare leaf would be a second store shape at
9403	each of those seams and would forfeit auto-publication, having no name to
9404	match. The name a one-state component's author is asked for is the name every
9405	service then uses, the discipline `output_types` already asks of ports. The
9406	leaf walk is not the obstacle: an `SArray` lays out wherever it sits.
9407	
9408	The check collects under [§13.1][s13-1]'s rule for declarative checks and runs first
9409	in the walk's primitive branch, since the classifier and the field checks
9410	cannot read the value. A primitive that fails it records `nothing` for its
9411	tier and is read no further in Stratum A. An empty `(;)` is the fallback's
9412	own value and keeps reading as "declares nothing".
9413	
9414	Annotation (2026-09-25): the empty store `(;)` is a `NamedTuple` and admitted; it
9415	is the stateless leaf's mandatory tier marker ([D-263][d-263]).
9416	
9417	**Rejected.**
9418	- *A single leaf as a second store form, addressed as the store itself:* a
9419	  second shape at every name-keyed seam (conditions, readers, trace, trim,
9420	  auto-publication, the return laws, the payloads), no auto-publication for
9421	  it, and no grounding model that wants it, the case studies carrying several
9422	  leaves per component.
9423	- *Canonicalizing a bare value into a one-field `NamedTuple` under a synthetic
9424	  name:* wrapped both ways, the author writes `x.value` and gains nothing;
9425	  unwrapped into the bundle and rewrapped at the returns, the bijection is
9426	  owed at every seam and the synthetic name still surfaces in conditions,
9427	  readers, traces and messages.
9428	- *A `:return_type` arm on `ConformanceFailure`:* a declaration-reading
9429	  failure filed under [§9.5][s9-5]'s probed, fail-fast return law.
9430	- *A whole-value arm on `IllegalStateLeaf` or `IllegalStoreField`:* two
9431	  per-field contracts bent, and the fix the message states differs in kind.
