12999	### D-310 — Park the built-in GUI, run every device spawned, and withdraw `gui = true`
13000	
13001	**Status.** ratified
13002	
13003	**Position.** The built-in Julia GUI is parked in favour of a browser
13004	cockpit over the bridge, and `run!` has one task topology.
13005	
13006	- The loop runs on the calling task in every run, and every rostered
13007	  device runs on a spawned task. `needs_calling_task`, its admission check
13008	  `CallerTaskConflict` and the inline-body branch of `run!` are withdrawn.
13009	- `gui = true` is withdrawn. A front end is attached by hand, once per
13010	  simulation, and the roster keeps it across that simulation's runs.
13011	- [§11.7][s11-7]'s semantics stand unchanged, derived liveness, the peek, staging
13012	  on interaction and the orphan label, and the framework's half of the
13013	  panel convention ([D-270][d-270]) is what the bridge serves. The GUI
13014	  package's half becomes the browser client's, and the panel method returns
13015	  a description rather than drawing. That amendment is ruled when the
13016	  cockpit's design reaches it.
13017	- The handle carries no pause ([D-268][d-268]). Whether it gains the control verbs
13018	  for the bridge is ruled with the bridge's design.
13019	
13020	**Spec.** [§11.1][s11-1], [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.6][s11-6], [§11.7][s11-7], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.2][s12-2], [§12.4][s12-4], [§12.6][s12-6], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC]
13021	
13022	**Rationale.** The browser cockpit was the endgame from the start, and it
13023	subsumes the built-in GUI for every use the design serves. It shows a
13024	snapshot one display frame later, about 15 to 35 ms from publication to
13025	pixel against 8 to 20, which a cockpit does not notice and a control loop
13026	through a widget would, a use the design never served. It lifts the
13027	main-thread constraint, the OpenGL dependency Apple has deprecated and
13028	CImGui.jl's churn. It works headless and remote, which the built-in GUI
13029	never could. Panel authoring by language models favours the browser's
13030	corpus and its verification loop, the type checker, tests over fixture
13031	descriptors and a headless screenshot, over a thin Julia wrapper with GL
13032	state under Revise. And the static inspector, the live inspector and the
13033	cockpit become one client over one protocol, so the external interface
13034	gets its reference client as a by-product.
13035	
13036	The attended mode existed for CImGui's main-thread rule alone ([D-109][d-109]), and
13037	the flag's run-scoping existed because a window's lifetime was the run's
13038	([D-175][d-175]). A browser tab's lifetime is the session's, so one explicit
13039	`attach!` per simulation is the right spelling, and `inspect(sim)` the
13040	sugar if any. Deleting the attended mode leaves `run!` with one shape to
13041	specify and test. [D-109][d-109]'s identity and admission halves survive in [§11.3][s11-3]:
13042	identity by instance, the stable id at attach, `AlreadyAttached` and
13043	`ClaimConflict`. Its affinity third and its topology rationale fall. [D-175][d-175]
13044	falls whole. The parked design, its source and its tests last stand
13045	together at commit b6f08b1, and the GUI record's built-in half was folded
13046	into the inspector's record when this entry was written.
13047	
13048	**Rejected.**
13049	- *Keeping `needs_calling_task` as harmless machinery:* two run topologies
13050	  to specify, test and reason about, with no device holding the trait. A
13051	  native-window device parks with the GUI it served, and the code is one
13052	  revert away.
13053	- *Building the built-in GUI as a warm-up:* the one step whose output the
13054	  endgame does not reuse. The bridge finds the handle's gaps as well,
13055	  and it is the first thing the cockpit needs.
13056	- *Retargeting `gui = true` to the bridge:* the flag's semantics are
13057	  a window's, attach at entry and detach in the tail. A tab outlives runs.
