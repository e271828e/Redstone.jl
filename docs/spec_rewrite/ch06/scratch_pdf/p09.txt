struct Junction{In, Out, N, F} :: AbstractComponent   # library-provided (§13.7)
    f::F                              # the fold, N-ary; instance data
end
const SumJunction{V, N} = Junction{V, V, N, typeof(+)}
x_init(::Junction) = (;)              # stateless, continuous: the empty store is the tier 
marker (§8.2)
u_types(::Junction{In, Out, N}) where {In, Out, N} =
    NamedTuple{ntuple(i :> Symbol(:in, i), N)}(ntuple(_ :> In, N))
y_types(::Junction{In, Out}) where {In, Out} = (out = Out,)
y_direct(j::Junction, (; u)) = (out = j.f(u::.),)
The parameters are the nominal port types and the arity  ( D-311), as in 
SumJunction{Wrench{Float64}, 3}, and the fold is instance data. Wrench is FlightPhysics' force-and-
torque type, here parametrized by its scalar type as a walked type of §7.2. SumJunction{V, N} is the 
Junction at +. A custom fold, such as a weighted blend or a mass-properties composition, is written 
at the site as Junction{In, Out, N}(f), which infers F.
The junction is a continuous leaf, so its contracts (a component's declared interface) are written at 
nominal Float64. On a continuous leaf the leaf walk (the framework's derivation of per-activation 
types) retypes them ( §8.2). An input entry at Wrench{Float64} is the tolerant form a promoting 
consumer writes. Walking, frozen and root-input contributors are all admissible behind it. A root 
input is the root component's own input face.
The Bool gates of §13.7 are the same leaf at | and & (D-311). The junctions themselves, summing 
junctions and Bool gates, are the seed of the standard component library committed in §13.7 (D-062). 
They are ordinary components with no framework privileges. The inventory grows by judgement 
against the three guidelines §13.7 states (D-313).
Wired at an ownership boundary, inside the assembly that owns the contributors, the junction is 
ordinary structure. An assembly is a component of pure composition. In the block below, Systems 
is the systems assembly. Its children include the aerodynamics aero, the powerplant pwp and the 
landing gear ldg, and a child dynamics that consumes the total. wr_sum::SumJunction{Wrench{Float64}, 
3} is a field of Systems like any other child (§8.5).
local_wires(::Systems) = (

What the explicit form buys
• Every bookkeeping mistake at the junction is loud under the declaration layer. A contributor 
the junction counts but nobody wires is an unconnected-input error naming in4. A double-wired 
slot violates single-connection. A stale arity surfaces as one or the other. The bookkeeping is 
ceremony, never silence. A contributor's output that nobody wires stays legal and silent, as any 
unconnected output does (D-084).
• The aggregate is a first-class signal. "wr_sum/out" is an ordinary port. It is loggable, GUI-visible, 
and fanned out to a second consumer (a component monitoring structural loads) for one wire.
