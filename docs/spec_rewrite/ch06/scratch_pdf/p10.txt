• Aggregation logic is arbitrary stage-2 code, such as mass-properties composition with its trans -
port terms, or weighted blends. It is not restricted to a declared commutative-associative binary 
op.
• Fold order is author ­visible (D-037). It is the positional order of the junction's inputs. Reas -
signing contributors to different slots changes summation order, hence bits, because float addition 
is not associative. The ordering is deterministic per configuration and under author control, which 
is strictly more explicit than a framework-canonical order.
• For the handful of real sites, a named site-specific junction documents the contributor set 
better than generated slots, at the price of hard-coding it into a type. An example is 
u_types(::VehicleWrenchSum) = (aero = …, ldg = …, pwp = …). The generic positional form remains 
the tool for configuration-variable sites. Both are plain components, and the framework is not 
involved.
The hierarchical aggregation idiom
Each assembly that owns contributors aggregates them with an internal junction and 
exports the total  (D-037). The junction is a component inside the assembly, and the assembly 
exports its out port (§8.6).
The §6.1 connection rules force this shape. A child is opaque to wiring at every boundary, so every 
assembly that owns contributors must export its aggregate.
For example, the landing gear Ldg sums its three legs and exports wrench. The systems assembly 
sums aero + ldg + pwp. The vehicle, the assembly one level above the systems assembly, wires the 
systems totals into the rigid-body equations of motion. The block above places the dynamics child 
inside Systems instead, and both shapes are legal.
Junctions placed this way replace FlightPhysics' tree walk. Each of its recursion steps becomes one 
visible junction at the level that owns the contributors. For Flight.jl's Cessna 172 model that is about 
four junctions and fifteen wires, written once. They read as a manifest of what weighs, what pushes 
and what spins. Only physical contributors publish the ports a junction sums. A strut publishes 
wrench, and avionics publishes nothing.
Reference-frame responsibility is as in Flight.jl. Contributors publish in the common body frame, 
applying their own mounting transforms at source.
Do not bundle wrench, mass properties and internal angular momentum into one contribution 
struct. Contributors are ragged, each having only some of the three quantities. A bundled contri -
bution struct forces zero-filled identity noise through every port, which is the "silently sum nothing" 
hazard in a new coat (D-037).
A sum_ports!-style helper would instantiate, wire and export in one call. Such a helper is guarded­
addition sugar (a capability the design admits but does not build), added when real models show 
the pattern repeated (D-037).
