Two consequences explain the rule. The first is that faces become the only currency crossing an 
assembly boundary, so substitutability holds at every boundary rather than only at generic ones. This 
is the contract-is-the-interface principle of §8.3 with its one leak closed. The second consequence is 
that the face graph is total (D-207). Every signal crossing a boundary bears a declared face there, at 
every level it crosses. Totality is what the condition algebra addresses against. An at prefix stopping 
at a child's face has a name to resolve through (§14.2).
The ceremony the rule costs is the re-export entry per level. It is the ceremony the design already 
pays almost everywhere, because every level of a realistic tree is a generic seam (a narrow, named 
interface kept deliberately thin). One-level routing was mandatory at a generic seam in any case 
(§8.8). Where the entries multiply, they are computed rather than typed, through input_passthrough/
output_passthrough over a single authored feed list (§8.8). Parallel routes threading many boundaries 
are the signal to gather them into a component of their own (D-207).
Enforcement lives in the path-resolution primitive itself, resolve (§13.3, D-130). It walks declared 
field types alongside instances ( D-061). An endpoint reaching past the immediate child is a 
PathResolution error (Appendix C). Paths are validated at build time, and renames break loudly.
Type­checking a wire
Once both endpoints of a wire resolve, two clauses type-check it ( §8.2). The first is the nominal 
bound check. Nominal means the Float64 activation (the build's typed products at a given scalar 
type), and a declaration's Float64 face. The bound check is stated over declaration evaluations. 
The producer's declaration at Float64 must be :: the consumer's input entry at Float64 (D-078). 
It is one uniform rule, and it degenerates to exact equality for a concrete entry. A violation is 
WireTypeMismatch.
Beside it stands the walk-compatibility clause (the walk clause for short), for a continuous consumer 
only. A walking producer leaf, one the producer left unpinned, requires an unpinned entry. A pinned 
producer leaf, one declared Pinned, satisfies either entry, because frozen values embed upward under 
any activation. A discrete consumer takes the bound check alone, so a continuous producer feeding 
a discrete consumer is unconditionally legal, for the reason §8.2 gives (D-295, D-263).
The producer's declaration and the entry are plain declarations that the leaf walk (the framework's 
derivation of per-activation types) retypes. The walk clause is therefore decided in the structure step 
by retyping them at a marker scalar. That is declaration reading, and no user stage code runs (§9.1).
An abstract entry is an input entry whose declared type is abstract. Its leaves cannot be enumerated. 
The walk clause decides an abstract entry on the whole declaration (D-236). The producer's 
declaration at the marker, or the same with every pinned leaf lifted to the marker, must be :: the 
entry at the marker (D-236).
The walk clause admits an opaque leaf on the same terms as a pinned or walking leaf. An opaque 
leaf (§4.3), a handle stored whole, has no scalar position to embed through, and the walk clause 
does not need one. A frozen handle arriving at an unpinned entry is the producer's cell (the 
signal-table entry holding its output), gathered as stored ( D-264). The consumer's own arithmetic
