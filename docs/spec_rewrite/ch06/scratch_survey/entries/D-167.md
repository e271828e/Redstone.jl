5932	### D-167 — Mandate `Type{T}` input signatures under the permissive reading
5933	
5934	**Status.** superseded → [D-263][d-263]
5935	
5936	**Position.** `input_types(::C, ::Type{T}) where {T <: Real}` is **mandated on
5937	continuous consumers**, plain on discrete consumers, under the **permissive
5938	reading**: entries state per leaf what the consumer *allows* — `T` = tolerant
5939	(the activation scalar or a frozen `Float64` are both lawful arrivals; walking
5940	producers, frozen discrete producers and root slots all admissible —
5941	substitution intact), `Float64` = **demands frozen** (this input must never
5942	carry partials — the FFI door: a component whose internals cannot propagate
5943	`Dual`s declares it, its AD-incompatibility becomes schema-visible, and the
5944	failure moves from a `MethodError` inside user math at the `Dual` probe to a
5945	named wiring error at build), `Int`/`Bool` and abstract reference-typed entries
5946	(field handles) as today.
5947	
5948	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§13][s13], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix D][sD]
5949	
5950	**Rationale.** Entries remain face bounds, not cell types: the nominal bound
5951	check is now "the producer's declaration evaluated at `Float64` <: the entry
5952	evaluated at `Float64`", unchanged in force.
5953	
5954	Wiring acquires a second clause beside it, **evaluable in Stratum A at a marker
5955	scalar** (both sides are declaration functions of `T`; no user stage code
5956	runs): *for a continuous consumer, a walking producer leaf requires a `T`
5957	entry; a pinned producer leaf satisfies either* (frozen values embed upward),
5958	violation being `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` (consumer path + entry, producer
5959	path + face, the leaf, both declared leaf types, both remedies in the message;
5960	[§13][s13]).
5961	
5962	**The clause is tier-scoped, and the scope is decisive**: discrete
5963	consumers take the nominal bound check only, because their stages read
5964	exclusively at real ticks in the nominal world — a `Dual`-carrying cell exists
5965	only inside activations discrete stages never run in — so continuous → discrete
5966	wires are unconditionally legal; unscoped, the clause would reject the entire
5967	sensor → controller pattern.
5968	
5969	Failure asymmetry, recorded: input-side forgotten-`T` (habitual `Float64` on a
5970	promoting input) fails **at first nominal build, at the wire, both endpoints
5971	named** — inputs have a build-time counterparty, outputs don't — with the
5972	didactic hint "if `C` promotes, declare the entry `T`".
5973	
5974	Root slots: the slot type remains the entry at `Float64` (concrete tight bound,
5975	unchanged); slot cells walk by evaluating the entry at the activation's `T`,
5976	making **seedability schema-visible** — a `T`-entry slot is a lawful
5977	linearization `B`-matrix tap, a `Float64`-entry slot is declaredly unseedable.
5978	
5979	The genericity obligation ("whatever scalars the wiring delivers, the
5980	consumer's math promotes") stays checked by the `Dual` probe, now scoped to the
5981	`T`-entries — a `Float64`-entry input imposes no such obligation, that being
5982	its point.
5983	
5984	Escape from [D-033][d-033]'s rejection, recorded: the permissive reading predicts
5985	nothing and is not constant across components (pinned entries are rare but
5986	real), which is the reading the original adjudication never had on the table.
5987	
5988	Annotation (2026-09-24): superseded by [D-263][d-263]. The mandate goes; the
5989	permissive reading, the walk-compatibility clause, its tier scope and the
5990	root-slot rules all stand, with `Pinned{P}` as the spelling of "demands
5991	frozen" and an unpinned `Float64` position as "tolerant".
5992	
5993	**Rejected.**
5994	- *The predictive reading ("this is what will arrive") and the envelope reading
5995	  ("I promise to promote"), [D-033][d-033] upheld:* the predictive reading remains
5996	  rejected — producer-, tier- and activation-dependent, breaks substitution
5997	  behind a face; the envelope reading remains rejected — a universal
5998	  obligation, zero information.
5999	- *Symmetric `T`-forms on the discrete tier:* both directions
6000	  unfalsifiable-or-false: no execution path puts a `Dual` in a discrete cell,
6001	  and discrete reads never see one; if [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data door opens, the
6002	  opt-in participation trait brings the `T`-signature with it — the hinge is
6003	  recorded here so the forms stay compatible.
6004	- *The unscoped wiring clause:* rejects every continuous → discrete wire;
6005	  caught in adjudication, recorded so the bug is never reconstructed as
6006	  intended semantics.
