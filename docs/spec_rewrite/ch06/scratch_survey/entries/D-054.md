1614	### D-054 — Producers determine activation types; consumers stay generic
1615	
1616	**Status.** ratified
1617	
1618	**Position.** Producers determine activation types, consumers accept — consumer
1619	obligation is genericity, checked by the `Dual` probe.
1620	
1621	**Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2]
1622	
1623	**Rationale.** Symmetric `T` on `input_types` is rejected as
1624	impossible-by-construction: an input's activation-time type depends on the
1625	producer's tier through the wiring (continuous → `Dual`, gated discrete → held
1626	`Float64`; consumers promote). The envelope reading (per-leaf genericity
1627	marking) is also rejected — zero information ([D-078][d-078]).
1628	
1629	**Rejected.**
1630	- *`input_types(::C, ::Type{T})`:* forces the consumer to declare its
1631	  producer's tier; breaks on discrete-for-continuous substitution behind the
1632	  same face; [D-033][d-033]'s exact-`Float64`-face check was already the only coherent
1633	  consumer statement.
