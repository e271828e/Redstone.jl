7691	### D-211 — Let a component declare one container name-transparent
7692	
7693	**Status.** ratified
7694	
7695	**Position.** A component type may declare at most one of its container
7696	fields **name-transparent** — the opt-in declaration
7697	`transparent_container(::MyType) = :field` beside the connection
7698	declarations, default `nothing` — and that field's elements are then
7699	contributed as children under their bare keys: `"key"`/`"1"` in place of
7700	`"field/key"`/`"field/1"`, everywhere a child name appears — wiring
7701	endpoints, `sample_times` keys, read paths, `at` prefixes, diagnostics. A
7702	bare key colliding with any sibling child name is a build error naming both.
7703	`Group` declares its `children` field transparent, so its values address
7704	their children directly — `"ctl/out" => "plant/u"`, `(ctl = Relative(2),)`,
7705	`at("ctl", …)` — amending [D-085][d-085]'s naming clause and [D-184][d-184]'s
7706	zero-new-declaration-rules framing; undeclared containers keep their key
7707	segment exactly as before, [D-207][d-207]'s one-level reading unchanged.
7708	
7709	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.7][s8-7]
7710	
7711	**Rationale.** [§8.5][s8-5] already rules containers "transparent grouping, not
7712	assemblies" — transparent in structure and contract, yet opaque in naming:
7713	the field segment rides every child name. The segment earns its keep where
7714	the parent mixes child sources or the roster name informs (`"aircraft/2"`);
7715	it carries nothing where the container is the type's sole child source, and
7716	`Group` is the extreme case — every child of every `Group` is
7717	`children/<key>`, boilerplate on all three authoring surfaces at once:
7718	wiring endpoints, rate keys (a slash-bearing key forcing the
7719	`var"children/ctl"` spelling), and the whole read/address side
7720	(`state(sim, "children/f/children/a")`, `at("children/ctl", …)`, every error
7721	message). Under [D-207][d-207] the composite name also became grammatically
7722	anomalous: a `Group` endpoint spells three segments where the rule reads
7723	immediate child + face, indistinguishable on sight from the deep paths just
7724	removed. With bare keys, a `Group`'s declarations are textually identical to
7725	a named assembly's — the anonymous-beside-named promise made literal. The
7726	declaration is opt-in with a collision check rather than a naming change,
7727	keeping the informative segment where parametric rosters and mounting rely
7728	on it; one transparent container per type keeps a bare name's declaration
7729	evident, and nothing wants two.
7730	
7731	**Rejected.**
7732	- *Qualification inside `Group`'s accessors — the connection declarations
7733	  prepending `children/` to what the author wrote:* zero framework change,
7734	  but it cleans only the declaration surface and splits the namespace — the
7735	  author declares `"ctl/out"` yet reads `"children/ctl/out"`, and build
7736	  errors quote strings never written. One consistent composite name beats two
7737	  inconsistent ones.
7738	- *Dropping the field segment universally:* breaks the informative naming
7739	  parametric rosters and mounting rely on (`"aircraft/2"`), and invites
7740	  collisions in multi-container types with no opt-out.
7741	- *Renaming `Group`'s field to something short (`c::C`):* shortens the
7742	  boilerplate without removing it from any surface.
7743	- *Sanctioning child discovery itself as an overloadable declaration:*
7744	  strictly more power than the defect calls for — naming is the flaw;
7745	  discovery stays structural.
