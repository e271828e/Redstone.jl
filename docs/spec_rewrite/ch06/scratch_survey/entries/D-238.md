8957	### D-238 — Decide embed-accept on the type: lift the arrival, compare exactly
8958	
8959	**Status.** ratified
8960	
8961	**Position.** A type `V` is accepted at a declaration `P` at activation `T`
8962	when lifting `V`'s `Float64` positions to `T` exactly where `P` has `T`
8963	yields `P` itself, compared by identity. [D-235][d-235]'s always-on check and the
8964	concrete arm of [D-236][d-236]'s wire relation both take this form.
8965	
8966	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5]
8967	
8968	**Rationale.** [D-166][d-166] states embed-accept per leaf: a declared-`T` leaf
8969	accepts the scalar or a `Float64`, every other leaf is exact. The built
8970	relation checked that statement on the leaves — a struct's name and field
8971	types, a static array's size and eltype — and was exact on the leaves while
8972	blind to the type around them: a `NamedTuple`'s field names, a non-numeric
8973	type parameter and an array's mutability are type parameters, not leaves, so
8974	`(b = T,)` passed at a `(a = T,)` entry, `Tagged{:b}` at `Tagged{:a}` and an
8975	`MVector` at an `SVector`, each surfacing later as a raw `FieldError` or
8976	running silently (increment 32's review). A parallel walk over the two types'
8977	parameters, lifting a `Float64` in `V` where `P` has `T` and leaving every
8978	other position as written, is [D-166][d-166]'s statement decided on the type: [§9.5][s9-5]'s
8979	exact match at nominal is the case where nothing lifts and the relation is
8980	`===`, and the embedding is the one asymmetry. The lift is positional on the
8981	type, so it also reaches a `T` that carries no scalar, such as a tag parameter
8982	(`Tag{T}` with no field of type `T`): the store holds leaves and rebuilds the
8983	declaration from them, so `Tag{Float64}` arriving at `Tag{D8}` is normalized
8984	to the declaration rather than converted, the pinned-into-walking direction
8985	[D-236][d-236] already admits, while `Tag{D8}` at `Tag{Float64}` stays refused. A
8986	parameterless type compares by identity, so [D-237][d-237]'s opaque leaves need no arm
8987	of their own.
8988	
8989	**Rejected.**
8990	- *Superseded position — the leafwise walk:* exact on leaves, blind to the
8991	  parameters that distinguish two concrete types with the same leaves.
8992	- *`<:` with embedding on concrete declarations:* concrete types are final, so
8993	  `<:` is identity there and adds nothing; the abstract case is [D-236][d-236]'s.
8994	- *Acceptance by `convert`:* admits every lossy or renaming conversion Julia
8995	  defines, which is the convert-on-write [D-053][d-053] rejects.
