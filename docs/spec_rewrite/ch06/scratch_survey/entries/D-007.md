447	**Status.** superseded → [D-037][d-037]
448	
449	**Position.** Aggregation is by explicit summing junctions ([D-037][d-037]).

451	**Spec.** [§6.2][s6-2], [§8][s8]

453	**Rationale.** See [D-037][d-037] for the surviving decision.

455	**Rejected.**
456	- *Superseded position — reduce-ports with a canonical fold (the earlier design
457	  of record):* reversed because it was the declaration vocabulary's last
458	  wrapper, a three-site census (all Newton–Euler, one library file) could not
459	  justify canonical-fold, multi-connection legality and identity-element
460	  machinery, and the aggregate wasn't even observable.
461	- *Σ-junctions as default:* arity/positional ceremony — objection dissolved by
462	  [§8][s8]'s loud declarations.
463	- *Contribution buses:* invisible dataflow — verdict unchanged; they also
464	  import scoping rules and admit accidental contributions — a component
465	  contributes by being in scope rather than by being wired.
