6173	### D-172 — Rule "face" kind-blind, defined at first use
6174	
6175	**Status.** ratified
6176	
6177	**Position.** "Face" is ruled kind-blind and defined at first use ([§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3],
6178	[§8.2][s8-2] fixed; the term was never adjudicated — presupposed by [D-033][d-033]/[D-041][d-041] — this
6179	row records it as deliberate): a component's *ports* are its signal endpoints
6180	(one cell, one producer — [§4.3][s4-3]'s periphery atom); its *faces* are the names
6181	those ports wear on its boundary — coinciding for a leaf, aliasing interior
6182	ports for an assembly ([D-170][d-170]'s declarations), never creating an endpoint; N
6183	faces across nesting levels may alias one port; wiring and the periphery
6184	address a child's faces without knowing whether it is primitive or composite
6185	([§6.1][s6-1]'s generic seam requires the kind-blindness).
6186	
6187	**Spec.** [§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§11.2][s11-2], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.6][s8-6]
6188	
6189	**Rationale.** Wording fixes: [§3.3][s3-3] "exported ports" → boundary faces; [§8.2][s8-2] "a
6190	primitive root has no faces" → no root slots (its faces are its own port
6191	names).
6192	
6193	**Rejected.**
6194	- *Unifying on "port"/"assembly ports":* kind-qualified vocabulary cannot state
6195	  [§6.1][s6-1]'s generic-seam rule; calling aliases ports falsifies [§4.3][s4-3]'s
6196	  one-port-one-cell atomicity and forces "port alias" repair vocabulary; "port
6197	  name vs port path" loses the two-notation rule's lexical anchor, [§8.6][s8-6]/[§11.2][s11-2].
6198	- *Replacement terms surveyed and rejected:* `gate` (flow-control connotation
6199	  plus fatal collision with multi-rate gating), `pin` (collides with pinned
6200	  cells, [D-166][d-166]–[D-168][d-168], and is the endpoint, not the name), `terminal`
6201	  (`resolve_terminal`; endpoint again), `handle` (`binding(handle)`),
6202	  `alias`/`label` (mechanism-true but obligation-weak — faces carry claims and
6203	  unconnected-input errors), `socket`/`portal` (passage metaphors); "face" is
6204	  the morphological root of [§8.3][s8-3]'s own "interface".
