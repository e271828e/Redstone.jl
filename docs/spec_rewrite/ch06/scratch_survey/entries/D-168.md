6008	### D-168 — Root-slot fan-out tolerance combines by meet, not agreement
6009	
6010	**Status.** ratified
6011	
6012	**Position.** Root-slot fan-out under permissive entries combines **tolerance
6013	by a meet, not by agreement**: the slot *type* stays the unique concrete
6014	declaration among its consumers at nominal (`RootSlotTypeConflict` unchanged —
6015	a tolerance difference is not a type conflict, `SVector{3,T}` and
6016	`SVector{3,Float64}` both evaluating to `SVector{3,Float64}`), while the slot's
6017	cells **pin at every activation if any consumer entry pins** and follow the
6018	activation scalar only when every consumer tolerates.
6019	
6020	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§11.3][s11-3], [§8.2][s8-2], [§14.10][s14-10]
6021	
6022	**Rationale.** Mixed tolerance is a legitimate configuration rather than a
6023	mistake — one command consumed by a promoting aerodynamics leaf and by an
6024	AD-opaque table is [§8.2][s8-2]'s FFI door in use — and the meet is the only
6025	assignment satisfying every consumer at once, forced by the direction of
6026	embedding: a pinned cell feeds a `T` entry lawfully (zero-partial embedding,
6027	[§9.5][s9-5]) while a `Dual` arriving at a `Float64` entry is exactly what that entry
6028	forbids; it is the mirror of [§6.1][s6-1]'s producer-side walk-compatibility clause.
6029	
6030	The cost is stated where it is paid: such a slot is declaredly unseedable, and
6031	a `B`-matrix tap selecting it is rejected at tap resolution naming the
6032	**pinning consumer** and its entry (`TapResolution` payload extended), not the
6033	face alone, because the author's next move — promote that leaf, or route the
6034	tap around it — depends on knowing which leaf froze the slot.
6035	
6036	Closes the fan-out gap left open by [D-167][d-167].
6037	
6038	Annotation (2026-09-08): read at the level of the whole root input, with the
6039	two-candidate computation, per [D-236][d-236].
6040	
6041	**Rejected.**
6042	- *Requiring agreement at the marker scalar (mixed = build error):* turns a
6043	  legitimate model into an error whose only remedies are a duplicated slot or a
6044	  false declaration, and makes one component's private AD limitation contagious
6045	  across wiring its author does not control.
6046	- *Join semantics (the slot walks if any consumer tolerates):* unsound in the
6047	  direction that matters — it delivers `Dual`s to an entry that declared it
6048	  cannot take them, the exact failure [§6.1][s6-1]'s clause exists to prevent, moved
6049	  from build time to inside user math.
6050	- *Per-tolerance-class slot cells (one cell per class):* duplicates the slot,
6051	  defeating [§11.3][s11-3]'s one-cell-per-slot staging, the trace header's single type
6052	  and `probe_value`'s single synthesis.
