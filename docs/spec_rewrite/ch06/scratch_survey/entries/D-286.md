12036	### D-286 — Hold a pinned leaf to the exact check as the schema-visible freeze
12037	
12038	**Status.** ratified
12039	
12040	**Position.** A pinned leaf is the schema-visible freeze, and the
12041	conformance check holds it to its word at every activation.
12042	
12043	- A pinned leaf, one wrapped as `Pinned` or an `Int`, `Bool` or enum leaf
12044	  that never walks, takes the nominal-style exact check at every activation.
12045	- An observed `Dual` at a pinned leaf is the misplaced-pin error, with the
12046	  didactic hint "if `F` participates in differentiation, remove its
12047	  `Pinned`".
12048	- An author who means to strip partials declares the leaf `Pinned{Float64}`
12049	  and strips inside the stage.
12050	- Stripping mid-expression at an unpinned leaf remains legal.
12051	
12052	**Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5]
12053	
12054	**Rationale.** [D-238][d-238]'s relation lifts a `Float64` position only where the
12055	declaration has `T`, so every other leaf is exact (as recorded in [D-238][d-238]),
12056	and a `Pinned` leaf is pinned at every activation ([D-263][d-263]'s first bullet).
12057	An observed `Dual` at a declared-pinned leaf has one honest cause, so the
12058	error can name the fix (as recorded in [D-166][d-166]). The hint's present wording
12059	has no prior entry; [§8.2][s8-2] and [§9.5][s9-5] state it.
12060	
12061	The marker is on the page, per leaf, schema-visible and conformance-checked,
12062	so [§14.10][s14-10]'s freeze door and the FFI door survive as declared doors (as
12063	recorded in [D-263][d-263]). Deliberate `value()` stripping is the stop-gradient, a
12064	deliberate-lie class (as recorded in [D-079][d-079]). The `T`-signature's two real
12065	losses, schema-visible participation and whole-leaf stripping detection, were
12066	both equally blind to mid-expression stripping (as recorded in [D-079][d-079]), and a
12067	local derivative rule strips
12068	mid-expression, which [§9.5][s9-5] leaves legal at an unpinned leaf (as recorded in
12069	[D-266][d-266]).
12070	
12071	**Rejected.**
12072	- *Superseded position — the mandated two-argument forms on the continuous
12073	  tier ([D-166][d-166], [D-167][d-167]):* ceremony on every continuous leaf for a per-leaf
12074	  choice made on few; reader honesty bought at the price of a second typing
12075	  convention beside the state side's walk. (As recorded in [D-263][d-263].)
12076	- *The framework stripping partials at a walking-into-pinned wire:* the
12077	  discard is a modelling decision, and an invisible one is the class [§9.5][s9-5]
12078	  calls the silent zero in the Jacobian. (As recorded in [D-266][d-266].)
