10646	### D-266 — Two doors for an AD-opaque implementation: the local rule and the `Freeze` block
10647	
10648	**Status.** ratified
10649	
10650	**Position.** `Pinned` records that a leaf carries no partials, not that an
10651	implementation cannot take them. An AD-opaque implementation has two doors
10652	and linearization keeps its one mechanism.
10653	
10654	- A component that must participate in differentiation keeps its entry
10655	  tolerant and supplies its own local derivative inside the stage,
10656	  dispatching on the `Dual` type and rebuilding the output by the chain
10657	  rule ([§14.10][s14-10]).
10658	- A walking producer feeds a pinned entry through the `Freeze{V}` library
10659	  block ([§13.7][s13-7]), a stateless continuous leaf with a tolerant input and a
10660	  `Pinned` output that strips with `ForwardDiff.value`; the wire then
10661	  declares a zero coupling in every Jacobian along that path.
10662	- Linearization has the seeded `Dual` pass alone ([§14.10][s14-10]) and no
10663	  perturbation-based fallback.
10664	
10665	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§7.2][s7-2], [§8.2][s8-2], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10]
10666	
10667	**Rationale.** A `Dual` can never become a `Float64` without discarding its
10668	partials, and [§9.5][s9-5] rules that no lossy cast exists inside the framework. The
10669	framework does not do the discard at a wire, on purpose, because dropping
10670	partials is a modelling decision the design wants on the page. The `Freeze`
10671	block is that page: at nominal `value` is the identity, so it costs one
10672	gather and one scatter; under a `Dual` activation it strips, and its output
10673	satisfies the pinned entry. A sampler is the other non-walking source, and it
10674	changes the model, holding the value between ticks and delivering it one
10675	sample late, which is right for a sampled consumer and wrong for a continuous
10676	value the consumer merely cannot differentiate through.
10677	
10678	Where the coupling through the opaque component matters to the Jacobian, no
10679	adapter fixes it, and the local rule is the answer. Every stage is a pure map
10680	from its bundle to its outputs, so any such map, however opaque inside,
10681	admits a rule of one shape: evaluate at the value, obtain a local Jacobian by
10682	whatever means the component has, a finite difference at a step its own grid
10683	suggests or an analytic slope, and chain it onto the incoming partials. The
10684	stripping happens mid-expression, which [§9.5][s9-5] leaves legal at an unpinned
10685	leaf, and the seeded pass sees an ordinary differentiable component. The
10686	derivative is finite-difference in quality on that one component and exact
10687	everywhere else.
10688	
10689	Trim keeps its derivative-free fallback ([§14.8][s14-8]) and linearization gets none,
10690	and the asymmetry is principled. Trim's fallback exists for problems with no
10691	Jacobian columns at all, the closed-loop sampled-data door where a frozen
10692	`state_update` gives the residual nothing to seed, and to keep the old
10693	algorithm reachable. Linearization has no analogous case: the discrete tier
10694	is frozen by definition and the continuous tier is always seedable.
10695	
10696	**Rejected.**
10697	- *A perturbation-based linearization backend beside the seeded pass:* adds
10698	  no reach, since the local rule covers every opaque leaf; degrades every
10699	  linearization of every model containing one opaque component, silently
10700	  once the keyword is flipped, where the local rule puts the cost on that
10701	  component alone; reintroduces the global step-size heuristics the seeded
10702	  pass retired, with a perturbation that may cross a guard where a seed
10703	  cannot and attitude states that need a manifold-aware rule the seeded pass
10704	  gets for free. The one argument for it, a legacy model with a dozen opaque
10705	  tables linearizing on day one, loses because the rule is written once per
10706	  opaque type and a model that linearizes with silently degraded couplings is
10707	  the failure class the marker exists to prevent.
10708	- *The framework stripping partials at a walking-into-pinned wire:* the
10709	  discard is a modelling decision, and an invisible one is the class [§9.5][s9-5]
10710	  calls the silent zero in the Jacobian.
10711	- *Reading a pinned entry as the only door for an AD-opaque implementation:*
10712	  conflates "cannot take `Dual`s" with "carries no partials", and makes a
10713	  coupling that matters unreachable.
