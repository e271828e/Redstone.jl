7548	### D-207 — Route every connection one level: faces are the only cross-boundary currency
7549	
7550	**Status.** ratified
7551	
7552	**Position.** Deep connection paths are removed: every endpoint in the three
7553	wiring declarations addresses an immediate child (container elements
7554	included) and one of its faces. Supersedes [D-009][d-009]. The bundled rulings:
7555	
7556	- `child_connections` wires sibling faces, `input_connections` routes each
7557	  face to immediate children's faces, `output_connections` sources each face
7558	  from an immediate child's face — the generic-seam stop becomes the
7559	  universal rule.
7560	- The face graph is therefore total — every signal crossing an assembly
7561	  boundary bears a declared face there — and the `Build` retains it, input
7562	  side beside the output side, so per-level `at` addressing of a fragment's
7563	  `inputs` payload resolves (amending [D-065][d-065]'s "deep `at` only within owned
7564	  concrete subtrees" clause: a prefix stops at faces on every child, owned
7565	  or not).
7566	- Deep structural paths survive on the read side — inspection, logging,
7567	  the face routes — untouched.
7568	
7569	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.8][s8-8], [§9.2][s9-2], [§13.3][s13-3], [§14.2][s14-2], [§14.3][s14-3]
7570	
7571	**Rationale.** The deep-path privilege made the face graph structurally
7572	partial: a root claim could cross intermediate boundaries without acquiring
7573	a face at any of them, leaving those levels blind to the signals traversing
7574	them — surfaced by the condition algebra, where
7575	`at("sub", fragment(inputs = …))` had no name to resolve against, and
7576	whether it could resolve depended on how an ancestor happened to spell a
7577	route. The privilege had meanwhile lost most of its value to the design's
7578	own growth: every level of a realistic tree is a generic seam ([§8.8][s8-8]), where
7579	one-level routing was already mandatory, and the worked assemblies route per
7580	level via `input_passthrough` and the single-authored feed list. Clarity and
7581	symmetry outrank convenience: the residual ceremony has sanctioned computed
7582	spellings ([§8.8][s8-8]), parallel routes proliferating through several boundaries
7583	are the signal to gather them into a custom type, and convenience remains
7584	recoverable a posteriori as sugar where clarity does not.
7585	
7586	**Rejected.**
7587	- *Deep paths within owned assembly types ([D-009][d-009]'s position):* the bypass
7588	  is the one place structure leaks across a boundary [§8.3][s8-3] declares closed,
7589	  and the partiality it induces in the face graph is invisible from a
7590	  fragment author's seat.
7591	- *Keeping deep paths, retaining the partial face graph:* fixes the
7592	  literal `at` refusal; leaves resolvability an accident of ancestor
7593	  spelling.
7594	- *Bundled faces as the universal ceremony mitigation:* bundling serves
7595	  component-fed signal highways; at a root input it collides with [§4.3][s4-3]'s
7596	  write-side granularity and forfeits partial scripting ([§8.8][s8-8]).
7597	
7598	Annotation (2026-10-07): the face graph the `Build` retains is the declared
7599	wiring per level, and the two-sided table is a function over it ([D-315][d-315]). The
7600	one-level rule and the totality it buys stand.
