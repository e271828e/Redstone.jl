8838	### D-236 — Type-check a wire by one relation with embedding, in Stratum A
8839	
8840	**Status.** ratified
8841	
8842	**Position.** A producer's declaration is accepted at an entry when some
8843	leafwise embedding of it, lifting any subset of its `Float64` leaves to the
8844	activation scalar, is `<:` the entry evaluated at that scalar. Both clauses of
8845	[§6.1][s6-1] are this relation, decided in Stratum A by evaluating the contract
8846	declarations at a marker scalar and collected under the stratum's barrier.
8847	- A concrete entry is checked leaf by leaf. An abstract entry is checked on the
8848	  whole declaration: the producer's declaration, or the same with every pinned
8849	  leaf lifted, must be `<:` the entry.
8850	- A root input's cells at an activation take the all-walking evaluation of the
8851	  root-input type when every consumer's entry admits it, and the root-input
8852	  type itself otherwise: [D-168][d-168]'s meet, at the level of the whole root input.
8853	- A root input whose entries are all abstract is `AbstractAtRoot`. Its concrete
8854	  entries must agree at `Float64` (`RootInputTypeConflict`); abstract
8855	  co-consumers are checked by the bound clause.
8856	
8857	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.1][s9-1], [Appendix C][sC]
8858	
8859	**Rationale.** At the nominal activation every lift is the identity and the
8860	relation is [§6.1][s6-1]'s bound check, `<:` with equality as the concrete case. Under
8861	a non-nominal activation the lifts express the walk-compatibility clause: a
8862	walking producer leaf must meet a `T` entry, and a pinned producer leaf meets
8863	either, because a frozen value embeds upward. So one relation is both clauses,
8864	and it is [D-166][d-166]'s embed-accept read from the input side. The abstract case
8865	cannot walk leaves. Its two candidates are exact whenever the entry's
8866	parameters are uniformly `T` or uniformly pinned; an abstract entry mixing the
8867	two against a producer pinning some of them is admitted by the definition but
8868	not by the check, a recorded limit with no known instance.
8869	
8870	The meet on a root input is stated for the whole root input rather than per
8871	leaf, which is [D-168][d-168]'s letter. The two candidates decide it without a leaf
8872	walk and let abstract co-consumers vote. A mixture of pins across leaves pins
8873	the whole root input, the conservative direction, which never delivers a `Dual`
8874	where an entry forbade one.
8875	
8876	Stratum A is where the check runs for two reasons. The contract declarations
8877	are functions of the scalar that construct types, so evaluating them at a
8878	marker runs no stage code. And the timing is the promise [D-167][d-167] makes: the
8879	input-side forgotten `T` fails at the first nominal build, at the wire. A check
8880	at the probe sees only what the current activation shows, so at nominal the
8881	walk clause is vacuous and the error waits for the first `Dual` activation.
8882	The marker pass evaluates `input_types` and `output_types` alone; `init_x` is
8883	by value and is walked, never evaluated.
8884	
8885	Annotation (2026-09-24): the marker pass retypes the plain declarations at
8886	the marker scalar instead of calling two-argument ones; the relation and
8887	both clauses are unchanged ([D-263][d-263]).
8888	
8889	**Rejected.**
8890	- *Equality modulo embedding, with nominal identity on struct types (the built
8891	  shape):* refuses an abstract entry's lawful concrete producer, so [§4.4][s4-4]'s
8892	  substitutability and [§8.2][s8-2]'s abstract entries have no code.
8893	- *The check at the probe, on the probed value's type:* one stratum late,
8894	  fail-fast where [§13.1][s13-1] collects, and blind to the walk clause at nominal.
8895	- *A per-leaf meet on root inputs:* legal by the clause but yields a cell type
8896	  no consumer declared, needs a leaf walk abstract co-consumers cannot join,
8897	  and goes beyond [D-168][d-168]'s wording for a case with no known instance.
