10525	### D-264 — Admit a frozen opaque leaf at a tolerant entry
10526	
10527	**Status.** ratified
10528	
10529	**Position.** The wire relation admits a pinned or discrete producer of an
10530	opaque leaf at an unpinned entry of that leaf's type, as the producer's cell.
10531	The identity rule for an opaque leaf ([D-237][d-237]) governs a store and the
10532	producer's own conformance check alone, never a wire.
10533	
10534	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5], [Appendix C][sC]
10535	
10536	**Rationale.** The refusal came from one predicate serving two questions.
10537	`_accepts` decides both the store's conformance check and the wire relation,
10538	and at a store its opaque short-circuit is essential: a producer's output
10539	cell at a `Dual` activation is a `Vector{Handle{Dual}}` when the declaration
10540	walks, and a `Handle{Float64}` cannot be placed there without the framework
10541	constructing a handle, which it refuses to do. At a wire nothing is
10542	constructed. The entry is a bound, and the consumer gathers the producer's
10543	cell as stored. Embedding at a tolerant scalar entry is a type-level
10544	admission plus Julia's promotion inside the consumer's math, and a handle
10545	behaves identically: the consumer reads `u.terrain.h0::Float64` beside `Dual`
10546	state and its arithmetic promotes. The derivative through the handle is zero
10547	along every seeded direction, and that is the true derivative, because the
10548	producer declared that no partials enter it. [§9.5][s9-5]'s argument for why a
10549	`Float64` at a walking leaf is honest applies unchanged. [D-238][d-238]'s parameter
10550	walk already gives the right answer once the short-circuit is lifted: lifting
10551	`Handle{Float64}` at the parameter position where the entry has `T` yields
10552	`Handle{Dual}`, the entry itself.
10553	
10554	The positive ground is [§8.2][s8-2]'s substitution promise, that a walking producer,
10555	a frozen discrete producer and a root input are all admissible behind a
10556	tolerant entry. For handles that promise was broken in both frozen forms. A
10557	library's landing gear declaring `terrain = TerrainField{Float64}` could not
10558	be fed by a static terrain source at all, and its author could not repair it
10559	without pinning the entry, which would then refuse a moving deck instead.
10560	The wire pass reported the direction under `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` with
10561	the endpoints reversed, calling the entry frozen and the producer walking.
10562	
10563	Two risks are specific to handles, and both fail loudly at the `Dual` probe.
10564	For numbers Julia promotes a `Float64` and a `Dual` in any expression; for a
10565	handle there is no such machinery, so a consumer signature that ties the
10566	handle's parameter to another argument's (`height(f::H{T}, p::SVector{3,T})
10567	where {T}`) fails with a `MethodError` whenever the handle is frozen and the
10568	position is not. That is the genericity obligation a tolerant entry already
10569	claims, checked by the probe as [§8.2][s8-2] says. And pass-through is refused where
10570	scalar pass-through is embedded: a consumer that re-emits a frozen handle
10571	under an unpinned output hits the store's identity rule, so a handle's pin is
10572	a property of its chain, and every component that forwards it pins that
10573	output too. No silent wrong Jacobian was found. The misplaced-pin class is
10574	covered identically: a pinned producer that builds `Handle(x.h + 1.0, z)` at
10575	a `Dual` activation produces `Handle{Dual}`, the identity check refuses it,
10576	and the pin hint fires. `companions/handle_walk_walkthrough.md` works the
10577	example through.
10578	
10579	**Rejected.**
10580	- *A named, collected refusal for the direction (a frozen face at a walking
10581	  entry), with remedies to pin the entry or build the handle at `T`:* breaks
10582	  the substitution promise for every frozen handle producer, discrete ones
10583	  included, and asks a library consumer to choose between pinning its entry
10584	  and refusing every static source; the refusal was the store's identity rule
10585	  applied to a question it was not written for.
10586	- *An embedding for opaque leaves (constructing the handle at the entry):*
10587	  the framework never builds handles; a cross-eltype construction can be a
10588	  grid copy per evaluation, which [D-237][d-237]'s identity rule exists to refuse.
