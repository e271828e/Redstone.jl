12727	### D-303 — Name the connection declarations for wires, and `Group`'s keywords for the declarations
12728	
12729	**Status.** ratified
12730	
12731	**Position.** An assembly's three connection declarations are named for the
12732	wires they return, and `Group`'s keywords and fields take the names of the
12733	declarations they feed.
12734	
12735	- The renames: `inner_connections` → `inner_wires`, `u_connections` →
12736	  `input_wires`, `y_connections` → `output_wires`. Signatures, entry
12737	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
12738	- `Group`'s keywords and fields: `wires` → `inner_wires`, `inputs` →
12739	  `input_wires`, `outputs` → `output_wires`, `rates` → `sample_times`.
12740	- In prose, "wire" covers an entry of any of the three declarations. A
12741	  sentence that means the child-to-child kind alone says "inner wire".
12742	- `fragment`'s `u` keyword stays, and it stands on [§8.6][s8-6] alone. The root's
12743	  input faces come from the boundary declaration or from `u_types`,
12744	  following the root's class, and nothing downstream tells the two apart.
12745	  [D-279][d-279] grounded the keyword on the `u_connections` name, and that ground is
12746	  replaced.
12747	- What stays: `u_types` and `y_types`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
12748	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, `x_deriv` and every diagnostic
12749	  kind name.
12750	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
12751	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.
12752	
12753	**Spec.** [§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.8][s8-8], [§9.1][s9-1], [§11.3][s11-3],
12754	[§11.7][s11-7], [§13.1][s13-1], [§13.3][s13-3], [§13.5][s13-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.1][s14-1], [§14.2][s14-2], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC],
12755	[Appendix D][sD]
12756	
12757	**Rationale.** An assembly writes the trio on adjacent lines, and no
12758	component writes both `u_types` and `u_connections`. Parallel names inside
12759	the trio therefore serve the author more than [D-279][d-279]'s pairing across the two
12760	classes. "Wire" is the spec's everyday noun, and the names are shorter. Each
12761	boundary declaration now shares its prefix with the helper splatted into it,
12762	`input_wires` with `input_passthrough` and `output_wires` with
12763	`output_passthrough`.
12764	
12765	`Group`'s docstring says its declarations are textually identical to a named
12766	assembly's. With keywords equal to declaration names, an author learns one
12767	vocabulary. And `inputs = ("in" => "c/e",)` no longer reads as a list of
12768	inputs, the objection [D-279][d-279] itself raised against `u` and `y` as keywords.
12769	
12770	The `inner_`/`input_` visual near-collision that [D-170][d-170] rejected returns. It
12771	is a reading nuisance only, because an entry written under the wrong
12772	declaration fails at build. Three `Group` builds checked this against the
12773	tree on 2026-10-02. One put an input entry under the inner declaration, one
12774	an inner entry under the input declaration, and one an output entry under
12775	the inner declaration. Each raised a `DiagnosticError`.
12776	
12777	Bare `wires` stays free for locals, so [D-279][d-279]'s ground for rejecting `wires`
12778	as a function name does not apply to the prefixed names.
12779	
12780	**Rejected.**
12781	- *`inner_conns`/`input_conns`/`output_conns`:* a coined abbreviation on the
12782	  authoring surface, where a plain word costs the same characters.
12783	- *`wires_in`/`wires_out`/`wires_local`:* noun-first order breaks the
12784	  pairing with `input_passthrough` and `output_faces`. `wires_in` also reads
12785	  as "the wires in this assembly", and `local` names a scope beside two
12786	  directions.
12787	- *`input_routing`/`output_routing`/`inner_routing`:* the boundary pair is no
12788	  shorter than the names it replaces, and "routing" names an arrangement,
12789	  not the pairs returned ([D-301][d-301]).
12790	- *Short `Group` keywords (`wires`, `inputs`, `outputs`, `rates`) beside the
12791	  declaration names:* two vocabularies for one thing.
12792	
12793	Annotation (2026-10-06): [D-314][d-314] renames `inner_wires` to `local_wires`;
12794	the near-collision this entry accepted as a nuisance is removed rather than
12795	tolerated. The boundary names, `Group`'s keywords, the "wire" convention
12796	and the rest stand.
