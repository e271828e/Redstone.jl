10013	### D-259 — Retire the strata: the build is three steps named by their products
10014	
10015	**Status.** ratified
10016	
10017	**Position.** The spec names the build's steps by what they produce and
10018	retires "stratum".
10019	
10020	- The three steps are the structure step (the root instance in, `Structure`
10021	  out), the nominal evaluation (`Structure` in; `Dataflow`, `Events` and the
10022	  nominal `Float64` `Activation` out) and activation at a scalar
10023	  (`Structure`, `Dataflow` and the nominal activation in, `Activation{T}`
10024	  out). [§9.1][s9-1] opens with a table of what each step consumes and produces,
10025	  with the deployment and the materialization on it as the steps after the
10026	  build.
10027	- Each step is a barrier, as each stratum was ([§13.1][s13-1]). No user stage code
10028	  runs before the structure step completes. The nominal evaluation's
10029	  products are names only. Only activation re-runs per scalar.
10030	- The activation gloss is "the build's typed products at a given scalar
10031	  type". "Stratum A" reads "the structure step", "Stratum B" "the nominal
10032	  evaluation", "Stratum C" "activation"; [Appendix C][sC]'s group headings, [§9.6][s9-6]'s
10033	  heading and the glossary follow.
10034	- The log keeps its vocabulary of the day (`decisions_style.md` rule 2).
10035	  [D-253][d-253]'s fourth bullet is amended in place to the nominal evaluation's
10036	  return.
10037	
10038	**Spec.** [§5.6][s5-6], [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.6][s9-6], [§13.1][s13-1], [§13.2][s13-2], [§13.3][s13-3], [§14.3][s14-3], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
10039	
10040	**Rationale.** The strata were named before any step existed as code. Once
10041	each step had a product ([D-253][d-253]), each letter had become a second name for
10042	that product: "Stratum A's product" for `Structure`, "a re-run of Stratum C"
10043	for an activation. A term that needs the product's name to be understood is
10044	the product's name. The letters also encoded a sequence the pipeline does
10045	not run. [D-253][d-253] as first written had Stratum C complete the nominal
10046	activation from B's stage-1 products, but `Events` is probed against every
10047	component's complete nominal product and the layout, so B runs the whole
10048	nominal chain, and a C run at `Float64` would be the separate pass [§9.1][s9-1]
10049	forbade. Stating each step by what it consumes and produces says that seam
10050	plainly: the nominal evaluation consumes one evaluation at `Float64` and
10051	fixes both the structure and the `Float64` typing, and activation consumes
10052	another scalar and fixes the typing at it. Everything the strata carried
10053	survives without the word: the barrier rule, the line between declaration
10054	reading and evaluation, and the line between structural and typed products.
10055	"Step" is the noun because the spec already uses it for deploying and
10056	materializing ([§9.2][s9-2]), and the integration step is always qualified
10057	("integration step", "step boundary", `step!`).
10058	
10059	**Rejected.**
10060	- *Keeping the letters with restated definitions:* the definitions would
10061	  have been the products' names with a letter in front.
10062	- *"Stage", "phase" or "pass" as the generic noun:* each already names
10063	  something (the stage functions, the sweep phases and the tick phase, the
10064	  declarative passes within a step).
10065	- *One function for the nominal evaluation and activation:* [D-253][d-253]'s
10066	  rejection stands; the nominal evaluation fixes structure and an activation
10067	  does not.
