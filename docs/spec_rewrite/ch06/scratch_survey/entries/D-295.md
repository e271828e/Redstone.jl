12460	### D-295 — The walk clause's tier scope, the obligation's scope, seedability and the unmarked stores
12461	
12462	**Status.** ratified
12463	
12464	**Position.** Four input and store rulings that [§8.2][s8-2] states are fixed here.
12465	
12466	- The walk-compatibility clause binds continuous consumers only. Discrete
12467	  consumers take the bound check only, because their stages read exclusively
12468	  at real ticks in the nominal world, where no `Dual`-carrying cell exists,
12469	  so continuous → discrete wires are unconditionally legal. The unscoped
12470	  clause is rejected: it would reject every continuous → discrete wire.
12471	- The genericity obligation, that the consumer's math promotes whatever
12472	  scalars the wiring delivers, binds the unpinned entries only and is checked
12473	  by the `Dual` probe. A `Pinned` entry imposes no such obligation, which is
12474	  its point.
12475	- A root input's type is its entry at `Float64`, and its cells at an
12476	  activation follow that entry retyped at the activation's `T`. Seedability
12477	  is therefore schema-visible: an unpinned root input is a lawful
12478	  linearization `B`-matrix tap, and a `Pinned` one is declaredly unseedable.
12479	- The stores take no marker. The type derived from `x_init` walks, and
12480	  `m_init` and `s_init` pin wholesale. `Pinned` has no place in a store,
12481	  because [§7.1][s7-1] admits no pinned state leaf.
12482
