2606	### D-084 — Drop the unconnected-output warning
2607	
2608	**Status.** ratified
2609	
2610	**Position.** No unconnected-output warning: under mandatory `output_types`
2611	models carry many observation-oriented ports no wire consumes ([§11.2][s11-2] blesses
2612	exactly that), and [D-083][d-083]'s path-bound readers attach post-build, so "unused" is
2613	undecidable at build.
2614	
2615	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§11.2][s11-2], [§11.7][s11-7], [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix C][sC]
2616	
2617	**Rationale.** Such a warning fires on every honest port and poisons the sole
2618	warning stream ([§11.7][s11-7]'s anti-diagnostic lesson); the hazard it would guard (a
2619	wire someone meant to draw) is the consumer side's unconnected-*input* error,
2620	where the information actually lives; the warning stream survives, empty.
2621	
2622	**Rejected.**
2623	- *Superseded position — warning on unconnected outputs (earlier design of
2624	  record):* a stream of false positives trains users to skip it.
2625	- *Narrowing it:* no principled subset exists — "should have been wired" is not
2626	  decidable from structure, and every candidate predicate still hits honest
2627	  diagnostic ports.
