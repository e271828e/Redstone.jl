899	### D-025 — One uniform device kind
900	
901	**Status.** ratified
902	
903	**Position.** One device kind: uniform handle with read (snapshot /
904	next-boundary) + stage + control capabilities; input-only/output-only as
905	degenerate uses; bidirectional peer = one device; GUI an ordinary device
906	(main-thread affinity and RMW widgets its only peculiarities).
907	
908	**Spec.** [§11.6][s11-6]
909	
910	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
911	
912	**Rejected.**
913	- *Input/output/GUI taxonomy:* lock choreography artifact — blocking rules of
914	  `get_data!`/`extract_output` under `io_lock`; forces bidirectional peers into
915	  two devices sharing a socket and shutdown.
916	- *Special-cased GUI interface:* `sync = 0` + render-under-lock ceremony,
917	  obsolete without the lock.
