6432	### D-176 — Unify trace retention on one sparse record format
6433	
6434	**Status.** ratified
6435	
6436	**Position.** Trace retention unifies on one sparse record format (supersedes
6437	[D-107][d-107]'s density rule): the verbatim-vs-sparse dichotomy is eliminated —
6438	**every** writer's drained batch is sparse-converted at the drain into
6439	(position ⇒ value) pairs against the header's schema, so the trace has one
6440	consumer-facing record format, no per-entry format flag, and replay one
6441	inverse-conversion path.
6442	
6443	**Spec.** [§9.8][s9-8], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.7][s12-7], [Appendix D][sD]
6444	
6445	**Rationale.** Costs recorded where they are paid: ~2× trace size on the dense
6446	component (two small values per touched face where the tuple held one, the
6447	order unchanged and [D-029][d-029]'s two-orders-below-the-log budget untouched); the
6448	conversion allocates once per drained batch, in-class with [§9.8][s9-8]'s retention
6449	carve-out and smaller than the log's per-boundary snapshot, which the carve-out
6450	already covers. The decision is **reversible as pure implementation**:
6451	conversion is lossless both ways, so verbatim retention could return as a
6452	per-entry storage optimization without touching the record semantics, the
6453	header or replay. Motivation: [D-174][d-174] made "enumerated ⇒ claim-narrow ⇒ dense"
6454	false — a greedy claim is enumerated and as wide as the root contract — so
6455	density could only be re-keyed on the claim source, i.e. on a distinction [D-174][d-174]
6456	exists to erase.
6457	
6458	**Rejected.**
6459	- *Keying retention by claim source:* a dichotomy needing re-justification at
6460	  every change to the surface machinery, resting on a premise — enumerated
6461	  means narrow — that `is_greedy` falsifies.
6462	- *Deciding density per batch at the drain:* per-frame heterogeneous record
6463	  formats, a format flag on every entry and two decoders in every consumer, for
6464	  no customer that ever asked to tell the two apart.
