12937	### D-305 — The zero-allocation invariant's scope, its grounds and its budgets
12938	
12939	**Status.** ratified
12940	
12941	**Position.** Three allocation rulings that [§9.8][s9-8] states are fixed here.
12942	
12943	- The zero-allocation invariant is scoped to the stepping loop. The
12944	  stopped-sim services were always allocation-tolerant.
12945	- The policy is scoped, not blanket dogma, which fights logging reality. A
12946	  policy is kept, because without one the type-instability canary is lost.
12947	- Guards and `x_projection`, which run unconditionally per boundary or frame,
12948	  take the exactly-zero budget. Handlers, which are episodic and run only on
12949	  firing, take the ticks' zero-by-idiom budget.
12950	
12951	**Spec.** [§9.8][s9-8]
12952	
12953	**Rationale.** Each bullet restates what an existing entry recorded outside a
12954	Position. The invariant's scope and the services' tolerance were recorded in
12955	[D-135][d-135]'s Rationale. Blanket dogma and the lost canary were recorded in [D-014][d-014]'s
12956	Rejected list. The budgets' membership was recorded in [D-116][d-116]'s Rationale,
12957	which calls the budgets tiers and the projection `project`.
12958	
12959	**Rejected.** None beyond the source entries' lists.
