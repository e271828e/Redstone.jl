13580	### D-317 — Split the `Model` from the `Simulation`, and run only the nominal one
13581	
13582	**Status.** ratified
13583	
13584	**Position.** A `Model{T}` is a deployment materialized at one scalar, the
13585	`Deployment` and its `Executor`, and a `Simulation` runs a `Model{Float64}`
13586	beside the data plane, the control plane and the run.
13587	
13588	- `Model{T}` holds the deployment, the executor, its status and its
13589	  diagnostic cell. `Simulation` is a mutable struct of four fields, the
13590	  model, the run, the data plane and the control plane. Every field of each
13591	  type is used by every instance of it.
13592	- Only a `Model{Float64}` is run, and the rule is a type on the one
13593	  constructor that owns a loop. `Simulation(model::Model{Float64};
13594	  join_timeout = 5.0)` refuses a model at any other scalar as a
13595	  `MethodError`, and `Simulation`, `Run`, `Trace` and `TerminationRecord`
13596	  lose their scalar parameter. `Model(deployment, T = Float64; chunk_size =
13597	  16)` is the materialization, and `Simulation(deployment; …)`,
13598	  `Simulation(build; …)` and `Simulation(root; …)` compose through a
13599	  `Float64` model. `_frozen` and the event compile guard in `build.jl` stay
13600	  as they are, and no `AbstractFloat` heuristic exists anywhere.
13601	- A `Model` at another scalar is public, and it is a service's scratch. It
13602	  can be written to, evaluated and stepped frame by frame, with no ticks and
13603	  no events. Activations stay cached on the `Build` by scalar type, and
13604	  executors are instantiated per use and never cached ([D-282][d-282]).
13605	- The clock keeps `t` and `t₀` on the executor, and `init!` on the model
13606	  sets both. The frame index and the boundary ordinal are the run's
13607	  counters. Every door sets them as it builds the run, the loop advances
13608	  them, and every snapshot publishes them.
13609	- The model owns the frame. `frame!(model, k, hooks) → Bool` runs
13610	  `frame_top!(hooks)` before it touches any state and `settled!(hooks)` at
13611	  every settled boundary, `t*` boundaries and the frame top's included. A
13612	  `true` from `settled!` abandons the frame's remainder and is `frame!`'s
13613	  return. The hooks are one `FrameHooks` struct with concrete fields, built
13614	  once per advance, and `NoHooks` does nothing and returns `false`. `init!`
13615	  on the model takes the hooks too, so boundary zero publishes through
13616	  `settled!`. The frame's chattering and firing-budget reports go to the
13617	  model's diagnostic cell, and the drain folds it into the loop's account
13618	  beside the loop's own cell.
13619	- The execution cursor is the model's. It stays on the executor, and
13620	  `frame!` writes every phase, the drain's included, before `frame_top!`
13621	  runs.
13622	- The model's status is `:built`, `:consistent` or `:inconsistent`, written
13623	  only by the model's own doors and catch sites. `Control` holds a running
13624	  flag in place of the lifecycle state, and `lifecycle(sim)` derives the
13625	  five states from the flag, the status and whether the run is closed. A
13626	  set flag reads `running`. With the flag clear, `:built` reads `built`,
13627	  `:inconsistent` reads `errored`, and `:consistent` reads `initialized`
13628	  while the run is open and `stopped` once it is closed.
13629	- The model's `frame!` and `init!` host the catch and build the
13630	  `StepError` through the one constructor. A throw inside a frame writes
13631	  `:inconsistent`, and a throw inside boundary zero writes `:built`. An
13632	  `InterruptException` is never wrapped and writes no status inside a
13633	  frame. The simulation's catch keeps the disposition alone, the mask, the
13634	  interrupt arms and the termination.
13635	- The model's doors are `init!`, `apply!`, `evaluate!`, `integrate!`,
13636	  `boundary!`, `offtick_boundary!`, `boundary_zero!`, `frame!`,
13637	  `phase_bodies`, `warnings`, `port`, `state` and `modes`. The simulation's
13638	  are `run!`, `step!`, `replay!`, `restore!`, `checkpoint`, `live!`, the
13639	  devices, the recording and its accessors. `init!` on a simulation
13640	  forwards to the model's after the operational gate and does the loop's
13641	  bookkeeping around it. The seven model doors a simulation already had
13642	  keep a forwarding method on it, and no model door carries a gate.

13670	**Spec.** [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [§11.5][s11-5], [§11.8][s11-8], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.6][s12-6], [§13.4][s13-4], [§13.5][s13-5], [§14.5][s14-5], [Appendix C][sC]
13671	
13672	**Rationale.** A `Simulation` built at any scalar other than `Float64` ran
13673	without discrete ticks and without state events. Two rules in `build.jl` do
13674	it. `_frozen` gates a discrete component off at every non-`Float64` scalar,
13675	and the event system compiles only under `T === Float64`. Both rules are
13676	correct, since they transcribe [§9.4][s9-4] and the glossary's `nominal`. The
13677	`Float64` activation is the only one that runs in real time, and every
13678	other activation evaluates the model at a frozen instant. A `Dual`
13679	simulation stepped through `run!` was that evaluation, and the suite
13680	exercised it on purpose. The defect was that `Simulation(deployment,
13681	Float32)` was accepted, so a request that reads as a precision choice was
13682	answered with a frozen-instant evaluator, silently.
13683	
13684	The deeper defect was that `Simulation` fused two things. The model's
13685	structure and behaviour lived in the deployment and the executor, and the
13686	runtime that steps it through time and talks to devices lived in the plane,
13687	the control and the run. The stopped-sim services and `init!` need only the
13688	first pair. `apply!` on a simulation was `apply!` on its executor,
13689	`boundary_zero!` read the executor and nothing else, and `port`, `state`
13690	and `modes` read the executor and the build. `trim!`'s `_scratch` already
13691	built the pair anonymously, as `compile(build, activation, schedule)` with
13692	no plane, control or run around it. A `Dual` simulation therefore allocated
13693	a data plane, a control block and a placeholder run it could never use, and
13694	exposed a loop whose semantics at that scalar were an accident of
13695	genericity. Naming the pair puts the scalar rule on the one constructor
13696	that owns a loop, as a type.
13697	
13698	The model owns the frame because a hybrid trajectory is defined by where
13699	its events fire. The `t*` boundaries are model behaviour, and whether
13700	anyone publishes them is the runtime's business. The frame computes its
13701	target as `t₀ + k·h`, so the model needs `t₀`. The origin is time, not a
13702	count, and it stays on the clock beside `t`. The frame index and the
13703	boundary ordinal are counts the trajectory owns, reset at every door, so
13704	they are the run's. The model's `frame!` and `init!` host the catch because
13705	they hold every fact a `StepError` carries, the cursor, `t`, the entry
13706	boundary `k - 1` and the host. The status takes three values because a
13707	model never initialized and one whose sequence threw are unusable for
13708	different reasons, which `lifecycle(sim)` reports as `built` and `errored`.
13709	With `frame!` writing the drain phase and its `comp = 0; fn = :none` reset,
13710	no writer of the cursor remains outside the model.
13711	
13712	**Rejected.**
13713	- *Widening the two `build.jl` rules to `AbstractFloat`:* an unspecified
13714	  third mode, the continuous tier at `Float32` beside a discrete tier
13715	  pinned at `Float64` ([§9.4][s9-4], [D-263][d-263]), with [D-053][d-053]'s embedding rounding at
13716	  every wire and nothing in CI pinning any of it.
13717	- *A type heuristic refusing `AbstractFloat` scalars at the `Simulation`
13718	  door:* it refuses the symptom and keeps the fusion, so a `Dual`
13719	  simulation would still carry a plane, a control and a run it cannot use.
13720	- *Refusing only the advance doors at a non-nominal simulation:* the same
13721	  fusion, and a lifecycle whose legal doors depend on the scalar.
13722	- *`t₀` on the `Deployment`:* the deployment is a value shared by many
13723	  simulations and compared as one at replay and restore. The origin is
13724	  chosen per trajectory at every door, two runs of one deployment start at
13725	  different origins, and replay checks the header's `t₀` as a clock
13726	  mismatch, not a deployment one.
13727	- *`t₀` on the run, with the counters:* the frame computes its target from
13728	  `t₀` and the model owns the frame, so a standalone model would need a run
13729	  to step.
13730	- *The model rethrowing raw and the simulation wrapping:* a standalone
13731	  model's failure would reach its caller without the cursor frame, and the
13732	  facts the wrap reads are the frame's own.
13733	- *Ad hoc closures as hooks:* a reassigned capture becomes a `Core.Box` and
13734	  an abstract captured field makes the return abstract, either one turning
13735	  the hook call into dynamic dispatch. A struct with concrete fields
13736	  specializes `frame!` as a closure would.
13737	- *A boolean model status:* it cannot tell a model never initialized from
13738	  one whose sequence threw.
13739	- *Two cursors, one the model's and one the loop's:* once `frame!` writes
13740	  the drain phase no foreign writer remains, so a second cursor has nothing
13741	  to record.
13742	- *Absorbing the drain into the frame:* the writers' cells, the trace record
13743	  and the replay feed are the simulation's, and a model that drained them
13744	  would hold the runtime again. The top hook keeps the ordering rule that a
13745	  batch taken at the top of frame `k` is recorded at `k`, because the run's
13746	  counter advances where the simulation chooses.
13747	- *Caching executors:* a cached buffer set has two owners, which is [D-282][d-282]'s
13748	  aliasing.
13749	- *Superseded position — the five-field `Simulation` ([D-256][d-256]):* it held the
13750	  model's pair beside the runtime, so every simulation carried a loop
13751	  whatever its scalar.
