8788	### D-232 — Refuse the roster operations on an errored simulation
8789	
8790	**Status.** ratified
8791	
8792	**Position.** `attach!` and `detach!` refuse an `errored` simulation with
8793	`ServiceLifecycle`, as they refuse a running one; [§11.3][s11-3]'s list of legal
8794	states — `built`, `initialized`, `stopped` — is exhaustive. The stopped-sim
8795	reads stay admitted there: post-mortem inspection is reading.
8796	
8797	**Spec.** [§11.3][s11-3], [§13.6][s13-6], [Appendix B][sB]
8798	
8799	**Rationale.** The implementation gated the roster operations and the log
8800	reader behind one check that refused only `running`, and called admitting
8801	`errored` deliberate on [§13.6][s13-6]'s post-mortem grounds. Those grounds cover the
8802	reads: [§13.6][s13-6] and [§14][s14] retain an errored simulation's stores, log and trace as
8803	a diagnostic read. A roster change is not a read. It configures the next run,
8804	and an errored simulation has none, every advance entry and `init!` refusing
8805	it under [D-059][d-059]. An attachment admitted there compiles a writer, stakes claims
8806	and returns a handle nothing will ever drain: the "device attached, nothing
8807	happens" shape [D-105][d-105] rejected as a hidden bug, here without a diagnostic.
8808	`detach!` gains nothing either. `AlreadyAttached` is per roster, so the
8809	device instance may join a fresh simulation without leaving the dead one, and
8810	the tail already ran its `shutdown!`.
8811	
8812	**Rejected.**
8813	- *Adding `errored` to the legal list:* the one rationale on offer,
8814	  post-mortem inspection, justifies reads and not configuration.
8815	- *One gate for all three, `logged` refused beside them:* [§14][s14] mandates the
8816	  post-mortem read.
