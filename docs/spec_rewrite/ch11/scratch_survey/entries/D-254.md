9829	### D-254 — `Deployment` and `Schedule` as artifacts between the build and the simulation
9830	
9831	**Status.** ratified
9832	
9833	**Position.** Deployment binding produces an artifact, and the `Simulation`
9834	materializes it.
9835	
9836	- `Deployment` is the build plus `h`, `N_base`, `Δt_base`, the algorithm
9837	  and the three event parameters. It is scalar-free. It carries the
9838	  `Schedule`, the grid diagnostics of [D-187][d-187] and its warnings.
9839	  `Simulation` materializes a deployment at `T`. Replay compares two
9840	  deployments as values.
9841	- `Schedule` is the typed schedule inside the deployment: per discrete
9842	  component `(D, Φ, Δt)` with anchor and rate-chain columns, the rate-scope
9843	  rows, and the `D`, `Φ`, `Δt` vectors.
9844	- The grid diagnostics land on the deployment: leave-one-out factors, prime
9845	  attribution, nearest non-refining offsets, the derivation line and
9846	  `GridUtilization`, the last as a deployment warning under [D-250][d-250].
9847	- `Simulation(build; kw...)` and `Simulation(root; kw...)` stay as sugar
9848	  composing the deployment constructor.
9849	
9850	**Spec.** [§8.7][s8-7], [§9.2][s9-2], [§10.5][s10-5], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.7][s12-7], [Appendix C][sC]
9851	
9852	**Rationale.** Deployment consumes the grid parameters and fixes everything
9853	they determine, the bound schedule and the grid diagnostics among them, and
9854	none of it depends on the scalar type. Today that step ran inside the
9855	`Simulation` constructor and its products lived as `Simulation` fields:
9856	`sched`, `D`, `Φ`, `Δt`, the grid parameters, the event parameters. The
9857	bound schedule was "a named printable artifact on the `Simulation`"
9858	([D-187][d-187]) with no type of its own, and the grid diagnostics it was to
9859	substrate were never built. A deployment that is an artifact can be
9860	compared, which is what replay's header check does with the deployment
9861	block today by hand, and it can be printed, which is what the anchor and
9862	component tables and the hyperperiod chart need ([D-257][d-257]). It also separates
9863	the two reasons a `Simulation` exists: one build backs many deployments,
9864	one deployment backs many activations at different `T`. The convenience
9865	constructors stay because every existing call site deploys and materializes
9866	in one call, and nothing in the artifact is lost by composing.
9867	
9868	Annotation (2026-10-09): amended by [D-317][d-317]. A `Model` materializes a
9869	deployment at `T`, and a `Simulation` runs a `Model{Float64}`. The two
9870	convenience constructors compose through a `Float64` model.
9871	
9872	**Rejected.**
9873	- *Binding inside the `Simulation` constructor, as today:* the products
9874	  have no type, cannot be compared or printed on their own, and the
9875	  `Simulation` carries twelve fields that are not its.
9876	- *The schedule on the `Build`:* it does not exist until `Δt_base` binds,
9877	  and one build backs deployments with different `Δt_base`.
9878	- *A `Deployment{T}`:* nothing in it depends on the scalar; parametrizing
9879	  it would make two deployments at different `T` unequal.
9880	- *Superseded position — the bound schedule as plain data on the
9881	  `Simulation` ([D-187][d-187]):* the artifact it names now has a type and an owner.
