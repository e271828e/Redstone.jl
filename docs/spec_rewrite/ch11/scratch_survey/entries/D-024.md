872	### D-024 — Inbound staging: per-device atomic batch cells
873	
874	**Status.** ratified
875	
876	**Position.** Inbound staging: one atomic batch cell per device; complete
877	writers overwrite, sparse writers CAS-merge own cell (retry bounded by drain
878	interception); drain by `atomicswap` in attachment order; conflicts resolved by
879	slot exclusivity ([D-044][d-044]); levels-never-deltas doctrine; mappings pure, on the
880	device task; device-tagged replayable input trace.
881	
882	**Spec.** [§11.4][s11-4]
883	
884	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
885	
886	**Rejected.**
887	- *Superseded position — attachment order as the cross-device conflict
888	  precedence policy (the earlier design of record):* superseded by slot
889	  exclusivity, which removes the conflict instead of ranking it (the per-device
890	  cells, CAS merge and drain are retained for atomicity and coalescing).
891	- *Per-slot cells:* conflicts by hardware store order — run-to-run behavioral
892	  variance; cross-device peek; no trace tag; atomic-width fallback on
893	  wide slots.
894	- *Shared batch stack:* temporal conflict order; unbounded pending under pause,
895	  taxing peeks.
896	- *Ordered write queue:* preserves intra-frame order nothing downstream can
897	  observe.
