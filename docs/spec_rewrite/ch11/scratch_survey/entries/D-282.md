11945	### D-282 — Give every buffer set exactly one owner
11946	
11947	**Status.** ratified
11948	
11949	**Position.** Every buffer set has exactly one owner, so buffers are never
11950	cached.
11951	
11952	- The `Simulation` owns its nominal activation's buffers, materialized from
11953	  the cached layouts at construction.
11954	- Every service invocation owns the scratch set it instantiates from those
11955	  same layouts.
11956	- The `Build` is immutable apart from its lazily filled activation
11957	  dictionary, and may back any number of deployments and `Simulation`s
11958	  concurrently.
11959	
11960	**Spec.** [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [§11.1][s11-1], [§14.8][s14-8], [§14.10][s14-10]
11961	
11962	**Rationale.** [§14.8][s14-8]'s per-invocation rule for `trim!` is the general one.
11963	[D-070][d-070] forced it: iterating on shared buffers aliases the sim's authoritative
11964	stores, warn-but-assign reborn. Single ownership is also what lets the `Build`
11965	back any number of `Simulation`s concurrently ([D-135][d-135]).
11966	
11967	Annotation (2026-10-09): amended by [D-317][d-317]. The `Model` owns its
11968	activation's buffers, and a `Simulation` owns its `Model{Float64}`.
11969	
11970	Annotation (2026-10-09): amended by [D-319][d-319]. Every service invocation owns a
11971	scratch `Model` for each half of its working set, materialized from the
11972	deployment of the `Model{Float64}` it was called on.
11973	
11974	**Rejected.**
11975	- *Cached shared buffers:* [D-070][d-070]'s aliasing — warn-but-assign reborn — and it
11976	  makes the `Build` mutable in exactly the way multi-`Simulation` sharing
11977	  forbids. (As recorded in [D-135][d-135].)
