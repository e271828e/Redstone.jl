2058	### D-068 — Enforce slot totality at the init/commit service boundary
2059	
2060	**Status.** ratified
2061	
2062	**Position.** Slot totality is enforced at the service: `init!` and commit
2063	compare resolved slot coverage against `input_faces` before writing anything,
2064	and a shortfall raises one batched, declaration-ordered `UninitializedSlots`
2065	diagnostic, all-or-nothing — a rejected init leaves the sim untouched.
2066	
2067	- `probe_value` is structurally unreachable from the services path: a condition
2068	  value or an error, no third branch; replay applies header-recorded slots and
2069	  never synthesizes, header slot capture being complete by construction.
2070	- Baselines are aircraft-shipped full-coverage condition functions
2071	  (`ready_for_taxi(ac)` — `SystemsInitializer` defaults reborn as user math, one
2072	  home).
2073	- `override(base, patch)` is admitted as the fourth node kind: ordered and
2074	  asymmetric against `merge`'s symmetric collision-intolerance, patch winning
2075	  with an origin recording both layers; within-layer collisions still error;
2076	  layering is variadic; trim commits `override(baseline, solution)`.
2077	
2078	**Spec.** [§14.6][s14-6]
2079	
2080	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
2081	
2082	**Rejected.**
2083	- *Face-declaration defaults:* condition data inside the wiring contract;
2084	  reopens [§11.3][s11-3] bare-types and the competing-defaults problem.
2085	- *Silent zero-fill of uncovered slots:* the [§9.3][s9-3] probe-value leak — a
2086	  fabricated zero is a fine probe input and a terrible flight condition.
2087	- *Totality as a condition-value property:* conditions are legitimately
2088	  partial; totality belongs to boundary-zero application.
2089	- *Service-level base keyword:* hard-codes two layers; composition semantics in
2090	  a service signature instead of the condition algebra.
