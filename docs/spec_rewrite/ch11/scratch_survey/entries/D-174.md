6346	### D-174 — Re-class the GUI as an ordinary enumerated writer
6347	
6348	**Status.** ratified
6349	
6350	**Position.** The GUI is re-classed as an ordinary enumerated writer and the
6351	derived surface class retires: a binding's claim set has two *sources* and one
6352	meaning — **returned** by `claims(b)`, or **computed** by the framework at
6353	attach as the unclaimed complement (all root input faces minus the union of the
6354	rostered claims) when the binding declares `is_greedy(b) = true` — so
6355	greediness is a claim source, not a device class, and everything downstream of
6356	claim acquisition is identical for both: exclusivity validation (trivially
6357	satisfied for the complement, disjoint from every rostered claim by
6358	construction), roster-entry storage, staging-shape/shim/merge/scatter
6359	compilation, drain, trace and detach-releases-claims.
6360	
6361	**Spec.** [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.4][s11-4], [§11.6][s11-6], [§11.7][s11-7]
6362	
6363	**Rationale.** A second greedy attach finds an empty complement and stakes an
6364	**empty claim** — legal, [§11.6][s11-6]'s honest may-write-nothing degenerate — plus the
6365	attach-time diagnostic `EmptyGreedyClaim` (warning (service), [D-159][d-159]'s sixth
6366	severity); partial-claims GUI bindings are legal, and **multiple interactive
6367	front ends with explicit disjoint claims** become legal (a web console claiming
6368	the autopilot faces beside a local GUI claiming the stick faces), only
6369	`needs_calling_task` staying singleton.
6370	
6371	The `interactive(b)` marker and `InteractiveConflict` are retired and admission
6372	narrows to three parts — identity, affinity, claims.
6373	
6374	The **harness writer** (renamed from the interactive writer) is the sole
6375	remaining derived surface: the framework-owned `stage!(sim, ...)` entry point
6376	and its always-present cell, covering the unclaimed complement, recomputed at
6377	every stopped-sim `attach!`/`detach!`, with the pending-batch renormalization
6378	seam now applying to it alone and `ClaimedFaceEntry` narrowed to it (an
6379	enumerated writer's out-of-surface entry, the GUI's included, is the ordinary
6380	`OutOfClaimEntry`).
6381	
6382	Widget liveness becomes: live iff the port's feed chain terminates in a root
6383	slot **inside the GUI's own claim**; the harness cell still drains last, now as
6384	plain convention, the "explicit hand of code beats a widget interaction"
6385	arbitration rationale being objectless once surfaces are disjoint.
6386	
6387	Supersedes the interactive-writer halves of [D-044][d-044], [D-104][d-104] and [D-106][d-106].
6388	
6389	**Rejected.**
6390	- *`UserInput`-as-device:* a taskless degenerate inside a loop-body device
6391	  contract, a claim-totality precondition on every other device, and an engine
6392	  auto-attach policy.
6393	- *Run-entry re-staking of greedy claims:* a second claim-staking point in the
6394	  lifecycle, where attach-time computation already has every fact it needs.
6395	- *A `GreedyConflict` singleton mirroring the retired `InteractiveConflict`:*
6396	  the empty remainder is well-defined under attach-time semantics and
6397	  diagnosable as a warning, while two explicit-claims front ends are just two
6398	  enumerated devices — nothing left to police.
