4196	### D-133 — Split spec-invoked numeric constants into deployment parameters vs owning-section defaults
4197	
4198	**Status.** ratified
4199	
4200	**Position.** The constants the spec invoked but never fixed split by whether
4201	they move the trajectory. The two **semantic** ones become deployment
4202	parameters — `Simulation` keywords beside `h`, `n` and the algorithm:
4203	`localization_tol`, a *relative* bracket-width convergence test for [§10.4][s10-4]'s
4204	root-finder (localization converges when the bracket is narrower than
4205	`localization_tol · h`), default `1e-6`; `event_budget`, the per-frame
4206	localization allowance, default 8 — validated with their siblings into
4207	`DeploymentInvalid` (positive tolerance, integer budget ≥ 1) but
4208	grid-independent, hence outside [§10.5][s10-5]'s harmonic-grid check, and **recorded** in
4209	the trace header's deployment block, which takes replay's compared set from
4210	four to six ([§12.7][s12-7]). The four **operational** ones get spec-fixed defaults in
4211	their owning sections and no user knobs: shutdown join timeout 5 s ([§12.4][s12-4](5));
4212	debt forgiveness at five frames' worth of budget, `5·h/p` ([§10.7][s10-7], replacing "a
4213	few frames' worth"); heartbeat staleness 2 s ([§12.2][s12-2]); `margin` = 2 ms ([§10.7][s10-7]'s
4214	own arithmetic), replacing [Appendix B][sB]'s `margin = <default>` placeholder.
4215	[§13.2][s13-2]'s per-device warning rate limit alone is **deferred** to the
4216	diagnostic-stream decision [closed by [D-136][d-136]: the bound is the diagnostic cell's
4217	ring, not a constant]. Finally `t_end`'s landing rule, stated once in [§12.4][s12-4](1):
4218	the run ends at the first grid boundary whose time reaches or exceeds `t_end` —
4219	whole frames only, overshooting by up to `h`, with the termination record
4220	carrying the actual final `t` ([§13.5][s13-5]). This amends [D-127][d-127].
4221	
4222	**Spec.** [§10.4][s10-4], [§10.5][s10-5], [§10.7][s10-7], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.2][s12-2], [§12.4][s12-4], [§12.4][s12-4](1), [§12.4][s12-4](5), [§12.6][s12-6],
4223	[§12.7][s12-7], [§9.2][s9-2], [§13.2][s13-2], [§13.5][s13-5], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
4224	
4225	**Rationale.** Semantic constants: `event_budget`'s default 8 covers a
4226	legitimate multi-event frame — three landing-gear struts touching down inside
4227	one step — needs three or four, chattering needs tens, so 8 bounds the
4228	pathology without ever binding on a healthy model; they are
4229	trajectory-determining in exactly the sense `h` and the algorithm are, and
4230	[§10.4][s10-4]'s "the run replays identically" is an empty promise unless what the
4231	localizer was told is recorded. Operational constants: the gap was ownership,
4232	not configurability — the shutdown join timeout is generous for GUI window
4233	teardown and socket closes, short enough that an abandoned join reads as a
4234	diagnosed timeout rather than a hang; debt forgiveness sits above the ms-scale
4235	GC/scheduler hiccups debt absorbs invisibly, far below the
4236	debugger/laptop-sleep stalls forgiveness exists for; heartbeat staleness is
4237	deliberately loose: the heartbeat is advisory, never a kill trigger, and must
4238	tolerate a device legitimately parked in a blocking read; `margin` = 2 ms
4239	reflects libuv's millisecond timer granularity plus `sleep`'s ≈1.4 ms measured
4240	median overshoot, and the section's "sleeps ~90% of a 20 ms budget"
4241	illustration is exactly this value. `t_end`'s landing rule follows [§12.6][s12-6]'s
4242	`t_plus` precedent ("whole frames until the boundary time first covers the
4243	duration") applied to the run clock, and marks the point where `t_end`, a grid
4244	fact, differs in kind from `stop_on`, checked at every published boundary, `t*`
4245	included.
4246	
4247	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-256][d-256], [D-234][d-234] and [D-181][d-181]. The two
4248	parameters are `Deployment` constructor keywords beside `h`, `N_base` and the
4249	algorithm, `event_budget` is `localization_budget`, and `firing_budget` joins
4250	them as a third recorded parameter.
4251	
4252	**Rejected.**
4253	- *An absolute-in-`t` localization tolerance:* breaks scale-freedom across `h`
4254	  — one number is slack at `h = 1` and unreachable at `h = 10⁻⁴`.
4255	- *Leaving the two semantic constants as implementation constants:* silently
4256	  breaks [§10.4][s10-4]'s replays-identically promise — a trajectory-determining fact
4257	  must be deployed and recorded, not buried in the localizer.
4258	- *User knobs for the operational four:* ownership was the gap, not
4259	  configurability — and [§10.7][s10-7] already settled `margin` as the single pacing
4260	  knob, so a second wall-clock knob reopens a closed decision.
4261	- *Harmonic-grid-coupled validation for `localization_tol`/`event_budget`:*
4262	  they are grid-independent; the coupling would be invented rather than
4263	  derived.
4264	- *Fixing the per-device rate limit now:* the diagnostic stream's structure
4265	  would re-decide it.
4266	- *A short final step landing exactly on `t_end`:* grid integrity `tₖ = t₀ +
4267	  k·h` forbids it, [§10.4][s10-4] — the bookkeeping hazard that rule kills.
4268	- *Ending at the last boundary before `t_end`:* contradicts the `t_plus`
4269	  precedent: a duration is covered, not approached.
