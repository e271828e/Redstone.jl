6466	### D-177 — Re-found the periphery on mandatory roots plus declared traits
6467	
6468	**Status.** ratified
6469	
6470	**Position.** The periphery's declaration idiom is re-founded on declared
6471	traits over mandatory neutral roots: every device subtypes `AbstractDevice` and
6472	every binding `AbstractBinding`, mandatorily, so `attach!(sim,
6473	dev::AbstractDevice, binding::AbstractBinding; should_abort = false)` gates at
6474	dispatch — [§8.5][s8-5]'s one-root rejection does not transfer, the periphery's
6475	single-inheritance slot being vacant (no domain hierarchies compete for it) and
6476	a binding's sidedness being its **public contract**, not the [§8.3][s8-3]-hidden
6477	implementation class a component's tier is.
6478	
6479	**Spec.** [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.6][s11-6], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], Appendices A/B/C, glossary [§D.6][sD-6]
6480	
6481	**Rationale.** Binding sides are **declared by Bool traits** with framework
6482	defaults on the root — `is_input`/`is_output` false, `is_greedy` false, the
6483	claim-source selector *within* the input side — and the enumeration obligations
6484	are enforced by **error fallbacks** (`claims(::AbstractBinding)`,
6485	`reads(::AbstractBinding)`) plus a **bidirectional conformance check at
6486	attach**: is_input && !is_greedy ⇒ `claims(b)` called once (a missing method
6487	fires the fallback loudly); is_input && is_greedy ⇒ claim computed and a
6488	defined `claims` method is an error; is_output ⇒ `reads(b)` called; neither
6489	side declared ⇒ attach-time error; is_greedy && !is_input ⇒ error; and a
6490	*specific* method of `claims`/`reads` defined for the binding type while the
6491	corresponding trait is false ⇒ error, detected by `which` against the fallback
6492	([§8.1][s8-1]'s reflection class, run at a stopped-sim service point).
6493	Trait-true-method-missing and method-defined-trait-false both report the new
6494	diagnostic `BindingContractMismatch` (service), naming the trait and the method
6495	at fault. `map_input`/`map_output` remain conventions of the author-owned loop
6496	idiom the framework never calls — taught in [Appendix A][sA], never checked. What
6497	this closes: under method-presence detection a bidirectional binding whose
6498	`claims` was written *without* extending the framework generic (the [§8.1][s8-1]
6499	shadowing trap, one level down) degraded **silently to output-only** — the
6500	device attached, staked nothing and wrote nothing; `is_input = true` now makes
6501	the absence loud. The `sides(b)` rejection is **answered rather than ignored**:
6502	its ground was redundancy with methods that must exist anyway, and redundancy
6503	*with a cross-check* is drift detection, which is what the bidirectional check
6504	makes of it — annotated inline where [§11.6][s11-6] lists the rejection. Side-encoding
6505	supertypes stay rejected (a bidirectional binding cannot subtype two sides),
6506	and the empty-enumeration doctrine survives untouched: `is_input(b) = true` +
6507	`claims(b) = ()` is the honest may-write-nothing degenerate, while the maximal
6508	surface still requires the explicit `is_greedy` declaration.

6518	**Rejected.**
6519	- *Presence-only probing, the prior design of record:* silent partial
6520	  degradation of a shadowed bidirectional binding, and `hasmethod` is the rarer
6521	  Julia interface idiom beside the AbstractArray/IndexStyle pattern of roots
6522	  plus traits.
6523	- *Optional, unenforced supertypes:* a signal half the ecosystem skips is worse
6524	  than no signal at all — the dispatch gate is the whole value.
6525	- *Side-encoding supertypes `AbstractInputBinding`/`AbstractOutputBinding`:*
6526	  unchanged from the standing rejection — a bidirectional binding cannot
6527	  subtype two sides.
6528	- *Annotation (from [§11.6][s11-6]'s inline treatment, migrated at the P3.2 rewrite,
6529	  2026-08-14):* under presence detection the degradation was not merely silent
6530	  but actively misdirecting — every diagnostic available pointed away from the
6531	  missing import.
