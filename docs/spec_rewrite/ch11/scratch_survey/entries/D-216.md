8011	### D-216 — Conform Appendix C to the prototype's diagnostic sites
8012	
8013	**Status.** ratified
8014	
8015	**Position.** The first increment to raise every refusal as a kind is Appendix
8016	C's first test, and the table moves where the sites showed it wrong.
8017	
8018	- `StopFaceInvalid` and `ClaimConflict` are collected — over the given faces,
8019	  over the device's claim set — and `ArgumentInvalid` collects over a
8020	  `TableBinding`'s entry table; they sit beside `DeploymentInvalid`, already
8021	  collected.
8022	- `AttachUnknownFace` and `ReadBindingUnresolved` name the device by type: a
8023	  refusal that precedes admission cannot carry the roster id admission assigns.
8024	- `ConditionResolution`'s payload lists what its sub-kinds differ by;
8025	  `DeploymentInvalid`'s parameter list gains the run-shaping keywords
8026	  (`join_timeout`, `log`, `log_every`, `log_max`, `t_end`);
8027	  `BindingContractMismatch` gains the binding declaring neither side.
8028	- `DeviceContractMismatch` joins the set: the device twin of
8029	  `BindingContractMismatch` (no `loop` method; `gather` against a binding with
8030	  no output side).
8031	- Every other payload the prototype carries short of its column stays as
8032	  written: the column is the design, and the prototype records its own gaps.
8033	  A selector carried as its rendered spelling is licensed — [§13.2][s13-2]'s rule is
8034	  against instances and model types, and a spelling is a string.
8035	
8036	**Spec.** [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix C][sC]
8037	
8038	**Rationale.** The payload and policy columns were untested design claims
8039	until a site had to construct each value. Three findings needed the table to
8040	move rather than the code. A check that runs over a list — the stop faces
8041	given, the faces a device claims, the entries of a table — is collecting by
8042	its shape, and making it throw on the first would discard violations the pass
8043	already holds, the cost [§13.1][s13-1] exists to avoid. An id assigned at admission
8044	cannot be in a refusal that keeps the device out. A column narrower than the
8045	sub-kinds it names cannot be the acceptance contract for tests that match on
8046	those sub-kinds' payload. Everything else — the wires on an `AlgebraicCycle`,
8047	the declaration on a `FaceNameCollision`, the shadowing notes, the binding site
8048	of a stop face — is data the design wants and the prototype does not yet
8049	gather; the column keeps naming it and the prototype's absence list carries the
8050	shortfall until a later increment closes it.
8051	
8052	**Rejected.**
8053	- *Making the three service checks fail-fast to match the column:* throws
8054	  away violations the pass already has, against [§13.1][s13-1]'s reasoning.
8055	- *Reserving a roster id before admission:* an id for a device the roster
8056	  never held, in every refusal's payload.
8057	- *Shrinking every column the prototype falls short of:* the table would
8058	  document the prototype rather than the design, and the shortfalls would
8059	  stop being visible as such.
8060	- *A datum naming no channel as a kind (`bindings.jl`'s third plain error):*
8061	  it runs on the device task inside the author's mapping, so it reaches the
8062	  framework as the wrapper's `cause` — `MalformedDatum` / `DeviceCrash`
8063	  territory, not a kind of its own.
