9282	### D-244 — Refuse the write primitives of a detached handle by name
9283	
9284	**Status.** ratified
9285	
9286	**Position.** After `detach!`, `stage!` and `report!` on the retired entry's
9287	handle throw `DeviceContractMismatch` with a `:detached` reason. The read
9288	primitives stay legal.
9289	
9290	**Spec.** [§11.6][s11-6], [Appendix C][sC]
9291	
9292	**Rationale.** The handle keeps its own staging cell and its own diagnostic
9293	cell, both compiled at `attach!`, and `detach!` leaves no drain over either.
9294	A batch staged through the retired handle therefore lands in a cell nothing
9295	reads, and a report into its diagnostic cell is equally orphaned, so no
9296	in-channel diagnostic can surface the loss. A throw is the only disposition
9297	that is not silent. It is safe because `detach!` is a stopped-sim operation
9298	([§11.3][s11-3]), so no run's task holds the handle when the entry retires. A straggler
9299	abandoned under the join timeout ([§12.4][s12-4](5)) that stages after a detach takes
9300	the throw inside its own wrapper, which is honest for an abandoned task. The
9301	cost is one flag on the handle, set at `detach!` and loaded once per stage;
9302	`stage!` scans no roster. The reads touch the simulation's published state
9303	and nothing of the entry's, so nothing is lost through them.
9304	
9305	**Rejected.**
9306	- *A kind of its own, `HandleDetached`:* the handle's misuse family is one
9307	  kind, `DeviceContractMismatch`, and `gather` without an output side already
9308	  refuses through it; a third reason arm is the whole difference.
9309	- *A silent discard under `OutOfClaimEntry`:* it lands in the orphaned
9310	  diagnostic cell, the same silence one channel over.
9311	- *A roster scan in `stage!`:* the per-stage cost the compiled handle exists
9312	  to avoid; the flag records the same fact at the one point it changes.
9313	- *Leaving the edge unguarded as run-scoped task equipment:* `attach!` hands
9314	  the handle to the caller, so it is not equipment of the task alone.
