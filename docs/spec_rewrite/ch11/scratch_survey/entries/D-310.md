13052	### D-310 — Park the built-in GUI, run every device spawned, and withdraw `gui = true`
13053	
13054	**Status.** ratified
13055	
13056	**Position.** The built-in Julia GUI is parked in favour of a browser
13057	cockpit over the bridge, and `run!` has one task topology.
13058	
13059	- The loop runs on the calling task in every run, and every rostered
13060	  device runs on a spawned task. `needs_calling_task`, its admission check
13061	  `CallerTaskConflict` and the inline-body branch of `run!` are withdrawn.
13062	- `gui = true` is withdrawn. A front end is attached by hand, once per
13063	  simulation, and the roster keeps it across that simulation's runs.
13064	- [§11.7][s11-7]'s semantics stand unchanged, derived liveness, the peek, staging
13065	  on interaction and the orphan label, and the framework's half of the
13066	  panel convention ([D-270][d-270]) is what the bridge serves. The GUI
13067	  package's half becomes the browser client's, and the panel method returns
13068	  a description rather than drawing. That amendment is ruled when the
13069	  cockpit's design reaches it.
13070	- The handle carries no pause ([D-268][d-268]). Whether it gains the control verbs
13071	  for the bridge is ruled with the bridge's design.
13072	
13073	**Spec.** [§11.1][s11-1], [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.6][s11-6], [§11.7][s11-7], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.2][s12-2], [§12.4][s12-4], [§12.6][s12-6], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC]
13074	
13075	**Rationale.** The browser cockpit was the endgame from the start, and it
13076	subsumes the built-in GUI for every use the design serves. It shows a
13077	snapshot one display frame later, about 15 to 35 ms from publication to
13078	pixel against 8 to 20, which a cockpit does not notice and a control loop
13079	through a widget would, a use the design never served. It lifts the
13080	main-thread constraint, the OpenGL dependency Apple has deprecated and
13081	CImGui.jl's churn. It works headless and remote, which the built-in GUI
13082	never could. Panel authoring by language models favours the browser's
13083	corpus and its verification loop, the type checker, tests over fixture
13084	descriptors and a headless screenshot, over a thin Julia wrapper with GL
13085	state under Revise. And the static inspector, the live inspector and the
13086	cockpit become one client over one protocol, so the external interface
13087	gets its reference client as a by-product.
13088	
13089	The attended mode existed for CImGui's main-thread rule alone ([D-109][d-109]), and
13090	the flag's run-scoping existed because a window's lifetime was the run's
13091	([D-175][d-175]). A browser tab's lifetime is the session's, so one explicit
13092	`attach!` per simulation is the right spelling, and `inspect(sim)` the
13093	sugar if any. Deleting the attended mode leaves `run!` with one shape to
13094	specify and test. [D-109][d-109]'s identity and admission halves survive in [§11.3][s11-3]:
13095	identity by instance, the stable id at attach, `AlreadyAttached` and
13096	`ClaimConflict`. Its affinity third and its topology rationale fall. [D-175][d-175]
13097	falls whole. The parked design, its source and its tests last stand
13098	together at commit b6f08b1, and the GUI record's built-in half was folded
13099	into the inspector's record when this entry was written.
13100	
13101	**Rejected.**
13102	- *Keeping `needs_calling_task` as harmless machinery:* two run topologies
13103	  to specify, test and reason about, with no device holding the trait. A
13104	  native-window device parks with the GUI it served, and the code is one
13105	  revert away.
13106	- *Building the built-in GUI as a warm-up:* the one step whose output the
13107	  endgame does not reuse. The bridge finds the handle's gaps as well,
13108	  and it is the first thing the cockpit needs.
13109	- *Retargeting `gui = true` to the bridge:* the flag's semantics are
13110	  a window's, attach at entry and detach in the tail. A tab outlives runs.
