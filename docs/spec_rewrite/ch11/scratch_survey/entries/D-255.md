9884	### D-255 — `Run`, the trace split and `StopPolicy`
9885	
9886	**Status.** ratified
9887	
9888	**Position.** A run is state with a type of its own, and its policy is a
9889	value bound per advance.
9890	
9891	- `Run{T}` is a mutable struct with four `const` fields, `t₀`, `mode`, `log`
9892	  and `trace`, and two writable ones, `policy` and `termination`. `init!`
9893	  and `replay!` construct one; the loop's tail writes `termination` once.
9894	  `closed(run)` is `termination !== nothing`.
9895	- `Run` is a `Simulation` field beside `control`, so `Simulation` is a
9896	  mutable struct. A `Simulation{T}` is constructed with a placeholder run
9897	  (`t₀ = zero(T)`, `mode = :live`, empty log and trace, no termination) that
9898	  `init!` and `replay!` replace; `Control.lifecycle` says whether it
9899	  started. `termination` leaves `Control`, which keeps the stop word, the
9900	  lifecycle and the wait. `init!` allocates fresh objects rather than
9901	  clearing.
9902	- `Trace{T}` is a fixed header plus two append-only lists, `schemas` and
9903	  `batches`, and its length, the drains since the capture. The header loses
9904	  its schema list; `attach!` and `detach!` push onto the list in place. The
9905	  drain advances the length on the live trace, and `trace(sim)` freezes it
9906	  into the value it hands back; no reader of the run needs it, every reader
9907	  of the length reads a trace. The register keeps its cursor fields and the
9908	  replay feed.
9909	- `StopPolicy` replaces `RunPolicy`: an immutable value of `t_end` plus the
9910	  stop faces and their addresses, built and validated by each advance and
9911	  rebound on `Run.policy`. `hit` leaves it for the loop's scratch beside the
9912	  cursor. `ControlRequestedStop` is outside the policy: the policy is what
9913	  the caller declares, the stop word is what anyone can issue.
9914	- `t_end` and `stop_on` leave the constructor and are keywords of `run!` and
9915	  `replay!`, with `Inf` and no faces as defaults, validated per call.
9916	  `step!` states the same defaults itself. [§13.5][s13-5]'s second binding site goes.
9917	  A run with `Inf` and no faces is allowed and raises `UnboundedRun` into
9918	  the loop's diagnostic cell, the status record carrying it.
9919	- The trace header holds the `Deployment` and `t₀`, no policy. Only the
9920	  terminating advance's policy explains the stop, and the termination record
9921	  holds that one.
9922	
9923	Supersedes [D-091][d-091], [D-217][d-217]'s header-records-the-constructor-pair bullet, and
9924	[D-060][d-060]'s "policy at `Simulation` construction" clause; [D-060][d-060]'s rule that
9925	termination is state, never an exception, stands.
9926	
9927	**Spec.** [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.4][s12-4], [§12.6][s12-6], [§12.7][s12-7], [§13.5][s13-5], [Appendix C][sC]
9928	
9929	**Rationale.** The run was spread across the `Simulation`'s fields and the
9930	control plane. `termination` sat on `Control` beside the stop word, so the
9931	outcome of a run and the surface anyone may poke shared a struct;
9932	`RunPolicy` was mutable because `hit` was written into it by the loop, so a
9933	declared policy and a loop scratch shared a struct; the trace header grew
9934	its schema list in place, so an artifact captured at `init!` was not
9935	immutable; and `t_end`/`stop_on` had two homes, the constructor and the
9936	call, which [§13.5][s13-5] called "the honest cost" of [D-091][d-091]. Each of those is the
9937	state/artifact criterion violated once. A `Run` collects what one run owns.
9938	Its `const` fields are the ones the constructing entry point fixes; its
9939	writable ones are the ones the loop advances. A `StopPolicy` is what the
9940	caller declares for one advance, so it is a value the advance builds, and
9941	the defaults belong to the calls that take it. With no constructor defaults
9942	the second binding site disappears and the trace header has no policy to
9943	record; the terminating record carries the policy that ended the run, which
9944	is the only one a replay reader needs. The placeholder run at construction
9945	is preferred to a `nothing` field because every accessor then has a run to
9946	read, and `lifecycle` already says whether it is meaningful. An unbounded
9947	run is allowed because it is the interactive session's ordinary shape, with
9948	`UnboundedRun` as its advisory and the operator interrupt as its escape
9949	([§13.5][s13-5]); the advisory lives in the loop's cell because `run!` mutates state.
9950	
9951	**Rejected.**
9952	- *Constructor defaults for `t_end` and `stop_on` ([D-091][d-091]):* two homes for
9953	  one fact, with a precedence rule to reconcile them.
9954	- *A mutable `RunPolicy` carrying `hit`:* the declared policy and the loop's
9955	  scratch in one struct.
9956	- *`Run` as an immutable value rebuilt per frame:* the log and trace are
9957	  append-only state; copying them per frame is the allocation [§9.8][s9-8] forbids.
9958	- *`termination` staying on `Control`:* the control plane is what anyone
9959	  may poke; a run's outcome is not.
9960	- *A `Union{Nothing,Run}` field:* every accessor branches on a state
9961	  `lifecycle` already reports.
9962	- *Refusing an unbounded run:* the interactive session's ordinary shape
9963	  would need a sentinel `t_end`.
9964	
9965	Annotation (2026-10-08): amended by [D-316][d-316]. `stop_on` is
9966	`ignore_stop_requests`, with `()` as its default, and `StopPolicy` is
9967	`t_end` plus the ignored requester paths. `UnboundedRun` fires when no
9968	requester is honoured.
