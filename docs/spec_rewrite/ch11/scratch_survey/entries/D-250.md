9599	### D-250 — Warnings live where the artifact criterion puts them
9600	
9601	**Status.** ratified
9602	
9603	**Position.** One warning policy replaces the two streams of [§13.2][s13-2]. An
9604	artifact is an immutable pure function of its inputs; state is single-owner
9605	and mutated in place. A warning raised while producing an artifact lives on
9606	that artifact. A warning raised while mutating state lives in that state's
9607	status. Logging is presentation, never a home.
9608	
9609	- `Build` and `Deployment` carry `warnings`. A stratum that throws renders
9610	  its warnings with the collection. One that completes carries them on the
9611	  artifact, and the entry point logs each once at return.
9612	- A scoped channel bound by the build around its three steps lets a helper
9613	  running inside a declaration body append to the build's warnings. The same
9614	  helper called standalone, outside any build, logs directly. (Amended by
9615	  increment 44's review: the walk was named as the binder, but a warning may
9616	  arise in any step, so the build binds once.)
9617	- [Appendix C][sC]'s `logged` policy widens from stopped-sim service calls to any
9618	  artifact-producing call, the build included. The collected-warning slot
9619	  stays open and empty ([D-084][d-084] stands).
9620	- `warnings(x)` is defined on `Build` and `Deployment`, and on `Simulation`
9621	  as the concatenation of its artifacts' lists. Status-side warnings stay in
9622	  the status record.
9623	- `EmptyGreedyClaim` moves from a log line to the roster entry's diagnostic
9624	  cell, since `attach!` mutates the roster, and surfaces through the status
9625	  record. The log line at return stays as presentation.
9626	
9627	**Spec.** [§9.2][s9-2], [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.8][s11-8], [§13.2][s13-2], [Appendix C][sC]
9628	
9629	**Rationale.** [§13.2][s13-2] placed warnings by where they were raised: the build's
9630	collection, the runtime's per-writer cells, and a third home for service
9631	warnings that "belong to neither stream" and are emitted once at return
9632	beside the value. That third home was the tell. `trim!` returns a
9633	`TrimReport` and duplicates its warnings into the report's fields because
9634	the report is where a caller looks, which is the artifact criterion applied
9635	once by hand. Stated generally, the criterion decides every case the
9636	redesign meets. A build is an artifact, so an empty selection in a
9637	passthrough helper ([D-251][d-251]) belongs on the `Build`, not on a logger the CI
9638	run never reads. A deployment is an artifact, so `GridUtilization` belongs on
9639	it ([D-254][d-254]). `attach!` mutates the roster, so `EmptyGreedyClaim` belongs in
9640	the entry's cell beside the other writer diagnostics, and the status record
9641	is where a reader finds it after the fact. The channel exists because a
9642	helper inside `input_connections` has no artifact in hand; the build that
9643	owns the steps binds one, and the helper appends without knowing the
9644	build. Outside a build there is no artifact, and a log line is the honest
9645	fallback. Logging each artifact warning once at return keeps the interactive
9646	experience unchanged while the artifact keeps the record.
9647	
9648	**Rejected.**
9649	- *The logger as the home:* a CI run, a test and a second session read the
9650	  artifact, never the log; a warning that lives only in a log line is lost
9651	  to every reader but the one at the terminal.
9652	- *A global warning collector:* two concurrent builds would interleave, and
9653	  the collector would outlive the artifact it described.
9654	- *Promoting build warnings to errors under a CI switch:* [§13.2][s13-2] already
9655	  rejects it; an empty selection is a fact about the model worth knowing,
9656	  not a defect.
9657	- *Superseded position — service warnings as a third stream with no home:*
9658	  the artifact criterion covers them; a `TrimReport` is an artifact, and its
9659	  duplicated fields were the criterion applied ad hoc.
