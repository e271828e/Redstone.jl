12827	### D-303 — Name the connection declarations for wires, and `Group`'s keywords for the declarations
12828	
12829	**Status.** ratified
12830	
12831	**Position.** An assembly's three connection declarations are named for the
12832	wires they return, and `Group`'s keywords and fields take the names of the
12833	declarations they feed.
12834	
12835	- The renames: `inner_connections` → `inner_wires`, `u_connections` →
12836	  `input_wires`, `y_connections` → `output_wires`. Signatures, entry
12837	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
12838	- `Group`'s keywords and fields: `wires` → `inner_wires`, `inputs` →
12839	  `input_wires`, `outputs` → `output_wires`, `rates` → `sample_times`.
12840	- In prose, "wire" covers an entry of any of the three declarations. A
12841	  sentence that means the child-to-child kind alone says "inner wire".
12842	- `fragment`'s `u` keyword stays, and it stands on [§8.6][s8-6] alone. The root's
12843	  input faces come from the boundary declaration or from `u_types`,
12844	  following the root's class, and nothing downstream tells the two apart.
12845	  [D-279][d-279] grounded the keyword on the `u_connections` name, and that ground is
12846	  replaced.
12847	- What stays: `u_types` and `y_types`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
12848	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, `x_deriv` and every diagnostic
12849	  kind name.
12850	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
12851	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.
12852	
12853	**Spec.** [§3.3][s3-3], [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.8][s8-8], [§9.1][s9-1], [§11.3][s11-3],
12854	[§11.7][s11-7], [§13.1][s13-1], [§13.3][s13-3], [§13.5][s13-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.1][s14-1], [§14.2][s14-2], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC],
12855	[Appendix D][sD]
12856	
12857	**Rationale.** An assembly writes the trio on adjacent lines, and no
12858	component writes both `u_types` and `u_connections`. Parallel names inside
12859	the trio therefore serve the author more than [D-279][d-279]'s pairing across the two
12860	classes. "Wire" is the spec's everyday noun, and the names are shorter. Each
12861	boundary declaration now shares its prefix with the helper splatted into it,
12862	`input_wires` with `input_passthrough` and `output_wires` with
12863	`output_passthrough`.
12864	
12865	`Group`'s docstring says its declarations are textually identical to a named
12866	assembly's. With keywords equal to declaration names, an author learns one
12867	vocabulary. And `inputs = ("in" => "c/e",)` no longer reads as a list of
12868	inputs, the objection [D-279][d-279] itself raised against `u` and `y` as keywords.
12869	
12870	The `inner_`/`input_` visual near-collision that [D-170][d-170] rejected returns. It
12871	is a reading nuisance only, because an entry written under the wrong
12872	declaration fails at build. Three `Group` builds checked this against the
12873	tree on 2026-10-02. One put an input entry under the inner declaration, one
12874	an inner entry under the input declaration, and one an output entry under
12875	the inner declaration. Each raised a `DiagnosticError`.
12876	
12877	Bare `wires` stays free for locals, so [D-279][d-279]'s ground for rejecting `wires`
12878	as a function name does not apply to the prefixed names.
12879	
12880	**Rejected.**
12881	- *`inner_conns`/`input_conns`/`output_conns`:* a coined abbreviation on the
12882	  authoring surface, where a plain word costs the same characters.
12883	- *`wires_in`/`wires_out`/`wires_local`:* noun-first order breaks the
12884	  pairing with `input_passthrough` and `output_faces`. `wires_in` also reads
12885	  as "the wires in this assembly", and `local` names a scope beside two
12886	  directions.
12887	- *`input_routing`/`output_routing`/`inner_routing`:* the boundary pair is no
12888	  shorter than the names it replaces, and "routing" names an arrangement,
12889	  not the pairs returned ([D-301][d-301]).
12890	- *Short `Group` keywords (`wires`, `inputs`, `outputs`, `rates`) beside the
12891	  declaration names:* two vocabularies for one thing.
12892	
12893	Annotation (2026-10-06): [D-314][d-314] renames `inner_wires` to `local_wires`;
12894	the near-collision this entry accepted as a nuisance is removed rather than
12895	tolerated. The boundary names, `Group`'s keywords, the "wire" convention
12896	and the rest stand.
