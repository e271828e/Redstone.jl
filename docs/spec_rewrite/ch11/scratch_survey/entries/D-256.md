9971	### D-256 — Regroup the `Simulation`'s fields by owner
9972	
9973	**Status.** ratified
9974	
9975	**Position.** `Simulation` keeps five fields: the deployment, the executor,
9976	the run, the plane and the control. The build is reached through the
9977	deployment, which is the build plus what the grid parameters fix, so a second
9978	reference to it would be an invariant with no enforcer. Every other field
9979	moves to its owner.
9980	
9981	- To the deployment: `h`, `N_base`, `Δt_base`, the three event parameters,
9982	  `sched`, `D`, `Φ`, `Δt` ([D-254][d-254]).
9983	- To the executor: `chunk_size`, `stepper`, `xnext`, `ẋnext`,
9984	  `has_localized`.
9985	- To the periphery's own types: `join_timeout` into `Control`; `loop_diag`,
9986	  `loop_acct` and `published` into the plane.
9987	- To the run: `t_end`, `stop_on`, `stop_addrs`, `policy`, `log`, `trace`
9988	  ([D-255][d-255]).
9989	- Constructor keywords follow their fields. The `Deployment` constructor
9990	  takes the grid and event parameters and the algorithm. `join_timeout`,
9991	  `trace`, `log`, `log_every` and `log_max` are keywords of
9992	  `Simulation(deployment, T)` and of the two convenience forms, the
9993	  recording flags carried to `init!`, which builds the run. `DeploymentInvalid`
9994	  names deployment parameters only; the materialization's keywords validate
9995	  under `ArgumentInvalid`.
9996	
9997	**Spec.** [§9.7][s9-7], [§10.4][s10-4], [§11.2][s11-2], [§11.8][s11-8], [§12.1][s12-1], [§12.4][s12-4], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC]
9998	
9999	**Rationale.** The `Simulation` had twenty-eight fields because it was the
10000	only struct standing between a build and a running loop, so every value
10001	deployment or the loop needed landed on it. With `Deployment` and `Run`
10002	typed, each field has an owner by the same criterion that placed the
10003	warnings: an artifact's fields go to the artifact, a piece of state goes to
10004	the state that mutates it. The executor owns its stepper and the arrival
10005	buffers because it is the one thing that writes them. `join_timeout` is the
10006	shutdown tail's parameter and the tail runs on `Control`. The loop's
10007	diagnostic cell and account are one more writer's, and the plane holds the
10008	writers. The five that remain are the five things a simulation is.
10009	
10010	Annotation (2026-10-09): amended by [D-317][d-317]. `Simulation` keeps four fields,
10011	the model, the run, the plane and the control, and the model holds the
10012	deployment and the executor. The model also owns a diagnostic cell for its
10013	frame, which the drain folds into the loop's account beside the plane's
10014	loop cell. `chunk_size` follows its field to the `Model` constructors.
10015	
10016	**Rejected.**
10017	- *Leaving the fields flat:* every reader of a grid parameter or a buffer
10018	  goes through the `Simulation`, so nothing below it can be tested or printed
10019	  alone.
10020	- *A separate `Periphery` struct for the plane, control and published
10021	  holder:* the plane already is that struct.
