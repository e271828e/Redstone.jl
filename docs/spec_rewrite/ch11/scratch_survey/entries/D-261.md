10249	### D-261 — Three ownership rules for fields, with the placements they settle
10250	
10251	**Status.** ratified
10252	
10253	**Position.** Three rules decide where a value lives, and the placements
10254	below settle under them.
10255	
10256	- A struct holds what it owns or what it must retain across calls. A callee
10257	  takes what it reads as an argument, never off a field added to a container
10258	  it already holds.
10259	- An artifact holds declared facts. Its consumer compiles what it needs from
10260	  them once, at one home, and the activation's cell layout is that home for
10261	  address facts. (Amended 2026-09-21, ahead of increment 48: an artifact
10262	  holds its facts as rows. `Structure` carries one `ComponentEntry` per
10263	  component, its path, instance, tier, triple, rate chain and resolved
10264	  inputs, and one `Anchor` per anchor, its `(T, τ)` with the declaring
10265	  scope and key, as the `Schedule` carries `ScheduleEntry` and `ScopeEntry`
10266	  rows; the parallel vectors of the old shape were the same facts with no
10267	  enforcer of their common length. The walk's accumulator keeps the
10268	  columns and is named for what it is, a `StructureDraft`. Extended
10269	  2026-09-21, ahead of increment 48b: the nominal evaluation's first
10270	  product is `Outputs`, one `ComponentOutputs` per component in walk order,
10271	  its path and the output names each stage produces, with the execution
10272	  order beside the rows as component indices, the order being a product of
10273	  the evaluation and not derivable from the rows alone. The old `ports`
10274	  column was the two name lists concatenated and the old `edges` column was
10275	  derivable from the structure's connections and the producers' stage-2
10276	  names, so both went, and the `Build` renders the feedthrough from those
10277	  where it shows the table. `Events` is one `ComponentEvents` per component
10278	  in walk order, its path, its policy table and its bundle names.
10279	  `Activation{T}` keeps its columns: it is compiled state the executor
10280	  indexes, and no user reads it as a table.)
10281	- An object is built after its inputs exist. The placeholder an accessor
10282	  needs is the one exception, and it carries no configuration and no
10283	  compiled state.
10284	- The `Schedule` is its component rows and its rate-scope rows, the `Δt`
10285	  column among them. The per-component `(D, Φ, Δt)` the executor compiles
10286	  over are derived from the rows at `compile`.
10287	- A `t*` stop hit is `frame!`'s return value, a face or `nothing`. The
10288	  execution cursor keeps its dispatch fields alone.
10289	- `StopPolicy` is `t_end` and the stop faces. Their compiled addresses are
10290	  the loop's argument, bound by the advance and carried to the sampling read.
10291	- `trace`, `log`, `log_every` and `log_max` are keywords of `init!` and
10292	  `replay!`, the doors that build a run, with their defaults unchanged.
10293	  `Simulation(deployment, T)` keeps `join_timeout` and `chunk_size`. The
10294	  placeholder run is an empty log and no trace, and `trace(sim)` refuses a
10295	  traceless `built` simulation on the lifecycle before it reads the switch.
10296	- The roster entry keeps the stable device id and the handle drops its copy.
10297	  The entry reads its writer, its diagnostic cell and its binding through
10298	  the handle.
10299	- A snapshot carries its frame index beside its published-boundary ordinal.
10300	- The periphery resolves root faces against the activation's cell layout
10301	  alone. It carries the root faces in structure order with their probe
10302	  values beside the address table, so the periphery's schema lookups and
10303	  its layout lookups ([§14.3][s14-3]'s two families) both read it, and writers,
10304	  claims and the harness compile against it.
10305	- The data plane compiles no drain thunk at construction. The thunks are
10306	  compiled at each door and each roster change, at one site that takes the
10307	  store as an argument.
10308	- `Deployment.grid`, `Dataflow.edges`, the header fingerprint's root faces,
10309	  and `StepError`'s time and boundary beside `NonfiniteState`'s payload stay
10310	  as they are. (`Dataflow.edges` fell with the 2026-09-21 extension of the
10311	  artifact rule above.)

10321	**Spec.** [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§11.2][s11-2], [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.6][s12-6], [§13.5][s13-5], [Appendix B][sB]

10323	**Rationale.** A survey of every struct in `src/` (104 structs, 22
10324	findings, `docs/reports/20260921_data_survey/`) found no field with two
10325	writers and no shared mutable state without an owner. What it found was
10326	redundancy and reach, and two mechanisms produced most of it. Reach: a
10327	callee needs one more value, it already holds a container, and the
10328	container grows a field instead of the callee growing an argument. The
10329	walk's root types, the cursor's hit, the plane's store, the handle's plane
10330	and the recording flags on the placeholder run were all this. [D-260][d-260] stated
10331	the test for the stop policy, a field written only so a caller without the
10332	argument can reach it, and applied it to that one field. Declared beside
10333	compiled: an artifact is the value a user inspects and also the table the
10334	next stage consumes, and where the two forms differ the artifact grows a
10335	compiled shadow of itself, the schedule's vectors beside its rows, the
10336	policy's addresses beside its faces, a plan's face slot beside its face
10337	list. Stating the rules makes the fixes an application rather than twenty
10338	local judgments, and gives every later increment a check.
10339	
10340	The placements follow. The schedule's vectors are the rows re-indexed by
10341	component, built in the same loop, and `==`, `hash` and the replay header
10342	walk each carried a clause the rows already decide. The hit as a return
10343	value costs no allocation and leaves the cursor to the catch site it exists
10344	for. The addresses are derivable from the faces and the layout, and nothing
10345	reads them off the record. The recording keywords configure the run's two
10346	`const` fields, which the doors build, so [D-256][d-256]'s own rule, keywords follow
10347	their fields, puts them on the doors. On the constructor they rode a
10348	placeholder run through every later door, and `trace === nothing` meant
10349	both "switched off" and "no door yet". Per-run recording comes with the
10350	move, a long untraced run and then a short traced one on one simulation,
10351	at no cost the doors did not already pay, since each rebuilds the log, the
10352	trace and the drain thunks. The device id survives a stop on the entry
10353	([§12.4][s12-4]), so the handle's copy was the dead one. The frame index is what
10354	places a `t*` snapshot in its frame, since a `t*` publication counts an
10355	ordinal of its own. The layout already holds the three facts the periphery
10356	reads, the faces, their types through the address parameter and their
10357	probe values, so reading both lookups off it costs one sentence where
10358	resolving against the structure instead would cost four signatures and a
10359	parallel-vector invariant across two structs. A thunk compiled before the
10360	first door closes over a trace that door discards, and the constructor's
10361	compile was the one reason the run had to be built ahead of the plane.

10369	- *The recording flags as a sixth `Simulation` field:* a parameter nothing
10370	  on the simulation reads, on the struct whose five fields are the five
10371	  things a simulation is ([D-256][d-256]).
10372	- *The flags on `Control` beside `join_timeout`:* a recorders' parameter on
10373	  the control plane by convenience alone.
10374	- *The periphery resolving root faces against the `Structure`, as the
10375	  services do:* uniform, but four signatures and a parallel-vector
10376	  invariant for names the layout already holds.
10377	- *Dropping the schedule's `Δt` column for `D · Δt_base`:* the row is the
10378	  printable schedule and `Δt` is what it prints; the product is the same
10379	  float either way.
10380	- *Skipping the thunk compile at a roster change before the first door:* a
10381	  lifecycle test in `reclaim!` to save one small closure per attach.
10382	- *A nullable run in place of the placeholder:* [D-255][d-255]'s rejection stands.
10383	  The placeholder stays and is emptied.
